# Lamma demo assets (Phase B4 Task 6)

The 29 WebP files in `assets/` are AI-generated ILLUSTRATIVE samples (Ruling TPL-3) for the
`storedemo-lamma` showroom: they are not a real kitchen's food and show no real brand, person or place.

- **Model / how:** FLUX.1 schnell on Cloudflare Workers AI (`@cf/black-forest-labs/flux-1-schnell`,
  8 steps), offline, by `gen.py` from the prompts in `prompts.py`; `encode.sh` crops (4:3 dishes,
  1:1 grocery cut-outs and the maker crop, 16:9 hero), caps the long edge at 1200 px and encodes with
  `cwebp` (each file <= 140 KB, the whole set <= 7 MB). No stock photo, no third-party image and no
  real person is used. The Workers AI call used the local wrangler login on the company Cloudflare
  account; nothing else was touched.
- **Unbranded (Ruling B4-11):** no logo, lettering or readable label. FLUX invents text from prompt
  words that describe layout: a first pass of `potatoes` came back with the words "BREATHING ROOM"
  printed under the pile (the prompt said "centered with breathing room"), and `maker` carried a
  small watermark-like line; both were regenerated and the layout words were removed from every
  prompt. Every file was viewed before commit.
- **No orange subject (Rulings B4-2, B4-3):** FLUX reads a negated word as a positive one: the first
  prompts said "no oranges, no carrots" and the fresh-milk photo came back with oranges and carrots
  beside the bottle, the kofta with an orange sauce bowl, the basbousa with an orange slice. The
  prompts now only NAME the colours wanted ("dark brown and green only", "white and ivory only").
  Eleven first passes were regenerated by eye or by the probe (bananas were replaced by green apples:
  their yellow sits at the probe's hue edge). Bread, tagines and kunafa are brown and gold by nature, so
  `orange-probe.py` is food-calibrated (hue 12-45, HSV saturation >= 0.65, value >= 0.60: crust,
  caramel and brown sauce pass, a glowing orange fails), <= 4 % per dish/product image and <= 6 % per
  hero, enforced by `tests/unit/lamma-demo-assets.test.ts` with negative controls (orange, saffron,
  amber, brass, terracotta) and a positive control (bread browns pass).
- **Faces:** none. The maker image is a hands-at-work crop (floured hands rolling a stuffed vine leaf),
  chosen over a generated portrait (Ruling B4-11).
- **Known illustrative limits:** the stuffed cabbage reads as pale rolls, the brown lentils read like
  coffee beans, the minced beef is diced: the illustrative-photo labelling in the announcement bar
  and the store description says so.
- **Served from:** locally `LAMMA_DEMO_ASSET_BASE=/__lamma-demo` (an untracked copy under
  `apps/web/public/__lamma-demo/`, deleted afterwards); in production the R2 prefix `demo/lamma/`
  (`https://media.ebda.app/demo/lamma`), uploaded by the controller after merge.
