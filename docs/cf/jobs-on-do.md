## Which jobs can serve a DO store

`JOBS_ON_DO=1` + a store in `STORE_DO_ENABLED_IDS` = a DO store for jobs. With `JOBS_ON_DO` unset nothing below applies (every store on Postgres, today).

| Class (`job-data.ts`)                         | Jobs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | On a DO store                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `none`                                        | `heartbeat`, `gmail-alert-poller`, `alerts-failure-watch`, `audit-log-archive-weekly`, `billing-renewal-daily` (platform tables, logs)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | runs unchanged                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `ported`                                      | `wholesale-pending-nudge-cron` (reads through `withStoreDb`); `cart-recovery-email`, `abandoned-cart-purge-weekly` (R4, Ruling CF-R4-5: rows through `storeRepo`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | runs, via the store's alarm or its event, reading its DO                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `ported` (money, Ruling CF-JOBS-4)            | `payment-webhook-process`, `accounting-post-order-journal`, `accounting-post-refund-journal`, `accounting-daily-close`, `accounting-period-reconciliation`, `order-created-stuck-sweep`, `order-status-fanout-stuck-sweep`, `refund-reversal-stuck-sweep`, `payment-reconcile-sweep`, `payment-abandonment-sweep`, `order-auto-cancel-unpaid`, `instapay-expiry`, `instapay-receipt-extract`, `wa-store-events` | run on a DO store: events by `storeMoney` / `storeDb`, sweeps on the store's alarm through `sweepStoresDb` / `acrossStoresDb`; new DO ops in `packages/repo/src/do/job-ops.ts`                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `none` (platform rows only, Ruling CF-JOBS-4) | `account-erased-propagation`, `ci-erasure-second-pass`, `contact-form-send`, `domain-claim-sweep`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | runs unchanged (they read `stores` / `users` and third parties, never a store-do table)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `ported` (non-money, Ruling CF-JOBS-4)        | the 16 bulk AI sweeps (`bulk-ai-*`), `bulk-import-products`, `bulk-import-orphan-cleanup`, `ai-autofill-{cleanup,product,sweep}`, `accounting-profit-anomaly-cron`, `analytics-{daily-rollup,rollup-on-demand}`, `answer-engine-{citation-scan-cron,scan-on-demand}`, `agent-write-denied-watch`, `ci-outcomes`                                                                                                 | run on a DO store: rows by `storeDb` / `storeWriter` (a Workflow resolves them inside the step: `lazyStoreDb` / `lazyStoreWriter`), the ledger and the CI stamp by `storeMoney` (`accountTotals`, `ciOrderStamp`), the guarded AI product write by the in-DO procedure `guardedAiWrite`; alarm sweeps on the store's alarm, and the three cross-store crons (`ALL_STORES_JOBS`) from their one cron run |
| `unported` (default)                          | every other job (`prisma`/`withStore`, row locks, raw SQL, money and stock models)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | **refused**: no handler call, no `onFailure`, alert P0 (`MONEY_JOBS`: payments, refunds, orders, accounting, stock) or P1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

How a run is decided: the store is the alarm's store or `event.data.storeId`; an event with no `storeId` names it through `EVENT_STORE_RESOLVERS` (`payment-webhook-process`: the inbox row's store, resolved by the route from the provider reference; the lookup is its own Workflow step), so it is refused for a DO store like any unported money job (Ruling CF-MONEY-1). A run no store can be named for stays on Postgres until the D1 entity index exists. Sweeps (crons, no store) run for Postgres stores only: inside a guarded run `storeDirectory` / `acrossStores` drop DO stores and an `unported` sweep alerts which ones it skipped.

To port a job: replace `prisma`/`withStore` reads and writes with `withStoreDb(storeId, db => ...)` (reads) / `storeWriter` (non-money writes, `docs/cf/repo-factory.md`), move its id from the default into `PORTED_JOBS`, and the unit test checks the source no longer touches `prisma`. Money and stock jobs wait for D2a/D2b. A cron that must see every store at once (a watcher ranking stores, a leader-locked sweep, one shared provider budget) is listed in `ALL_STORES_JOBS` and lists the DO stores itself with `allStoresDirectory` / `acrossAllStoresDb` (`lib/jobs/store-db.ts`); it is not a skipped sweep for the flip gate.

The 15-minute abandoned-cart sweep (`abandoned-cart` in `cf/cron.ts`, detection, reminder steps, the e-mail claim) is a legacy cron, not a guarded job: it lists every store and reads each through `storeRepo`, so one pass serves Postgres and DO stores (Ruling CF-R4-5).

## Alarms

Armed for DO stores only (`syncStoreSchedules`, hourly). A Postgres store keeps the global Cron Trigger. `JOBS_CF_ALARMS=all` is therefore safe to leave on.

## Write freeze (`WRITES_FROZEN=1`)

| Writer                                                  | While frozen                                                                               | After the thaw                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| HTTP                                                    | 503 `READ_ONLY_WINDOW` (middleware/write-freeze.ts)                                        | normal                               |
| Cron Trigger, sweep (fires again within 6 h)            | tick skipped                                                                               | the next tick does the work          |
| Cron Trigger, rarer job; legacy daily crons             | job started and parked / queued as `legacy-cron`                                           | runs once                            |
| Queue consumers (email, sms, jobs, CI, WhatsApp events) | each message re-sent with a 300 s delay, acked only after the re-send                      | consumed normally                    |
| DLQs, and queues this Worker does not produce to        | retried with a backoff (5 min doubling to 12 h)                                            | consumed normally                    |
| Workflow instance                                       | sleeps 5 min at a time before its first step and before every later step (leases released) | continues at its next step           |
| StoreSchedule alarm                                     | due time kept, re-armed 5 min out, no attempt spent                                        | same occurrence (same event id) runs |

Limits: a step already executing when the flag lands finishes; a freeze over 24 h aborts a parked Workflow loudly and lets the platform retry/DLQ path alert; an alarm frozen over 24 h skips the stale occurrence.

## Flip gate and Ruling CF-JOBS-4

`node scripts/cf/not-on-do.mjs` also counts jobs: `jobsRefused` (MONEY_JOBS still unported) and `sweepSkips` (a ported cron sweep with no alarm: `alarm: true` in JOBS_MAP + `ALARM_READY`) CLOSE the gate; `jobsAccepted` (non-money unported jobs, P1 alert) is counted and listed. `tests/unit/do-not-on-do-guard.test.ts` keeps the manifest equal to the source. A ported cron sweep that runs from its cron with DO stores hidden and no alarm enabled pages (P0 for money). `jobsAccepted` after Ruling CF-JOBS-4: the second half (alphabetical `ebda-ads-conversion` .. `webhook-fanout`, another track) and `accounting-expense-ocr-process` (BLOCKER: it needs the expense / account / journal services on the DO; same unblock as `accounting-post-expense-journal`). Still unported money jobs (no DO engine yet): `shipment-create`, `order-created-fanout`, `order-status-changed-fanout`, `order-refunded-fanout`, `accounting-post-expense-journal`, `accounting-post-po-receive-journal`, `accounting-post-stock-adjustment-journal`, `stock-reorder-scan-*`, `warehouse-reconcile-*`.

## Non-money jobs, second half (Ruling CF-JOBS-4B)

Ported: `review-request`, `review-moderation`, `invoice-email`, `order-capi-conversion`, `lifecycle-emails`, `lifecycle-first-order`, `wa-plan-sync` (alarm-ready sweep), and `ebda-ads-conversion` / `legal-notice-send` (class `none`: platform tables only). Store data goes through `storeDb` / `storeWriter` / the `orderStamp` DO op; the buyer of a DO order is attached from the platform with `customersById`. The 23 jobs still in `docs/cf/not-on-do.json` `jobsAccepted` are listed with their blockers in `docs/rulings/CF-JOBS-4B.md`.

## Every job serves a DO store (Ruling CF-JOBS-4C)

`docs/cf/not-on-do.json` `jobsAccepted` is empty. Platform-only jobs (`gsc-ingest-*`, `search-log-purge-daily`, `soft-deleted-user-anonymize-monthly`) are class `none`. Sweeps over store data run on the store's alarm (`gold-rate-*`, `instapay-receipt-retention-daily`, `order-anonymize-5y-monthly`, `queue-depth-watch`, `store-usage-snapshot`, `webhook-delivery-purge-daily`, `search-index-backstop`, the stock scans); the event jobs run on the store's event. `r2-orphan-sweep` is one global cron that reads each DO store's references from its DO and aborts before any delete if one cannot be read. Open items (Vectorize index for DO product search, the request-time R2 reference check, plan-limit counts) are listed in `docs/rulings/CF-JOBS-4C.md`.

## Money jobs, shipment and order fan-outs (Ruling CF-JOBS-4, money half)

`shipment-create`, `order-created-fanout`, `order-status-changed-fanout`, `order-refunded-fanout`, `accounting-post-{expense,po-receive,stock-adjustment}-journal`, `warehouse-reconcile-{sweep-cron,on-demand}` and `stock-reorder-scan-{cron,on-demand}` serve a DO store through the DO ops in `packages/repo/src/do/{job-ops,books-journals}.ts` (claims are one conditional statement in one section). `MONEY_JOBS` is empty and `PORTED_MONEY_JOBS` lists every money job.
