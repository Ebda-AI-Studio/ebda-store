# B5-4

> Section: 2026-10-22 — Phase B5 Dahab (B5-\*)

Ruling B5-4 — gift wrap is a variant option, the card message is personalisation, no schema. A gift product carries an option `التغليف: عادي / علبة هدية` (the price delta lives on the variant, the existing path) and a personalisation field labelled `رسالة على الكارت`. On cards "تغليف هدية متاح" is one of the at most 2 fact chips (the taste bar holds); the Gift box PDP shows it in the buy box. Evidence: variants carry a price delta today, and personalisation reaches cart, order, admin, invoice and email (B0 section 5). Cost if wrong: a merchant cannot sell wrap as an order-level add-on (one wrap per order); a `giftOptions` order field can come later without touching the looks.
