Ruling CF-JOBS-3 (2026-10-30, INFRA-CF-1 fix for Opus review criticals C1 and C2, PR #356) - the money jobs serve a store on its Durable Object, and the flip gate counts job refusals. Ported (no longer refused): `payment-webhook-process` (a DO store applies the delivery through `PaymentService.handleWebhook` -> `paymentWebhook`; the DO dedupes on `payment_event_log`, so there is no Postgres `webhookProcessedAt` read or stamp; an external fact is recorded in full), `accounting-post-order-journal`, `accounting-post-refund-journal`, `accounting-daily-close`, `accounting-period-reconciliation`, `order-created-stuck-sweep`, `order-status-fanout-stuck-sweep`, `refund-reversal-stuck-sweep` (with the refund-claim scan), `payment-reconcile-sweep`, `payment-abandonment-sweep`, `order-auto-cancel-unpaid`, `instapay-expiry`, `instapay-receipt-extract`, `wa-store-events` (incl. the WhatsApp `customer.erased` step); `payment-webhook-stuck-sweep` is `none` (it only reads the platform webhook inbox). Sweeps are alarm jobs (`alarm: true` + `ALARM_READY`): the cron lists the Postgres stores, a DO store's alarm reads its own DO through `sweepStoresDb` / `acrossStoresDb`. New DO ops (`packages/repo/src/do/job-ops.ts`): `paymentEventRecord`, `abandonStamp`, `trialBalance`, `periodGlCredits`, and `cancel` takes `unpaidOnly` (a paid order wins the race, checked in the cancel's own section). A ported cron sweep that finds DO stores hidden while its alarm is not enabled now pages (P0 for money): a silent skip is the failure the guard exists to prevent. The flip gate (`scripts/cf/not-on-do.mjs`, `docs/cf/not-on-do.json`) now also counts `jobsRefused` (money jobs still unported - closes the gate), `sweepSkips` (ported cron sweeps with no alarm - closes the gate) and `jobsAccepted` (non-money jobs still refused, P1 - counted, listed, not closing the gate until Ahmad rules). Evidence: `tests/unit/jobs-money-on-do.test.ts` (each ported job on the DO path with Postgres mocked to throw), `packages/repo/test/do-job-ops.test.ts`, `tests/unit/do-not-on-do-guard.test.ts`. Residual: the money jobs still in `jobsRefused` (shipment-create, the three fanouts, the expense / PO / stock-adjustment journals, stock reorder and warehouse reconcile) have no DO engine yet, so the gate stays CLOSED. Cost if wrong: a non-money job refused for a DO store silently loses its P1 work (reviews, invoices, lifecycle mail) until it is ported; the money path is not affected.
