# B5-36c

> Section: Lifecycle emails and referral attribution (2026-10-22, branch `feat/lifecycle-emails`)

Ruling B5-36c - a fixed variant with no own price under a gold product is refused (409 `GOLD_VARIANT_NEEDS_PRICE`) rather than auto-frozen at a guessed price. Legacy priceless fixed variants are left out of the product minimum (if every variant is priceless the product keeps its price). Cost if wrong: a merchant must type a price before choosing "fixed" for such a variant; a legacy priceless fixed variant still sells at the product's price, which is now the gold variant's price, not the old ratcheted minimum.
