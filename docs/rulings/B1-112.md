# B1-112

> Section: 2026-10-15 — Phase B1 Qamar (B1-\*)

Ruling B1-112 — the showroom (`storedemo`), the local look stores and the scenario stores run the Professional plan, and a sale on a variant product is seeded onto its variants. Evidence: the demo backorder product (`evening-gown-rose`) read "Out of stock" with every size disabled on a starter store because `inventory.backorder` is a Professional feature and `gateBackorder` switches it off (API `acceptsBackorder: false`; with the plan, `true` and the Pre-order CTA renders); the sale product showed its strike on the card but not on the PDP because the PDP reads compare-at from the picked variant (`lib/product-pricing.ts`) and the seed gave variants neither price. Fix: `seed.mjs` writes `plan: 'professional'` and, for products with a compare-at, `price` + `compareAtPrice` on each variant. The production run (Task 11) therefore moves ONE row: the showroom's own plan (our store, no billing). Cost if wrong: the showroom is not a faithful starter store; a visitor never sees what a starter merchant lacks.
