Ruling OFFBOARD-1 - an owner with a live billed store cannot close their account. `DELETE /auth/me` (non-customer roles) calls `liveBilledStoresOf(userId)` (services/account-closure-guard.ts): the live stores the user owns whose `StoreSubscription.status` is `active`, `past_due` or `trialing`. Any hit refuses with 409 `OWNER_HAS_LIVE_BILLED_STORE` and an Egyptian-Arabic message (cancel the subscription from the billing page or transfer ownership, then close), before anything is deactivated, audited or revoked. `comped` (free grant) and `canceled` do not block; a store with no subscription row does not block. The same predicate is the guard for the merchant/staff closure trigger (M-024). Evidence: tests/unit/auth-delete-me-owner-guard.test.ts (refusal test fails on the old code, which deactivated the account), tests/unit/account-closure-guard.test.ts. Cost if wrong: a `trialing` store that never added a card is treated as billed, so its owner must cancel the trial first (one extra click); a miss in the other direction would strand a paying store with no owner.
