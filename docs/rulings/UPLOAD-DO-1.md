# UPLOAD-DO-1

Ruling UPLOAD-DO-1 - the upload routes (`/uploads/image`, `/images`, `/video`, `DELETE /uploads/:key`, `/font`) and the product 3D model routes scope their work with `runInStoreTenantContext` (a bare store scope on a Durable Object store, the tenant transaction on a Postgres store) instead of `runInTenantContext`, and every upload audit row names its store explicitly so the audit sink writes it to the store's DO. Evidence: production 2026-11-10 21:44:08Z `POST /api/v1/uploads/image` 500 `POSTGRES_REMOVED ... ($transaction)` (requestId 4da616ec, store 1bb14d0e, onboarding step 5); `workers-test/merchant-journey.test.ts` reproduces 500 on the photo upload and on `POST /product-models/:id/glb` on main and answers 200 after. Cost if wrong: an upload audit row that the sink cannot place is queued and alerted rather than written (the audit write already swallows its own failure, so the upload itself still succeeds).
