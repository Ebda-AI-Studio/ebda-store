# B2B-13

> Section: B2B wholesale pricing (feat/b2b-wholesale, 2026-10-21)

Ruling B2B-13 (coordinator, 2026-10-21; supersedes the "list price" wording of B2B-10; AMENDED by B2B-17 and B2B-18 on 2026-10-21) - A partial return reprices the units the buyer KEEPS at the LOWEST price they were still entitled to at sale time for the kept quantity (B2B-18: the group percent and any tier still reached, never bare list price when the group percent applies), judged on the product aggregate the way the tier was awarded (B2B-17). The merchant sees the calculation and can override it. Cost if wrong: a buyer who returns part of an order is refunded less than `price x quantity`; the merchant can override per return.
