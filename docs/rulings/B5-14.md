# B5-14

> Section: 2026-10-22 — Phase B5 Dahab (B5-\*)

Ruling B5-14 — the formula and its rounding (Task 8). Formula. `metal = weight_mg × rate_per_g`, `making = fixed ? value × 1000 : weight_mg × value_per_g` (both in piastre·mg/g), `base = metal + making`, `num = base × (10 000 + margin_bps)`, then one rounding, directly to the step: `final = round_half_up(num / (10^7 × step)) × step`, with `step` = the store's rounding step (1, 100, 500 or 1 000 piastres; default 100 = whole pounds). `round_half_up(a / b)` for positive BigInts is `(2a + b) / (2b)` (floor division). `num > 0` always holds, because `margin_bps ≥ −5 000`. Margin scope and rounding. The margin applies to metal + making together. There is exactly one rounding in the whole computation: no per-component rounding and no piastre-then-pound double rounding. Double rounding was caught while writing this plan: 19 230.4975 EGP rounds to 19 231 via piastres-then-pounds but correctly to 19 230. Task 8 pins that case as a negative control. Range. `final` must lie in `1..2 147 483 647` (Postgres `int4`). Otherwise `GOLD_PRICE_OUT_OF_RANGE`. Why half-up to the nearest pound. It is neutral (neither side gains systematically) and matches how Cairo shops quote. Ceil-to-pound would hand the merchant up to 99 piastres per piece silently. Cost if wrong: a merchant who wants ceil rounding cannot have it. That is one enum value later, with no data change.
