# ANALYTICS-DAY-1

Ruling ANALYTICS-DAY-1 (2026-11-14, analytics audit D1 + D2) - the per-day counters `product_daily_views` and `qr_scan_daily` are keyed by `regionDayKey(d)` (`@ebda/shared`): the UTC midnight of the UTC date of the Cairo day-start instant (so Cairo day D is keyed D-1 00:00Z, DST-safe, injective). Writers (`view-ingest.service`, `qr.service`) and readers (`runFunnelRollup`, `qrStats`) all use it, and the DO op contract (`dayArg` in `packages/repo/src/do/analytics-ops.ts`: a UTC midnight) is unchanged because it is already exactly this key; the callers were wrong (they passed the Cairo midnight instant, 22:00Z/21:00Z, so every `viewRecord`/`qrScanRecord` threw BAD_ARGS and the fail-open catch hid it; reads keyed on the Cairo instant would also never have matched copied history). Evidence: the Postgres `date` columns held the UTC date of the Cairo-midnight instant (Prisma casts a Date to its UTC date), `scripts/cf/copy-analytics-to-do.mjs` refuses any non-UTC-midnight `date` (so history in the DO is already on this key and no rewrite is needed), `tests/unit/analytics-day-key-real-do.test.ts` (real DO ops + real prisma-lite reads, winter/summer/either side of Cairo midnight). The search log is a separate, internally consistent convention (Cairo date label as UTC midnight, `search-log.service` writes, purges and reads with the same `dayMs`) and is NOT changed. The fail-open catches now also report to Sentry (`SentryService.captureException`, tags `area=analytics`). D2: the Funnel "abandoned" figure is a per-day FLOW (carts created exactly 7 Cairo days earlier and still unrecovered), because the tile sums the daily value over its window; the old unbounded `lt` summed the whole backlog once per day. Cost if wrong: a day label one off from the Cairo calendar day in the raw counter tables (never shown to merchants; rollup `periodDate` stays the Cairo instant), and the keying of any future writer that forgets `regionDayKey` is refused loudly by the DO op rather than stored under a wrong day.
