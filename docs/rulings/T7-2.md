# T7-2

> Section: 2026-11-09 — T7 signature section per look

Ruling T7-2 - signature presets carry only copy that is true of any store (a title, a button to the product list) and the STRUCTURE of a list (spec names, the days of the week), never a claim about the merchant's products; a list-driven signature (spec sheet, ingredients, kitchen days) with fewer than two filled rows renders nothing publicly and a hint in the editor, while the collage and the window render from their title alone (a typographic fallback) and with no photo. Existing stores are untouched: the sections reach a store only through a fresh store's look mix, a look switch the merchant runs (a draft) or "Add section"; the demo stores carry filled examples (`scripts/storedemo/*-home.mjs`). Evidence: "an unfilled feature renders nothing" (Rulings B4-1, B5-7), the publish gate refuses placeholder copy (LOOKS-9), and a preset like "5000 mAh" or "Sunday: molokhia" would publish a false product claim on a real store. Cost if wrong: a new Souq / Jasmine / Lamma store shows its signature only after the merchant fills it, so the look is less "premium by default" until then.
