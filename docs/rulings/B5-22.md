# B5-22

> Section: 2026-10-22 — Phase B5 Dahab (B5-\*)

Ruling B5-22 — the emergency brake is a script, and it goes through the service (Task 13). `scripts/gold-rate/freeze-all.mjs` turns every gold-priced product of one store (or of all stores) into a fixed-price product at the price it has now. It calls `GoldRateService.bulkSwitchMode(..., 'fixed')`, 500 products per call, so the freeze takes the advisory lock, writes the audit rows and runs the IMMEDIATE price purge on every cache layer (a SQL `UPDATE` would freeze a price that stayed stale in Redis for the cache TTL). Dry-run by default, loopback guard, `--yes-production` is Ahmad's to pass, an agent never does. Re-runnable: a product already fixed is skipped. The settings switch (`enabled`) is deliberately NOT turned off by it: a merchant who wants gold pricing back re-switches products, and the store keeps its rate history. Cost if wrong: after a freeze the merchant must re-enable gold pricing product by product (or by the bulk switch), which is the safe direction to be wrong in.
