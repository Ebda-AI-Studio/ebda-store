# B2B-4

> Section: B2B wholesale pricing (feat/b2b-wholesale, 2026-10-21)

Ruling B2B-4 — Quantity for tier lookup = total quantity of that product (or variant for variant-level tiers) across cart lines in the same cart, summed after merging note-lines. MOQ is per product, summed across its variants. Cost if wrong: a buyer of 6 + 6 across two sizes is priced as 12 when the merchant meant per-variant; mitigated by a per-tier `scope` is NOT added (YAGNI).

---

> Section: B2B wholesale pricing (feat/b2b-wholesale, 2026-10-21)

Ruling B2B-4 (extended, I4) - Only orderable, loose (non-bundle) lines are summed into the product / variant quantity; an unavailable line must not carry a cart over a tier. Ruling B2B-8b - bundle components do not count toward tier quantity or MOQ, and MOQ does not apply inside a bundle. Cost if wrong: a merchant expects a bundle to count toward a reseller's 10+ break; the follow-up is a per-bundle flag.
