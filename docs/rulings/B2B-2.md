# B2B-2

> Section: B2B wholesale pricing (feat/b2b-wholesale, 2026-10-21)

Ruling B2B-2 — Price identity is the authenticated user id, never a typed phone/email (extended by B2B-2c: bound through `customers.user_id`). `resolveCustomerContext` matches a Customer by shipping phone/email and is correct for promotion targeting (a guest typing a VIP's phone gets, at worst, a promo). A PRICE must never work that way: anyone could type a wholesaler's phone at guest checkout and be charged wholesale. The resolver takes `buyerUserId` from the verified session; guests, WhatsApp-bot sessions and payment-link payers are retail unless the merchant creates the order for a named customer in admin. Cost if wrong: a verified-by-WhatsApp wholesaler orders at retail until a later plan adds the WA path.
