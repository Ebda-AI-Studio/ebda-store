# B2-22

> Section: 2026-10-21 — Phase B2 Souq (B2-\*)

Ruling B2-22 (Task 7 scenario matrix, 2026-10-21) — the Souq skin baseline drops the branded base's section colours. Found because the scenario stores (a fresh store, then `applyLook(reset)`, the real merchant path) rendered Catalogue and Showroom with a cobalt category band and 3.2:1 ink captions, while the seeded look stores, built from their own documents, looked right; the pack had been shot only on the latter. Cause: `buildSouqBaseline` kept `BASE_BRANDED_LAYOUT`'s `bgColor: 'token:primary'` and `token:card` typography on hero, categories, new arrivals, best sellers and testimonials, and a look's `design` sets neither the band on a section it does not mention nor typography. Souq now drops those (as Dahab does, `buildDahabBaseline`); Night's own inverse band is unaffected. The same baseline also sets the listing/search/related card frame to the neutral `token:border` (it was `token:primary`: a cobalt or brass box round every card, a second loud accent; seen in the pack's category shots). Evidence: `tests/unit/souq-look-mix.test.ts` (fresh layout page + each look: no `token:primary` band, no `token:card`/`token:secondary` heading colour); the scenario matrix re-run. Cost if wrong: a Souq store that relied on the branded primary band (none exists: the template is new) loses it; Qamar and every other template baseline are untouched. Lesson recorded for STOP-2: the look stores must come from `applyLook`, not a hand-built document.
