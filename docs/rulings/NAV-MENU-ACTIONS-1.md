# NAV-MENU-ACTIONS-1

Ruling NAV-MENU-ACTIONS-1 - in the marketing site's Menu (under 1200px) the page's CTA and WhatsApp are ONE block (`li.menu-actions`), the last item of the panel, pinned to the panel's bottom edge (`position: sticky`) so both stay in view while the links scroll under them; the language link sits above the block. The CTA is in the block under 800px only: from 800px it is in the header bar (Ruling NAV-BAR-FIT-1) and the block holds WhatsApp alone. The two share a 10px radius and a 44px height, sit on one row when both labels fit and on two full-width rows when they do not; a label never wraps. From 521px up the panel also gets a max-height and scrolls inside itself. Evidence: measured on a phone-sized build before the change (390x844 and 360x740): the WhatsApp pill, the language link and the CTA were three unrelated rows at y 1069, 1125 and 1181 inside a panel 754 / 650px tall, so 420 to 520px below its fold. After the change, measured 2026-11-10 on the built site (service lines on) with the Menu open on 7 pages (`/`, `/services/`, `/services/marketing/`, `/store/`, `/en/`, `/en/services/marketing/`, `/en/store/`) at 13 sizes from 360x740 to 1199x700, the consent bar removed: 91 of 91 trials pass. In every one the block is inside the screen and within 1.5px of the panel's bottom edge, before and after the panel is scrolled to its end; the last link stops 13.8 to 14px above the block; the CTA and WhatsApp are 44px tall; no link in the Menu is under 44px; the page does not scroll sideways. The CTA is in the block at every size under 800px and out of it from 800px. The block is 65px tall on one row and 117px on two (two rows: English from 521 to 799px with "Choose a product" or a product CTA). Keyboard, Menu open at 390x700, 600x600 and 1024x600: 17, 17 and 13 links take focus in order, none under the block or above the panel, each with a 3px outline. Contrast: CTA 6.37:1, WhatsApp 18.6:1 (one colour scheme: a run with the dark scheme requested returned the same colours). Residual, measured the same day: with the consent bar showing (a first visit) the bar covers the block by 104 to 123.5px in 91 of 91 trials. The panel's max-height here already subtracts `--ebda-consent-h`, but nothing on this branch sets that variable; PR #510 sets it, and the same probe must be re-run once both are on main. The static header from `siteNavHtml` in `packages/shared/src/brand/index.ts` (rendered by `apps/status/src/render.ts` and `packages/site-kit/src/build/shell.mjs`) keeps its own last row `li.menu-cta` and has no WhatsApp link in its nav (read in code); no file under `packages/shared` changes here, and whether its CTA shows without scrolling was not measured. Cost if wrong: the pinned block takes 65px (one row) or 117px (two rows) of the Menu's height on a small phone; removing the `.menu-actions` rules in `apps/marketing/src/app/chrome.css` returns it to an ordinary last row.
