# MONEY-LAPSE-1

> Section: 2026-11-15 — Money-abuse audit fixes (#836, branch `fix/instapay-lapse-downgrades`)

Ruling MONEY-LAPSE-1 — amends EBDA-FUNNEL-11. When the InstaPay grace (`INSTAPAY_GRACE_DAYS`) passes unpaid, the store is still suspended (`unpaid`) and the retry cleared, AND the subscription is now downgraded to free / `canceled` (`downgradeToFree(..., 'dunning')`, the same end state as a card's third failed retry). Three more pieces belong to the same ruling. (1) A row an earlier build left `past_due` with `nextRetryAt = null` is selected by the daily due scan and downgraded the same way (self-heal). (2) Until the cycle runs, `getEntitlements` treats that lapsed shape as the free plan (`isLapsedManualSubscription`). (3) Any store suspended for billing (`unpaid` or `over_store_limit`) gets no AI spend (`AI_STORE_BILLING_SUSPENDED`, 402, in `assertStoreAiEnabled`, which `assertAiQuota` and `requireAiEnabled` both ask). A parent whose subscription is `canceled` takes its covered children's mirrored rows to free / `canceled` too; its next payment re-covers them and copies the plan back. A recorded payment still restores everything at once (`recordManualPayment` accepts a canceled row). Evidence: money-abuse audit S4, 2026-11-15. The grace step only cleared the retry, so the row stayed `past_due` on its paid plan forever: `findDueSubscriptionIds` selects `nextRetryAt <= now` only, and `getEntitlements` gives `past_due` the paid limits. A merchant who paid one month of Business kept Business admin features and AI at Ebda's cost indefinitely. Cost if wrong: a merchant who pays on day 4 of the lapse sees free-plan limits (not only a paused storefront) for the hours until a super admin records the payment; that payment brings back the plan and the features.
