# MKT-NO-HEXAGRAM-1

Ruling MKT-NO-HEXAGRAM-1 - no marketing motif, pattern generator or SVG may draw a {6/2} star (two overlapping triangles); it reads as the Star of David, which is unacceptable for an Egyptian and Islamic brand (Ahmad 2026-11-03, after seeing the 6-star medallion). The Chat family is now the 12-point star (still 6-fold symmetric, lattice of 12-point stars with tips touching along the 6 lattice directions); the Store family stays the 8-fold star (two overlapping squares, Rub el Hizb). The 6-star medallion was removed outright and its section reuses the accepted octagon-ring medallion; the divider, strip and tessellation files were redrawn with the 12-point star (`public/motifs/*12*.svg`, `GirihPoster.tsx` pattern `g12w`, `studio-pattern.js` family `12`). Guard: `tests/unit/marketing-no-hexagram.test.ts` fails on any 12-vertex 6-tip star polygon or two triangles sharing a centre in `public/motifs/*.svg` and the rendered poster, and on any source call asking for a 6-tip star; the detector has a negative control. Cost if wrong: a new motif drawn with a different command set (arcs, curves) could slip past the polygon detector and would only be caught by review, a brand-safety miss, not a runtime failure.
