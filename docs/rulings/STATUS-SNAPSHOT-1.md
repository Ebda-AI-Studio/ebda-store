# STATUS-SNAPSHOT-1

Ruling STATUS-SNAPSHOT-1 - the public reads of status.ebda.app (`/`, `/en`, `/search.json`, `/api/status.json`) are served from a per-colo Cache API snapshot (`apps/status/src/snapshot-cache.ts`), not from a live StatusStore Durable Object call per request. Staleness class: BOUNDED-stale. A fresh snapshot (<= 30 s, the page's own max-age) is returned with no DO call; an older one (<= 10 min, two cron intervals) is returned at once while ONE background refresh runs; past that the visitor waits for a live read, and if the DO is failing the old snapshot is served rather than a 500. The probe round (`runProbes`, cron every 5 min) and every successful incident POST write a fresh snapshot into the colo they run in, so a new incident shows there at once and in other colos within 30 s of their next visit. Evidence: measured 2026-11-19, 20 sequential requests to `/`: median 0.60 s, 7 of 20 over 1 s (4.0-16.2 s), plus 500s; `/healthz` (no DO) 1 of 20 over 1 s. apps/status/test/snapshot-cache.test.ts (first read loads, later reads do not touch the store; stale served with one background refresh; past-window live read; store down serves old; nothing cached and store down throws), mutation-checked by removing the fresh-hit return. Cost if wrong: a colo that has not been visited for 10 min makes its first visitor wait one DO read, and an incident created from another colo can take up to 30 s to show there; the Cache API is per colo, so there is no single global snapshot to invalidate.
