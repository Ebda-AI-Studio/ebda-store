# SEARCH-T11-10

> Section: 2026-10-15 — Phase B1 Qamar (B1-\*)

Ruling SEARCH-T11-10 (review I2; supersedes the SEARCH-T11-7 residual) — a poison product is quarantined, it never stalls its store. When a batch's embed call fails non-retryably, or retryably on the step's LAST Inngest attempt (`attempt === INDEX_RETRIES`, 3), the step embeds the batch one product at a time (`embedEachIsolating`). A product is a poison when it fails non-retryably or fails while another product in that pass succeeded; when every product fails retryably it is an outage, the run fails (P1 via `alerts-failure-watch`) and nothing is quarantined. Poisons go to `product_embed_failures` (new, store-scoped, strict RLS, FK cascade to products and stores) keyed on the product with the md5 of the failing text; stale detection and the backstop probe skip a product while `md5(current doc)` equals that hash, so editing the text re-admits it, and a successful embed deletes the row. One P1 (`search-index.quarantine`) per NEWLY quarantined product or text (the upsert only returns a row when it is new or the hash changed), never per run. `storeIndexStatus` gains `failed` (the viewer, Task 12). The in-process `indexStore` isolates at once (no retries there). Evidence: `tests/integration/product-index.test.ts` "review I2" (as `ebda_app` + guard: 2 of 3 embedded and the poison quarantined with one alert; a second run makes no provider call and no alert; an edit re-admits and clears it; a retryable error throws before the last attempt and isolates on it; an outage quarantines nothing) — red before the change; a mutant that drops the quarantine skip turns 2 cases red. Cost if wrong: a product that failed during a partial provider outage is quarantined until its text changes (an operator can delete its `product_embed_failures` row to retry it at once).
