Ruling FOUNDING-1 (Ahmad 2026-10-26, M-082) - free months are a ledger, not a flag. (a) Founding-20: the first 20 stores to make a PAID subscription payment (a succeeded billing_events row with amount > 0) get 1 free month; "first 20 merchants" is read as the first 20 PAYING merchants, so a signup that never pays burns no slot. (b) Referral: the referred store's first PAID payment gives 1 free month to BOTH sides; a referrer is rewarded at most 3 times; the referred side is never capped by the referrer's cap. The link is the referrer's store slug passed as `ref` to POST /onboarding/store (owner's first store only; same-owner, unknown or repeat links are ignored, first link wins). (c) Every grant is one append-only `billing_grants` row (whole months only, CHECK months = 1, plus the plan price it was worth in piastres), with a UNIQUE idempotency key (`founding:<store>`, `referral:<referred>:referred|referrer`), so a replayed payment grants nothing twice. Caps are counted under a transaction-scoped advisory lock (one for the founding counter, one per referrer). (d) A grant is applied by moving `currentPeriodEnd` one month out under the subscription row lock; it stays `pending` while the store has no period (free/comped) or a renewal/upgrade/checkout charge is in flight (moving the end under an in-flight charge would make the settle see a different `forPeriodEnd` and raise "charge not applied"), and is applied by the next payment effect. (e) Super-admin sees every grant in /billing-events -> Free months. Tables are platform data with no store_id and no RLS (same class as billing_webhook_events). Evidence: tests/integration/billing-grants.test.ts (real Postgres: 19 filler + 6 concurrent payers -> exactly 20; 5 concurrent referred payers -> exactly 3 referrer grants; 7 concurrent replays -> 1 grant, +1 month). Cost if wrong: if Ahmad meant the first 20 SIGNUPS, a signup that never pays would hold a slot in his reading, and we would need to move the grant to store creation; one-line change in createGrantsForPayment plus a new key.
