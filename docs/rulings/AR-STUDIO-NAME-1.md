# AR-STUDIO-NAME-1

Ruling AR-STUDIO-NAME-1 - the Arabic studio name is spelled «ابدأ ستوديو» (no alef), matching the legal entity «ابدأ ستوديو للذكاء الاصطناعي والبرمجيات والتسويق الرقمي»; marketing uses «ابدأ ستوديو للذكاء الاصطناعي» where the long form is needed and «ابدأ ستوديو» short; English stays "Ebda AI Studio". «ابدأ استوديو» is replaced everywhere (code, i18n, docs, help, status, titles, OG, JSON-LD) and `BRAND.company.shortNameAr` / `nameAr` are «ابدأ ستوديو للذكاء الاصطناعي» (controller, 2026-11-03: the vendored foundation `brand.ts` is the source and carries the same values; links such as About may use the short «ابدأ ستوديو»). The legal entity name (AR and EN "Ebda Studio for Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, and Digital Marketing") appears only on the legal notice and legal pages, plus one small "Legal entity" line at the foot of /about that links to the legal notice (controller, 2026-11-03); never on /contact, in a hero or elsewhere on marketing pages. Evidence: public sites audit 2026-11-03 P1-13/P1-14 (109 of 111 pages carried the old spelling). Cost if wrong: the studio name reads differently from the legal entity on customer-facing pages, fixable by one edit of `BRAND.company` in packages/shared/src/brand/index.ts.

Superseded in part by LEGAL-NAME-2 (2026-11-03): the legal entity names quoted above are replaced by «ابدأ ستوديو لتكنولوجيا المعلومات» / "Ebda Studio"; the spelling and short-name rules here stand.
