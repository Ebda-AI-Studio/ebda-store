# B2B-18

> Section: B2B wholesale pricing (feat/b2b-wholesale, 2026-10-21)

Ruling B2B-18 (controller, 2026-10-21; final review P1-2; amends B2B-13, whose "falling back to list price" predates B2B-3b) - Kept units are repriced with the buyer's FULL order-time entitlement from the snapshot: `max(paid, min(list, group-percent price, best public tier reached, best group tier reached))`. The group percent applies at every quantity, so it is snapshotted as `groupPercentMinor` (quantity-independent) and bounds the repriced unit; never bare list price for a group member. Evidence: group 10% off (90) with a group tier 10+ at 80, buy 10, return 5 -> refund 350 (was 300: the buyer paid 50 more than a fresh order of 5 would cost). Cost if wrong: a merchant who wanted a returning group member to lose the group percent too is under-recovering by the percent on the kept units; the override covers it.
