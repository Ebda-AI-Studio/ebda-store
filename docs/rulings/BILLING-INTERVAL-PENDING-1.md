# BILLING-INTERVAL-PENDING-1

> Section: 2026-11-23 - Amelia Aurum billing display (branch `fix/billing-interval-pending`)

Ruling BILLING-INTERVAL-PENDING-1 - on an ACTIVE subscription, the interval chosen at InstaPay checkout (`chooseInstapayPlan`) is stored in `store_subscriptions.pending_billing_interval` and replaces `billing_interval` only when the payment for that quote is recorded (`recordManualPayment`, card settle and the D1 upsert all clear it). `instapayDetails` prices and records the claim at the pending interval; `getSubscriptionBillingState.nextCharge` (the renewal quote) stays on the CURRENT interval and the Billing page adds "Switching to <interval> billing from <date>" (`trial.switchingInterval`, ar + en). Trialing, past_due and canceled stores have no live paid period to protect and still write the interval directly. Origin: Amelia Aurum was comped Business yearly on 2026-11-12 (period end 2027-11-12); a later monthly InstaPay pick rewrote `billing_interval` to monthly, so the page showed "9,990.00 (Monthly)" next to a date a year out. Label, amount and date all came from one record; the record was mid-switch. Evidence: `tests/integration/instapay-receipts.test.ts` (BILLING-INTERVAL-PENDING-1 block), `tests/unit/signup-funnel.test.ts` (nextChargeOf override), billing `page.test.tsx`. Repair of the existing Amelia row is a separate decision. Cost if wrong: a merchant who pays the monthly quote while still active sees the renewal quote on the old interval until the payment is recorded, which is the pre-payment truth.
