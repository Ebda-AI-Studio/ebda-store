Ruling M066-1 (audit M-066) - the accounts password and emailed-code guess budgets no longer lock an account; they lock an (account, client address) pair and slow the account progressively. The old per-subject 5 per 15 min counter let anyone type 5 wrong passwords against a victim's email and lock the victim out for 15 minutes (a targeted denial of service, and the same for reset and verify codes). Now (apps/accounts/src/throttle.ts, `brake: true` on `login` and `code`): (a) the hard refusal is per (email, IP) at 5 per 15 min, so an attacker locks only their own address out of that account; (b) the per-IP budget (60) still bounds one address across all accounts; (c) the account as a whole is never refused: past 5 attempts in the window each attempt waits 250 ms doubling (cap 20 s) before the guarded work, and a success clears the tally. The second-factor, step-up and passkey budgets are unchanged: they need the password or a session first, so an outsider cannot trip them for a victim (and a challenge's own attempt cap still holds). Residual: an attacker with M addresses gets 5 x M guesses per window against one account, each slowed by the delay; credential stuffing at that scale is the WAF and bot-score layer's job. A victim signing in during an attack is slowed (up to 20 s), never refused. Evidence: apps/accounts/test/targeted-lockout.test.ts (2 of 3 fail on the old code); identity.test.ts and review2.test.ts updated from "5th failure from any address locks the email" to "from one address". Cost if wrong: a distributed guesser gets more attempts per window than before; a victim under sustained attack waits seconds, where before they were locked for 15 minutes.
