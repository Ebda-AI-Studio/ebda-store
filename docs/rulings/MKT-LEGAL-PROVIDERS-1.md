# MKT-LEGAL-PROVIDERS-1

Ruling MKT-LEGAL-PROVIDERS-1 (audit F-2, 2026-11-09 marketing audit, filed 2026-11-11) - provider names (Cloudflare, Sentry, Google Gemini, PostHog, Meta, OneSignal, Kashier) stay in the privacy policy (sections 5 and 6) and the Ebda Chat DPA; the "no provider names" copy rule applies to marketing pages only, and marketing copy carries none. Evidence: `apps/marketing/src/lib/legal-privacy-stack.ts` section 5 names each provider with its purpose and region (en + ar); a sub-processor and cross-border-transfer disclosure is a legal duty under the Egyptian PDPL, so removing the names would make the policy non-compliant. Cost if wrong: if a reviewer reads the copy rule as covering legal pages, the policy loses its sub-processor list and Ebda is exposed on PDPL disclosure.
