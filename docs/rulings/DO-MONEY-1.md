Ruling DO-MONEY-1 (2026-10-25) - the StoreDO checkout core (`packages/repo/src/do/checkout.ts`, one `transactionSync`) after the Opus money review (4 HIGH, 7 MEDIUM, 9 LOW) and the controller's finding on guest coupon limits. FIXED with tests (money-stock.test.ts, 18 node cases; workers-test, 10 cases on a real Durable Object incl. 100 parallel checkouts on one SKU, a 10-use coupon, a wallet, 50 identical idempotency keys, and 50 parallel guest redemptions at limit-1): coupon targeting (customer group, first order only, min quantity, product/category scope, discount base = matching subtotal) ported from `promotion-targeting.ts`; free_shipping zeroes shipping; per-customer coupon limit enforced for GUESTS by the E.164 phone (`normalizePhoneForStorage`, the buyer-key normaliser) or lowercased email, counted in the same transaction as the redemption (cancelled/refunded orders do not count); a guest with NO phone and NO email is REFUSED a per-customer coupon with COUPON_NEEDS_CONTACT (the Postgres path lets them through: a documented tradeoff at order.service.ts:1728 and :3379 that the controller rejected; the Postgres path keeps it until cutover, flagged); `usage_limit`/`per_user` of 0 mean unlimited (Postgres truthiness); stock-hold cap (2 manual / 5 gateway per phone or email, 8 per IP); backorder only when the caller passes the store's `inventory.backorder` feature; untracked products are not decremented (variants too); a variant without a price inherits the product's; `expectedTotal` gate (PRICE_CHANGED); idempotency replays only for the same customer and lines (else IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT); the customer must exist in this store; no zero wallet transaction; fully wallet-paid orders are `confirmed`; totals capped at 2e9. TRUST BOUNDARY (written in the code): the core trusts `customerId`, `shipping`, `tax`, `backorderEnabled` and `expectedTotal`: the ONLY caller is the store Worker, which takes `customerId` from the verified session and computes shipping/tax server-side. NOT PORTED yet (later slices, each with its own parity test, none may be skipped before a store checks out through the DO): promotions, bundles, loyalty, price tiers/groups, wholesale audience (refused fail-closed), gold-rate gate (blocker for gold stores), COD fee, MOQ, shipping and tax engines, cancel/refund un-claiming a coupon and restocking, `backorder_lines` rows. Same-class sweep: the only other per-user/first-order checks are `order.service.ts` (two sites, same skip) and `promotion-resolver.ts` (identity by customer or typed guest identity: not ported). Cost if wrong: money paths stay behind the per-store flag and the Postgres default; a divergence is a failing parity test before any store moves.
