# CF-R4-3

Ruling CF-R4-3 (2026-10-30, INFRA-CF-1 PR R4, Ahmad via the controller) - cart pricing on a DO store. The price MATHS was already one pure function shared by every path (`resolveUnitPrice`); what read Postgres was the LOADING, so only the loaders move: `PriceResolver.loadTiers` and `loadBuyerGroup` (the `customers` row and its `group`) read through `storeDb(storeId)`, `CartService.computeTotals` hands the public gold-pricing facts a `storeDb` client, and `loadCurrentRates` reads an unlocked rate with the model read `goldRateCurrent.findMany` (only the `FOR UPDATE` / `FOR SHARE` readers keep raw SQL: a row lock is Postgres's). The platform pieces stay where they are by design: the session user's account tests (`prisma.user`), and the `b2b.wholesale` entitlement (`featureService`). On Postgres `storeDb` IS the `prisma` proxy, so the very same calls run. Evidence: `tests/integration/repo-factory-carts-contract.test.ts` "cart pricing": a cart with a MOQ product, a quantity tier, a wholesale `percent_off` group and its member, a gold-rate piece with a current rate and a variant line is priced for an anonymous buyer, a group member, after the quantity reaches the tier break (the tier and the group price per the resolver's rules) and with the entitlement off (tiers and group prices dropped, MOQ stays): unit, total, list price, source, tier, ladder top, `belowMoq`, `moqRequired` and the gold facts are equal on real Postgres 17 and on the DO schema. No workerd concurrency test: cart pricing takes no lock on Postgres (the only lock in this area, the gold rate `FOR SHARE`, belongs to checkout and is already the DO's single thread, CF-ORDERS-1). Other callers of `PriceResolver` (order and abandoned-cart detection) get the DO behaviour from the same change; the product detail page's gold facts (`ProductService`) still read Postgres and are not part of the cart. Cost if wrong: a wrong displayed cart price is corrected at checkout (the engine re-prices from the rows and refuses a changed total, `PRICE_CHANGED`); nothing is charged from this read.
