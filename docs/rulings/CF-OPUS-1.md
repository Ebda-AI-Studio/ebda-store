# CF-OPUS-1

Ruling CF-OPUS-1 (2026-10-30, INFRA-CF-1 Opus review) - Erasure propagation runs for DO shoppers and the DO erasure scope is finalized only by its success path. `account-erased-propagation` and `ci-erasure-second-pass` touch only OneSignal, PostHog and the R2 CI bucket (no store table), so they are `none` in `job-data.ts` and are no longer refused for a DO store; `finalizeErasure` moved from `AuthService.eraseAccount` (after the enqueue) to the job's last step, after every propagation step succeeded (`tests/unit/erasure-finalize-after-propagation.test.ts`: finalizes on success, not when a step fails, no-op for Postgres). Supersedes the finalize-after-enqueue clause of CF-R6A-3; the 30-day alarm purge stays as the backstop. Residual: the Ebda WhatsApp `customer.erased` leg lives in `wa-store-events`, still refused for DO stores until its link-row read is ported. Cost if wrong: a third-party copy of a shopper persists until the job is retried; the erasure scope is kept until then, never dropped early.
