Ruling AIPATCH-2 (page-builder AI v2, 2026-11-17) - the AI page edit stays a closed, reducer-backed op set, widened by six ops (addBlock, moveBlock, removeBlock, setRef, setViewportSetting, setViewportHidden) and still produces only a DRAFT the merchant reviews; AIPATCH-1's "media, links and catalogue references are never set by the model" is replaced by: they are set ONLY through `setRef`, and only to a reference of THIS store that the service showed the model (`loadBuilderAiCatalogue`: at most 40 public active products and 30 categories, read through the store's own scope; the allow-list is exactly those ids, slugs, first-image URLs and internal link paths, so an invented reference is rejected with NOT_ALLOWED and never saved). NEVER-OVERWRITE-1 is enforced in code, not in the prompt: a text, textarea, image or link setting is fill-only (`isStarterValue`: empty, placeholder copy, the setting default, or the value a section preset ships with); a merchant's own value is rejected with OVERWRITE_PROTECTED and the preview says how many parts were left alone. Design settings (colour, range, select, switch) are not words and stay editable; a per-viewport override of a words setting is refused. The model also sees the viewport the merchant is on and their language. Product and category names are merchant text and are handed over as data. Not built: creating a whole new page, and filling a block added in the same request (the model cannot know its id). Cost if wrong: a merchant who WANTS the assistant to rewrite their headline is told it left it as is and must edit by hand; relaxing that is one rule in `isStarterValue`.
