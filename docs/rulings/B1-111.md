# B1-111

> Section: 2026-10-15 — Phase B1 Qamar (B1-\*)

Ruling B1-111 — accepted residual: a MERCHANT `primaryColor` at an extreme (near-white `0 0% 98%` on a light look, or pure yellow on white) is honoured verbatim (`docs/architecture/store-theme-flags.md`: explicit brand colours are the merchant's), so `text-primary` prices and kickers fail contrast on those stores. The Task 10 matrix (`qamar-edge-*` stores) therefore ignores the axe `color-contrast` rule on stores whose primary is overridden to an extreme; every other rule still applies. Evidence: matrix run 2026-10-21, `color-contrast` on `[data-zone="productPrice"] .text-primary` and a `tracking-[0.25em]` kicker, only on the edge stores. Platform-wide (every template), not a Qamar defect; a pick-time warning in `ColorField` is the right fix and is out of this task. Cost if wrong: a merchant who picks near-white as the brand colour gets illegible prices until they change it.
