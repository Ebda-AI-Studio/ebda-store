# B4-21

> Section: 2026-10-22 — Phase B4 Lamma (B4-\*)

Ruling B4-21 (final review minors, 2026-10-22) — amends B4-PLP-1, B4-17, B4-16. (1) The listing grid is `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(11.5rem, 20rem))` centred from `lg` on every template (Dahab keeps its density control): empty tracks collapse, so 3 dishes fill the row and 1-2 centre at 320px instead of leaving ~350px empty, and no card is wider than 20rem. Residual: Modern's card caps itself at 200px, so on a 2-product page it floats in a 320px track. (2) Family table cart and checkout panels (`main .bg-card.shadow-sm` under `[data-look='lamma.table']`) are white sheets with a pomegranate-tinted hairline and a soft lift; dish cards keep the surface. (3) Arabic at `lg` and up inside `main` on every Lamma look reads one step larger (`text-sm` 15px, `text-xs` 13px; the measured sizes were 14/12px, identical across looks, which an Arabic face reads small at 1280). (4) `StickyHeaderOffset` publishes 0 unless the sticky element has room to stick (its parent taller than it): the default inner `<header>` sits in a wrapper of its own height, never sticks, and used to publish its own height, leaving the menu strip a header-sized gap. Tested in `souq-stop2-a11y.test.tsx` (sticky in a tall parent = its height; in a parent as tall = 0px). Cost if wrong: (1) a store of 1-2 products sees centred narrow cards; (3) Lamma Arabic is 1px larger on desktop.
