# TEMPLATES-MOCK-7

Ruling TEMPLATES-MOCK-7 - P5 card chrome. `theme.cardHeart`, `cardSwatches` and `cardTags` (`on` | `off`) are absent-until-picked keys; a look store defaults to `off` (the mock's bare card), a store with no look is untouched. They hide the heart, the colour dots and the tag chips ONLY inside home/builder sections (`[data-section]`): the product list, product page and wishlist keep them, so the wishlist stays reachable. Implemented as `<body data-card-*>` attributes from `lookTraitAttrs` and `data-card-chrome` hooks on the three elements (the dots' screen-reader count is hidden with them, since the dots it describes are gone). The "new" badge and the sale badge are not touched: the "new" badge is already a merchant toggle (`showNewBadge`, off unless chosen) and a sale badge is information. Evidence: `tests/unit/mock-traits.test.ts`, editor-matrix rows. Cost if wrong: a look store without a saved choice loses the home-card heart/dots/tags until it picks `on`.
