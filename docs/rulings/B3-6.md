# B3-6

> Section: 2026-10-21 — Phase B3 Jasmine (B3-\*)

Ruling B3-6 — the star rating shows on the PDP only, never on a card, and the bundle saving is live. B0-4 limits a card to dots plus two fact chips; a rating line is a third element on 13 card paths and per-card stars belong to the loud retailers the taste bar rejects. The PDP line reads `ratingStats` (BOUNDED-stale, 120 s + 60 s edge; review approve / reject / delete / status edits now purge the product caches through `refreshRatingCaches`, which they did not before). The bundle-saving line and the routine "add all" read `GET /stores/public/:storeId/bundle-savings`, computed from `resolveBundle` per request and answered `no-store` (NEVER-stale for price). Evidence: `tests/integration/pdp-rating-bundle.test.ts` (a bundle price edit changes the percentage on the next call; an approved review reaches `ratingStats` and the purge fires), `tests/unit/pdp-rating-bundle.test.tsx`. Cost if wrong: Jasmine cards lack the social proof Gulf beauty shoppers scan for; revisit after launch data.
