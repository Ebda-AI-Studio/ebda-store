# EBDA-FUNNEL-16

> Section: 2026-10-07 — Signup funnel: plan choice, card on file or InstaPay, 14-day Growth trial (branch `feat/signup-funnel`, stacks on #10)

Ruling EBDA-FUNNEL-16 — Platform VAT gets ONE seam: every subscription amount goes through `subscriptionChargeAmount()` in `apps/api/src/services/billing/charge-amount.ts`. That covers checkout, the trial-end and renewal charge, the upgrade charge, the next-charge display, reminders and the InstaPay amount. Its `resolvePlatformVat()` returns OFF until the separate VAT work (`getPlatformVat()`, docs/plans/done/2026-10-07-egypt-vat-and-eta.md) merges; wiring it is then a one-line change. While OFF, the total equals the plan price exactly and there is no VAT line (unit-tested). ON is integer half-up math in both modes: exclusive adds VAT on top, inclusive carves VAT out of the price. Both modes are unit-tested, and the VAT line carries the TRN and legal name for the invoice. Cost if wrong: until wired, VAT is never charged on subscriptions (the intended default).
