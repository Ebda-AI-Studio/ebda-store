# CF-EXP-1

Ruling CF-EXP-1 (2026-10-30, INFRA-CF-1 integration 2) - the job `accounting-post-expense-journal` was ported (it posts through the DO op `journalExpense`), but nothing could reach it for a DO store: `approveExpense`, `postExpense`, `rejectExpense`, `listExpenses` and `getExpense` read and wrote Postgres, whose copy of a DO store is sealed, so a DO expense (created on the DO by `createExpense`) could never become `posted` and the event never fired. They now branch on the store's backend: the status step is the DO money op `expenseTransition` (one conditional UPDATE in one section: approve draft/pending, post approved, reject draft/pending; a second click changes nothing), the two-step rule is decided in the Worker from the platform store row, list/get read the DO, and `postExpense` still fires `accounting/expense-posted`. Errors keep the Postgres twin's text. `Expense` is NOT added to the generic DO-writable models: the only writers are the draft op and this status op. Evidence: `packages/repo/test/do-job-ops-b.test.ts` (expenseTransition), `tests/unit/expense-lifecycle-on-do.test.ts`, `packages/repo/test/do-job-ops.test.ts` (journalExpense), `tests/unit/jobs-money-on-do.test.ts`. Cost if wrong: a DO store's expense cannot be approved or posted (the pre-fix behaviour), never a wrong ledger entry.
