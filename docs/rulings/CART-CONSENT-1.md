# CART-CONSENT-1

> Section: 2026-10-16 — Search program, PR 4, Task 13 (quality eval): SEARCH-T13-\*

Ruling CART-CONSENT-1 (2026-10-21, review item 1) - the checkout box is a purpose-specific consent, and the footer says what the shopper actually agreed to. Unticked-by-default "ابعتلي إيميل لو نسيت حاجة في السلة / Email me about my cart" (shown only when `store.cartEmailOptIn`: the plan has abandoned-cart recovery AND the merchant turned the email channel on). Ticking POSTs `/public/cart/email-optin`, which stores `carts.guest_email` + `guest_email_consent_at` and upserts the store's `newsletter_subscribers` row with `source='checkout'`, `consent_scope='cart_reminders'`, `consent_at`. The send gate is unchanged (an ACTIVE subscriber of the same store, no unsubscribed casing variant, re-checked at send time), so unsubscribe tokens, headers and the one-click route work as before. Scope is what keeps the purpose honest: `cart_reminders` subscribers are EXCLUDED from campaign audiences (`buildCampaignAudience`, `audienceCount`) and from the legacy newsletter broadcast, and their email footer reads "you opted in to cart reminders from <store>" / "اشتراكك في تذكيرات السلة", never "newsletter". An existing newsletter subscriber keeps scope `newsletter` (never downgraded). A deliberate "no" is never overridden: the endpoint refuses to re-activate an unsubscribed address and records no cart consent for it (anyone can type anyone's email into a checkout). Unticking clears the cart's consent but leaves the subscriber row (cancelling it is the subscriber's one-click link). Guests: detection also accepts a cart with `guest_email` AND `guest_email_consent_at`; the abandoned-cart row gets that address, and no `nextStepAt` when there is no customer, so a guest receives ONLY the email channel (push/SMS steps need an account or phone). Evidence: `tests/unit/cart-email-optin.test.ts` (12), `campaign-audience.test.ts` (cart_reminders excluded, red before), `abandoned-cart-detection-entitlement.test.ts` guest cases (red before), `cart-recovery-email.test.ts` footer cases. Residuals: (a) `carts.guest_email` is PII with the cart's lifetime, a copy also lands in `abandoned_carts.customer_email` as before; cart retention governs both. (b) a shopper who unticks but whose earlier tick already created a `cart_reminders` subscriber stays subscribed until they use the unsubscribe link. Cost if wrong: a cart-reminder-only subscriber cannot be mailed a campaign even if the merchant wants to; they must use the footer newsletter form.
