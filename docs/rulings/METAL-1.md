# METAL-1

> Section: 2026-10-22 — Phase B4 Lamma (B4-\*)

Ruling METAL-1 (Ahmad, 2026-10-22; closes final-review P2-5 of #123) — brass and antique gold are METAL, not orange. They are allowed for buttons, chips and accents in EVERY template; nothing else may be orange (Ahmad 2026-10-16 stands). Food photographs may use the looser food-calibrated probe (B4-3: hue 12-45, HSV saturation >= 0.65, value >= 0.60; <= 4 % per photo, <= 6 % per hero or page-wide shot). The per-look allowlists of B2-18 (Night souq), B2-26 (Night chips), B3-2 / B3-14 (Apothecary) and B4-2 (Notebook, including the accent roles and the "metal rule" of saturation <= 52 % / lightness 45-75 %) collapse into ONE band, `isMetal` in `tests/unit/helpers/orange-gate.ts`: hue 38-46 degrees (rounded), HSL saturation <= 60 %, HSL lightness 40-76 %. A colour is forbidden when it is orange-family (B2-2 / B4-13) and NOT metal (`isForbiddenOrange`). The band holds the brass family (`#C89B3C`, `#D3B87C`, `#C99B3C`) and excludes saffron `#E0A526` (HSL saturation 74 %), `#F2B632`, saturated amber `#D4A03C` (64 %), `#E8A33D` and the tan `#C8A06A` (hue under 38), terracotta `#B5482E`, lantern `#E68245`, brand orange `#FF6E01` and the platform amber `38 92% 50%`. Negative controls live in `tests/unit/metal-rule.test.ts` and every gated template's token test now reads the same helper. Consequence: the off-by-one-hex cases of B2-18 / B4-2 are no longer exceptions to guard (the band replaces them), and brass is no longer scoped to one look or one role. The Qamar and Dahab token tests were never gated on orange and are unchanged (Dahab's bronze accent `#9A6E2A` and Qamar Vitrine's `#D8B96A` line are outside this rule; extending the gate to them is a separate decision). Cost if wrong: a near-orange gold between the band and the saffron edge reads as brass to the gate; tightening is one constant.
