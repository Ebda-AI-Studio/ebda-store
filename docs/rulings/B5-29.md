# B5-29

> Section: Lifecycle emails and referral attribution (2026-10-22, branch `feat/lifecycle-emails`)

Ruling B5-29 — the Workshop card matte is captured at the look root as `--workshop-matte`, not read from `--foreground` at the tile (controller, 2026-10-22). Independent taste audit: PLP/home card photos rendered as dark ghosts on teal. Measured: the tile's computed background was `rgb(15,77,75)` (the page teal) because a section on the inverse band re-points `--foreground` to the page colour (`section-bg.ts`), so `hsl(var(--foreground))` painted a teal matte under a multiplied photo. After the fix the tile computes `rgb(255,255,255)` on the PLP and the photo reads (shots re-taken at 390-ar and 1280-en); `dahab-look-tokens.test.ts` pins the rule. Souq Night, Lamma and Jasmine use the same `hsl(var(--foreground))` pattern and were NOT changed here (Lamma and Jasmine already exclude the band with a `:not()`); a Souq Night card inside an inverse band is the same shape and is logged for its own look pass. Cost if wrong: a Workshop merchant who re-colours the theme gets a matte that follows the new ink, never a stuck white.
