# NAV-BAR-FIT-1

Ruling NAV-BAR-FIT-1 - the marketing header bar holds only what fits, on one ladder where items are only ever added as the screen widens: up to 520px the wordmark, the Menu button and search; from 521px also WhatsApp and the Menu's label; from 680px also the language link and Log in; from 800px also the CTA; from 1200px the links sit inline and the Menu is gone. Whatever is not in the bar is in the Menu: language and Log in as rows, the CTA in the pinned pair (Ruling NAV-MENU-ACTIONS-1). English from 1480px keeps the 1200px link spacing (gap 18px, start padding 8px). This widens the phone rule of Ruling MKT-IMPECCABLE-3 (the CTA in the Menu at 440px and under) to every width under 800px. The rules are in `apps/marketing/src/app/chrome.css`; `globals.css` is not changed. Evidence: the finding, measured 2026-11-10 on production (https://ebda.app, read-only, header bar swept from 320 to 1920px): every bar item is one line and `.site-header` clips sideways, so an item that does not fit is cut off with no scrollbar. Arabic `/`: items run past the row's edge from 441 to 456px (by up to 19.5px) and from 521 to 752px (235.5px at 521px, 156.5px at 600px, where the CTA is fully off the screen); English `/en/`: from 441 to 444px (6.6px) and from 521 to 724px (207.1px at 521px, 128.1px at 600px); English from 1480px had 2px to spare. The fix, measured the same day on the built site (service lines on) with the same sweep on 18 pages (studio, service and product pages, Arabic and English): no item past the row's edge at any width, the page never scrolls sideways, the row stays 64px tall. Smallest free space per band, Arabic / English: up to 520px 19 / 84.1px; 521 to 679px 32.8 / 113px; 680 to 799px 55.4 / 140.6px; 800 to 1199px 43.5 / 71.9px; 1200 to 1479px 87.2 / 43.4px; 1480px up 76.7 / 38px. With web fonts blocked (6 pages): no overflow, smallest 46px Arabic and 42.8px English. `tests/unit/marketing-header-actions.test.tsx` keeps the ladder's rules from being deleted; it does not measure widths. Cost if wrong: between 521 and 799px a visitor reaches language, Log in or the CTA through the Menu instead of the bar; a longer label or a new bar item can still overflow, and only a browser sweep shows it.
