# CF-R4-2

Ruling CF-R4-2 (2026-10-30, INFRA-CF-1 PR R4, Ahmad via the controller) - `OrderService.createAdminOrder` has a DO branch: ONE `transactionSync` money op `createAdminOrder` (`packages/repo/src/do/admin-order.ts`), the port of the Postgres `withMoneyTx` and of everything that fed it. (1) REUSE, not a copy: the cart price resolver (`resolveCartPrices`: tiers, the merchant-selected customer's group price, wholesale entitlement, MOQ), the gold gate (`assertGoldLinesPriceable`, `requireQuote:false`), `calculateShipping`, `computeOrderAmounts` / `resolveActiveTaxRate` / `frozenTaxInvoiceFields`, `indexEntity` and the gapless `issueSaleDocument` are the functions `placeOrder` runs. (2) WHAT IT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO, exactly as Postgres: reserve or decrement stock (the merchant confirms availability, the mark-paid path decrements), take a cart, apply promotions, bundles, loyalty, wallet or a COD fee, apply the stock-hold cap or the minimum order value, or refuse an area with no rate (shipping falls back to 0; a negotiated `shippingOverride` replaces it). (3) COUPONS: the rules are enforced by default with the Postgres codes and statuses (`COUPON_NOT_STARTED`, `COUPON_EXPIRED`, `COUPON_LIMIT_REACHED`, `MIN_ORDER_NOT_MET`, `COUPON_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `COUPON_NEEDS_CONTACT`, per-customer limit by customer id or by the guest's phone / e-mail), the discount is `discount`, not `coupon_discount`, as on Postgres; the redemption is claimed in the section (an override still counts the use and only skips the `< usage_limit` guard). The two overrides (coupon rules M-OVR-1, minimum order quantity) are decided in the API from the verified session BEFORE the call (role `COUPON_OVERRIDE_ROLES`, a reason) and recorded as `order_events` (`coupon_override`, `moq_override`, with actor, reason and the violated rules) in the SAME section: an overridden order cannot exist without its record; the audit-log rows follow the commit as before. (4) ERRORS keep the Postgres contract (`PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND` 404, `VARIANT_REQUIRED` 400, `VARIANT_NOT_FOUND` 404, `VARIANT_INACTIVE` 409, `BELOW_MOQ` 422, `NOT_FOUND` 404 for an unknown coupon or customer): the DO answers are NOT passed through `translateDoRefusal` (it renames checkout codes). (5) EVIDENCE: `tests/integration/repo-factory-orders-contract.test.ts` (5 scenarios, real Postgres 17 vs the DO schema, equal traces of return values, error status+code, order, items, coupon usage, events, fiscal documents and cart rows): a guest order with notes, a coupon with a negotiated fee, three coupon violations enforced / refused for staff / refused without a reason / overridden with the event, MOQ refused 422 / staff 403 / no reason 400 / overridden with the event, and the product and variant refusals; the `inventory` is asserted unchanged. Real workerd (`apps/api/workers-do/admin-order.test.ts`, where Postgres used a conditional UPDATE and an advisory lock): 40 concurrent phone orders on a coupon with 7 uses get exactly 7, usage ends at 7; 25 concurrent orders for one guest under a per-customer limit of 1 get exactly 1; 12 concurrent overrides are all recorded and counted (12 events, 10 with violations); 50 concurrent orders get 50 consecutive receipt numbers; 30 phone orders racing 30 checkouts of the last 10 items leave the stock decided by the checkouts alone. Residual, accepted: the order of two simultaneous faults can differ (Postgres reported a missing product before a forbidden override; the API checks permissions first). Cost if wrong: a wrong DO phone order is reachable only for a flipped store and creates a pending unpaid order the merchant can cancel; no stock moves.
