Ruling CI-10 (Store session, 2026-10-29; CI-2/CI-3) - CI-3 hints ADVISE, NEVER BLOCK and are gated twice: the per-store feature `customer_intelligence` (in no plan baseline, off until the merchant or a super-admin switches it on; this is also the merchant's turn-off) and a platform kill switch per hint in `system_settings` (`ci.gate.<feature>`, read uncached). One thresholds module decides what may show: store cell n >= 30 over >= 14 days, cohort cell n >= 100, >= 5 stores, max store share <= 40%, aggregate <= 7 days old, 'elevated' only when the Wilson 95% lower bound exceeds the platform baseline (the cohort's overall cell, else the store's own when the cohort has none). Buyer history is read inside the store that recorded it, never across stores. The fee hint uses the COD AMOUNT band as its proxy because the stream carries the COD amount, not the shipping fee. A static test allowlists the files that may import the hint/aggregate modules, so checkout, order-create and booking cannot read a CI value. Aggregates are cached in-process 5 minutes (BOUNDED-stale; the files change daily); the gate is never cached. Each COD hint shown is logged once per order in `ci_hint_logs` with its n and later the outcome. Cost if wrong: a merchant may see a hint up to 5 minutes behind a fresh run, and the amount proxy can suggest a deposit threshold that a real shipping-fee split would place differently.
