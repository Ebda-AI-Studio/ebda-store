# B5-11

> Section: 2026-10-22 — Phase B5 Dahab (B5-\*)

Ruling B5-11 — assets are unbranded and illustrative (TPL-3 / B2-10). No logos, hallmarks with legible text or readable engraving in generated photos; every file is viewed before commit; the strict orange probe (hue 12 to 50, HSV saturation >= 0.35, 2 %) runs on every asset. Measured on the first pass: FLUX invented text on three of 24 images (a stamp "UTINAL BULLION 2012" on the bar, "13S7 Brillon" and a caption under the chain, "butieley tionls." in a corner of the set), all regenerated on plain grounds; and yellow gold measured 0.4 to 11 % orange before grading. The answer is art direction, not a looser probe: gold-toned pixels are capped at saturation 0.30 inside the band (`grade.py`), so a piece reads as pale champagne gold, and the files measure at most 0.7 %. No people and no skin (skin reads as orange), so the "worn" shots are pieces on linen by a window. Cost if wrong: the demo gold looks paler than real 21 karat gold, which the credits state.
