Ruling AI-SLICE-ATOMIC-1 (2026-11-16, money builder, issue #845) - the per-store daily AI slice is reserved atomically before the model call, on the AuthState Durable Object counter (`redis.incrementRateLimit` = INCR + EXPIRE through the ioredis-shaped `CfRedis`), not in a D1 `UPDATE ... WHERE used < cap` row. `assertAiQuota` takes one slot (`lib/ai-store-reservation.ts`), then refuses when `ai_usage rows today + slots in flight (itself included) > cap`; the counter holds only IN-FLIGHT calls in two 120 s buckets (240 s key TTL), so it needs no seeding and no midnight reset, and the rows stay the source of truth for the day. A refusal gives its slot back at once; `recordUsage` (the single funnel every successful call passes) releases the slot once the row exists, so a settled call is not counted twice and no call site can forget it; a failed call never reaches `recordUsage`, so its slot expires with its bucket (at most 4 minutes) instead of being refunded by hand. The global daily and monthly caps and the bulk interactive reserve are unchanged (still count-based). Why the DO and not D1: the primitive already serves the rate limiters and lockouts, needs no table, migration or per-store D1 hop, and the number only has to be exact for the seconds a model call lasts. Infrastructure failure falls open to the old count-only behaviour, like every limiter here. Evidence: `tests/unit/ai-quota-atomic-slice.test.ts` (20 parallel calls at cap-1 give exactly 1 success; fails on the old code). Cost if wrong: after a model failure the store loses at most one slice slot for up to 4 minutes per failed call (it never gains extra AI), and if the counter were unavailable the store is back to the pre-fix race.
