# B5-16

> Section: 2026-10-22 — Phase B5 Dahab (B5-\*)

Ruling B5-16 — max rate age is 26 hours by default and is a hard ceiling; the merchant may lower it to 1 hour (Task 10/12). Why 26 h. A daily rate typed at 10:00 must survive until the next morning's entry with a 2-hour grace. Past the ceiling, fail closed. Checkout (all three order paths, B5-17) throws 409 `GOLD_RATE_STALE` with "مش هنقدر نكمّل الطلب دلوقتي لأن سعر الدهب ما اتحدّثش النهارده. جرّب تاني بعد شوية أو كلّم المحل." The PDP and cart disable add/checkout with the same sentence. The storefront decides display staleness client-side from `rateAppliedAt` + `maxAgeMinutes` in the payload, so time-based staleness needs no cache purge. Merchant alerts. At 22 h the owner gets an email and the admin dashboard a banner ("حدّث سعر الدهب عشان المحل يفضل يبيع"). At 26 h: a second email, a persistent "البيع واقف" banner, and a store-scoped P1 to operators. Each alert fires once per rate (sentinel). Cost if wrong: a merchant who skips a day loses sales until they type a rate. That is the requirement ("never sell at a stale price silently"), and the 22 h warning plus the automatic rate exist to make it rare.
