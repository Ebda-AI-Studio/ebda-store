# STUDIO-CORE-1

Ruling STUDIO-CORE-1 - ebda.app presents Ebda AI Studio as an AI and software company at the core: `BRAND.services` holds exactly three categories, in this order: software (`software`), artificial intelligence (`ai`), and consulting and training (`training`, re-scoped to AI and software: AI adoption workshops, training teams on AI tools and automation, technical and architecture advisory). Marketing, advertising and media, home engineering, interior and exterior design, and events are removed from every consumer: studio home and About grids, contact-form topics (the API enum is derived from `BRAND.services`, so `marketing`, `homes`, `design` and `events` now fail with `invalid_topic`), Organization JSON-LD `knowsAbout` / `makesOffer`, `llms.txt`, About and meta copy (the studio line is "an Egyptian studio for AI and software") and `docs/api/studio-enquiry.md`. Ahmad decided 2026-11-09. Evidence: `tests/unit/studio-nav-footer.test.tsx`, `contact-enquiry-shared.test.ts`, `contact-enquiry-route.test.ts`, `marketing-contact-form.test.tsx`, `marketing-site.test.tsx`. Accepted residual: Ebda Chat's own marketing-message feature, merchant store verticals (fashion, food, jewellery) and the privacy notice's advertising-cookie clause describe products and operations, not studio services, and stay. Cost if wrong: an enquiry for a removed service has no matching topic and goes under "other", and the site underplays work the studio still takes on until those services get their own homes.
