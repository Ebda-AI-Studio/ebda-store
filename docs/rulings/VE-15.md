# VE-15

> Section: Lifecycle emails and referral attribution (2026-10-22, branch `feat/lifecycle-emails`)

Ruling VE-15 (controller, 2026-10-22) - the classic page builder no longer edits the product, products, categories, cart and checkout page design, the filter panel or the reviews switch. Those keys are owned by the visual editor's per-page documents (VE-13), and a home publish keeps their live value, so a classic save of them would show "saved" and never reach the storefront. The cards are replaced by links to the visual editor's page settings; the classic form strips the owned keys from its save (`withoutPageOwnedKeys`); `PUT /admin/stores/:id/layout` answers 400 `PAGE_SETTINGS_MOVED` for any of them instead of dropping them silently. No dual-write path: the classic editor is retired in Task 12 and there are no real merchant stores. Evidence: `tests/unit/layout-save-purges-storefront-cache.test.ts` (400 per key), `tests/integration/page-builder-pages.test.ts` (an editor save of `productsPage.bgColor` reaches the public layout). Cost if wrong: a script or old client that still PUTs those keys gets a 400 and must move to the page-document API.
