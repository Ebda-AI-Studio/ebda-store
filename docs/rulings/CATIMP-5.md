Ruling CATIMP-5 - untrusted PDFs are never parsed on the request path. The upload route checks magic bytes and size and stores the original; the job step runs the bomb pre-scan (more than 8 /ObjStm, a /Filter chain over 2, a declared /Length larger than the file, and a streaming inflate probe fed 2 KB at a time that stops at 48 MiB total), then the page-tree count, then the page cap BEFORE any page is copied, then the size cap on the saved output. A refusal fails that file (`PDF_TOO_COMPLEX`, `UNREADABLE_PDF`, `TOO_MANY_PAGES`, `PDF_TOO_LARGE_AFTER_NORMALIZE`), uncharged, and still consumes one of its 3 attempts. Evidence: tests/unit/catalog-import-sniff.test.ts (object-stream hidden pages, 100 MiB inflate bomb, huge declared /Length), tests/integration/catalog-import.test.ts. Cost if wrong: a legitimate PDF with unusual structure is refused until re-exported; a bomb under 48 MiB inflated still costs a job step some CPU.
