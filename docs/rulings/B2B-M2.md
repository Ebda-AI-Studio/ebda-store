# B2B-M2

> Section: B2B wholesale pricing (feat/b2b-wholesale, 2026-10-21)

Ruling B2B-M2 (coordinator, money review M-2) - Free-shipping coupons are ALLOWED on tier / group priced carts. Shipping is not a price discount; the B2B-5 exclusion applies to the discount BASIS (percentage / fixed amounts), not to the shipping line. Cost if wrong: a wholesaler's tier cart ships free with a free-shipping code; the merchant limits such codes by scope or minimum.
