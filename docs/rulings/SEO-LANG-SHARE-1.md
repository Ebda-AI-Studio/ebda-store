# SEO-LANG-SHARE-1

Ruling SEO-LANG-SHARE-1 - an English SEO field is "written in Arabic" only when MORE THAN HALF of its letters are Arabic script (`isMostlyArabic`, `apps/admin/src/app/stores/[id]/seo/language-guard.ts`); digits, spaces and punctuation count for neither side, and text with no letters is not Arabic. The admin AI-suggestion guard (`normalizeLanguagePair` / `applyAiSeoSuggestion` / `applyAiLanguagePair`, used by the store SEO page, product edit and category form) swaps and drops by this rule, and the SEO page's manual-save refusal (`arabicInEnglishFields`) uses it too; the Arabic slot keeps its old rule (it must contain some Arabic). Before, one Arabic letter dropped or refused the whole English title, so "Qamar قمر — Evening Dresses" (the brand's Arabic name, 3 of 15 letters) was lost (PR #512 review). The API's own check (`seoLanguagesOk`, `apps/api/src/services/ai.service.ts`) keeps its stricter 30% ceiling for English but now skips the product/store name words in the English fields too, as it already did in the Arabic ones, and judges a field made only of those words whole. Evidence: `seo-language-guard.test.ts` (both cases: an English title with the Arabic brand kept whole, a mostly Arabic one dropped, a swapped pair whose Arabic slot is English-with-brand swapped back) and `tests/unit/ai-seo-language.test.ts`; the four new admin cases fail on the old guard. Cost if wrong: an English field that is about half Arabic is accepted as English; the merchant sees it in the field before saving and can edit it.
