# NZ-D15B

Ruling NZ-D15B (Ahmad 2026-11-24, plan N-24) - the closed-store purge. A closed Durable Object store is purged only when 7 years (RETENTION_YEARS) have passed since its last fiscal document, or 90 days after closure when it never issued one. Order: owner notice at least 30 days before; export every fiscal record to the private archive (write-once); read the archive back and require identical row count and sha256; only then wipe the Durable Object (which itself refuses unless it is closed and its fiscal row count equals the verified export) and hard-delete the directory row and the store's platform rows. Any mismatch aborts, raises a P1 and deletes nothing. DRY RUN is the default: live needs `CLOSED_STORE_PURGE_LIVE=true` and a date on or after 2026-12-24; dry run exports and verifies but wipes nothing. Basis: tax-record retention (Law 206/2020 Art. 37, Law 17/1999 Art. 26 as cited in RETENTION-1, 5-year minimum + 2 buffer), then storage limitation; NOT counsel-verified. Rollback: none after the wipe. Evidence: `tests/unit/closed-store-purge.test.ts`, `apps/api/workers-do/closed-store-purge.test.ts`. Cost if wrong: an unverified export plus a wipe loses a merchant's tax record permanently; too early breaches the retention law; too late only keeps data longer than needed.
