Ruling CF-JOBS-2 (2026-10-29, INFRA-CF-1 PR E; supersedes the Inngest-pause half of CUTOVER-3) - `WRITES_FROZEN=1` freezes EVERY writer, not only HTTP, and nothing is dropped. Inngest is gone (G5-3), so "pause Inngest in its dashboard" is obsolete and Ahmad has no dashboard step. Per entry point: Cron Trigger - a sweep (the same cron fires again within 6 h) skips the tick, because the next tick after the window does the work; anything rarer is started and parked (a rare legacy cron is queued as a `legacy-cron` message), never skipped. Queue consumers (email, sms, jobs, CI, WhatsApp store events, every DLQ) - `parkIfFrozen` at the top of the Worker's `queue()` re-sends each message to its own queue with a 300 s delay and acks only after the re-send succeeded (no delivery attempt is spent, so a long window cannot reach the DLQ); a queue this Worker does not produce to, and every DLQ, is retried with an exponential delay (5 min doubling to 12 h); the jobs consumer also re-sends in-handler (`frozenWaits`, 24 h cap, then the platform retry/DLQ path alerts). Workflow - the instance sleeps (`freeze-wait-N`, 5 min) before its first step and before EVERY later step, releasing its concurrency leases for the sleep, and aborts loudly after 288 waits (24 h). DO alarm - `StoreScheduleBase.fire` leaves `next_at`, `fence` and `attempts` untouched, sets `retry_at` 5 minutes out and re-arms, so the same occurrence (same event id) runs after the thaw. The flag is read from `process.env` or the bindings and is on only for exactly `1`. No job is exempt (even `heartbeat`, a log line, is skipped as a sweep). Evidence: `tests/unit/jobs-freeze.test.ts` runs all jobs of JOBS_MAP through the queue runner (no handler runs, every message re-sent and acked, the same message runs once after the thaw) and the Workflow runner (every job sleeps before its first step, runs its body only after the thaw), plus the cron, legacy-cron, mid-run-freeze, 24 h-cap, DLQ and queue-parking cases; workers-test `store-schedule.test.ts` proves the alarm on workerd. Residuals: (a) a step already executing when the flag lands finishes (at most one per instance; the runbook waits 2 minutes and then re-counts rows), and the failure path of a job that fails in that moment can still mark a row failed; (b) an alarm frozen for over 24 h skips the stale occurrence (CATCH_UP_MS); (c) a 12 h cron parked in the window runs once after it, not once per skipped tick. Cost if wrong: one background write lands between the final dump and the flip and is lost on the Cloudflare side (demo data only before launch, CUTOVER-1); the runbook's three-count re-check at the end of the window catches it.
