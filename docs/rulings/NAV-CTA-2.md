# NAV-CTA-2

Ruling NAV-CTA-2 - on a service page (`/services/` and every `/services/<line>/`, both locales) the header CTA reads "كلّمنا / Talk to us" (`SERVICES_PAGE[locale].headerCta`) and opens the contact form (`/contact/#form`; on a line page `/contact/?topic=<line>#form`, the same link as that page's own hero button, so the service is preselected). It is the short label: the hero's sentence "احكيلنا عن شغلك / Tell us about your work" (`contactCta`) stays on the hero button and is not used in the header, where the bar has room for two words. The CTA sits in the header bar from 800px up and in the Menu's pinned pair under 800px (Rulings NAV-BAR-FIT-1 and NAV-MENU-ACTIONS-1). Every other studio page keeps "اختار منتجك / Choose a product" (Ruling NAV-CTA-1, unchanged; this is its one exception) and product pages keep their product CTA. The swap exists only in a build with the service lines on (`EBDA_SERVICE_LINES=on`); with them off every studio page keeps NAV-CTA-1. Evidence: owner feedback 2026-11-10 ("the choose product and whatsapp button are not good there"); a visitor on a service page is choosing a service, and the product windows the old CTA led to are a different offer. Measured 2026-11-10 on the built site (service lines on), header bar swept from 320 to 1920px on `/services/`, `/services/marketing/`, `/services/design/`, `/en/services/`, `/en/services/marketing/` and `/en/services/events/`: no item leaves the bar at any width. Smallest free space left in the bar with the CTA in it: Arabic 90.4px (800 to 1199px), 134.1px (1200 to 1479px), 123.6px (1480px up); English 132.6px, 104.1px, 98.6px. Under 800px, CTA in the Menu: Arabic 19px (up to 520px), 32.8px (521 to 679px), 55.4px (680 to 799px); English 84.1px, 113px, 140.6px. Landing, from the Menu's CTA at 390x844: `/services/marketing/` opens `/contact/?topic=marketing#form`, `/en/services/design/` opens `/en/contact/?topic=design#form`, `/services/` opens `/contact/#form`; in all three the form's top edge is at 65px, the header's bottom edge (65px), and the topic field holds `marketing`, `design` and nothing. Cost if wrong: a service-page visitor who wanted a product needs the Products menu instead of one button; reverting is one expression in `apps/marketing/src/components/Header.tsx`.
