Ruling CATIMP-3 - catalogue import runs on the repo's Cloudflare job layer (`catalog-import-run` and `catalog-import-create-drafts`, kind `workflow`, group 4-ai, one durable step per file or per item), through the same `inngest.createFunction` registration shim every job uses; no Inngest service is involved. A job is never orphaned: enqueue happens after commit and a throw marks it failed (`ENQUEUE_FAILED`) and deletes the originals; `onFailure` marks it failed; a job stuck queued or processing is failed on read (`NOT_STARTED` after 10 min, `STUCK` after 30 min); expired jobs (30 days) and their private objects are purged on the next list by that store. Evidence: tests/integration/catalog-import.test.ts. Cost if wrong: an inactive store's expired originals stay in the private bucket until its next visit, so production needs an R2 lifecycle rule on stores/_/catalogimport-_ (not creatable by prefix wildcard) or a super-admin purge sweep.
