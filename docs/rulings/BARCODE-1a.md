Ruling BARCODE-1a (Ahmad via controller) - the scan self-check for 1D barcodes is an IN-HOUSE reader (`packages/shared/src/barcode/scan1d.ts`), not a dependency: the maintained 1D readers are WASM blobs or fail the <12 months / maintained rule. It reads 15 scanlines through the rendered image, thresholds each at the midpoint of its own light/dark levels (retrying +/-12%), measures run lengths, decodes Code 128 (sets B and C with switches, check character verified) and EAN-13 / UPC-A (parity pattern and check digit verified), also right-to-left, and PASSES only when at least 2 scanlines agree on the value AND it equals the expected value. A line fails with no quiet zone (< 6 modules each side) or light/dark spread < 80. Proof: `tests/unit/barcode-scan1d.test.ts` (187 tests: every one of the 24 BARKOD shapes plus plain at three real sizes against our own encoder, synthetic Code 128 / EAN-13 / UPC-A, and the failures that must fail: no quiet zone left/right, faint print, inverted print, corrupted module, bad check digit, one lucky scanline, wrong value, a shape too coarse for its size; differential against zxing in tests only). Mutation check `node scripts/ci/mutation-barcode-scan1d.mjs` removes one rule at a time (checksum, quiet zone, contrast floor, 2-line agreement, EAN check digit, reversed reads, value comparison, pattern tolerance): 8 of 8 killed. Residual: a clean synthetic raster is easier than a phone camera; the reader proves geometry, not lighting or focus. Cost if wrong: a label that passes the check can still fail on a worn thermal head, so the page keeps the "test one printed label with a phone" note.
