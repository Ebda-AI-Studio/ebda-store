# COMING-SOON-CRM-ERP-ONLY-1

Ruling COMING-SOON-CRM-ERP-ONLY-1 - SUPERSEDES the "coming-soon" parts of SERVICES-IA-1 (Quote Studio, Invite, Ads named as coming-soon software, and the Marketing/Design/Events service lines as public pages) and SUBDOMAIN-SOON-2 (a public `<label>.ebda.app` coming-soon page for crm, erp, invite, ads, quote). From now on the ONLY coming-soon products shown publicly are **Ebda CRM** and **Ebda ERP** (COMING-SOON-CRM-ERP-1 stays in force). **Ebda Quote Studio, Ebda Invite and Ebda Ads are not public**: no card, nav row, llms.txt line, sitemap entry, subdomain coming-soon page or contact topic names them in public copy (Ahmad's public-copy rule: nothing is announced publicly that is not offered or next in line). Scope of the supersession, stated so it is not read wider: (1) public copy and public pages only; the brand names stay reserved in `BRAND` and the reserved-slug list so no merchant can claim `invite`, `ads`, `quote`, `quote-studio` as a store slug and the hosts never reach the store lookup (that half of SUBDOMAIN-SOON-2 is kept); (2) the studio Worker answers those hosts with the neutral not-found page (404) or a redirect to ebda.app, NOT a coming-soon template; (3) CRM and ERP keep their `crm.ebda.app` / `erp.ebda.app` pages and home cards unchanged; (4) SERVICES-IA-1's service lines stay behind `EBDA_SERVICE_LINES`, and nothing in them may name the three removed products. Follow-up implementation (NOT part of this ruling PR; a separate code PR must land it): trim `BRAND.comingProducts` to crm + erp, remove the invite/ads/quote cards, nav rows, llms.txt lines, contact topics and `studio-pages` coming-soon labels, and update `studio-coming-soon-pages`, `web-cross-tenant-cache`, `marketing-service-lines`, `brand-name-never-bare` and `store-host-resolver` tests that pin the five-product list. Evidence: the two superseded rulings and `docs/rulings/COMING-SOON-CRM-ERP-1.md` as read 2026-11-11; instruction from the coordinator quoting Ahmad. Cost if wrong: Quote Studio, Invite and Ads have no public teaser until the ruling is reversed (low: the pages were coming-soon only and the names stay reserved); until the code PR lands, production still shows the five-product list that this ruling says is wrong.
