Ruling WA-OPTIN-WORDING-1 (Controller, 2026-10-27) - contract v1.5: `customer.consent_changed` gains the optional `wording: { text, locale }`, the exact words shown beside the checkout's WhatsApp opt-in tick in the locale the buyer saw (ar or en), as proof of what was agreed. Present only when `marketingOptIn` is true and the Store recorded the locale; a withdrawal has none. The words live in ONE place (`packages/shared/src/whatsapp-optin-wording.ts`: label then note) and the checkout renders from the same constants, so the proof cannot drift from the screen. Residual: the wording is the CURRENT text for the recorded locale, not a per-row snapshot; if the sentence is ever changed, rows ticked under the old words would be re-announced with the new ones until a per-row wording column exists, so a wording change needs that column first. `CONTRACT_VERSION = '1.5'` is exported by wa-v1.ts; envelope `v` stays 1 (additive). Cost if wrong: a consent could be evidenced with words the buyer did not literally see, after a future wording edit.
