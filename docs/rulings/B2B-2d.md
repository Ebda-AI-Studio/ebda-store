# B2B-2d

> Section: B2B wholesale pricing (feat/b2b-wholesale, 2026-10-21)

Ruling B2B-2d - An admin order or payment link prices as the Customer the merchant explicitly selects BY ID, never from a typed phone or email; with nothing selected it is retail. Record `priceSource` per line, `Order.priceGroupName` and an audit entry with the acting staff id. Cost if wrong: a merchant who types a phone expecting auto-pricing gets retail and must pick the customer.
