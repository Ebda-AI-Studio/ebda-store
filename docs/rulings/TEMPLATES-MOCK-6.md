# TEMPLATES-MOCK-6

Ruling TEMPLATES-MOCK-6 - P4 type scale. `theme.headingWeight` (`strong` | `regular`) and `theme.priceColour` (`accent` | `text`) are absent-until-picked keys; the look supplies the default through `traitDefault` and `lookTraitAttrs` writes it as `<body>` data attributes (`data-heading-weight`, `data-price-colour`) that globals.css reads. Defaults: bold section headings except where the look's display face ships a single weight (`LOOK_FONT_SPECS`: Lalezar, Kufam, Reem Kufi, Markazi, Cormorant... stay regular, as the plan said), and the card price in the look accent, emitted as `--look-price-colour` ONLY when the accent itself reaches 4.5:1 on both the page and the card; otherwise `text`. The DEFAULT is `accent` only for a light-page look whose accent reads 4.5:1 on the page and surface; dark-page looks (qamar.lookbook, souq.night, jasmine.apothecary, lamma.notebook) default to `text` because their light sections re-scope the surface (axe measured 1.2:1 on three of them with an accent default). A merchant pick of accent is honoured, and `accentPriceReads` withholds it only when the composed theme proves it unreadable on page or card (axe measured 1.2:1 on `qamar.lookbook` and `souq.night`, where a pale accent sat on a light card over a dark page, when the first version darkened the accent against the page only). A merchant's own heading/price zone is an inline style and still wins. A store with no look gets no attributes (unchanged); a saved value is never rewritten. Evidence: `tests/unit/mock-traits.test.ts`, editor-matrix rows. Cost if wrong: a look store without a saved choice shows bold headings / accent prices until it picks regular / text.
