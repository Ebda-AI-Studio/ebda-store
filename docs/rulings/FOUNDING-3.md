Ruling FOUNDING-3 - security review of FOUNDING-1/2 (supersedes the "no clawback" half of FOUNDING-2 b). (a) The qualifying payment FAILS CLOSED: an event with no plan, an unknown plan, or a plan priced 0/null (free, comped, granted) never qualifies, whatever the amount (mutation-checked). (b) Every grant stores its source payment (`source_event_id`, `source_store_id`; a referrer's grant points at the REFERRED store's payment). When that payment is refunded in full (gateway refund via settleRefundInTx, or a manual full reversal / void) every live grant it backs goes `revoked` (idempotent conditional UPDATE; the DB trigger makes revoked final), the free month is taken back off the beneficiary's period exactly once (`restored_at`, under the subscription row lock; own store inline in the refund transaction so the manual flow sees the corrected period, a referrer in another tenant via `restoreRevokedGrants` right after and in the daily sweep), a revoked founding grant gives its slot back and a revoked referral reward frees the referrer's cap slot. The daily sweep also revokes grants whose source payment is `refunded` when a refund path missed them. `grantedPaidThrough` counts only `applied` grants and, for a grant backed by the store's own payment, only while that payment is still `succeeded`. (c) There is no chargeback handling in billing yet; the day one is added it must call `revokeGrantsForEvent` for the disputed billing_events row. (d) A partial refund does not revoke (the month was paid for). Evidence: tests/integration/billing-grants.test.ts (fails-closed, refund revokes + restores once + frees the slot, referred refund revokes both sides and the referrer's month via the sweep; M1 fail-closed and M2 sweep-revoke mutations fail the suite; the once-only take-back is held by the DB trigger). Cost if wrong: a referrer's take-back after a webhook-driven refund can lag up to one daily sweep (about a day of one free month).
