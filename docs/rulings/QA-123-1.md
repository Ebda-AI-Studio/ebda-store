# QA-123-1

Ruling QA-123-1 - plan change on an active paid period (Amelia, Business yearly, 2026-11-20). (a) "Keep my plan" (`resumeSubscription`) restores plan AND interval: the picker overwrote `billingInterval` with the new plan's interval, so it is re-derived from the paid period's own dates (`intervalOfPeriod`: >= 300 days = yearly); no new column. The current plan card offers the interval switch when the picker interval differs from the store's. (b) A transfer for a pending DOWNGRADE (cheaper than the current period's price) before the current period is within 7 days of its end (`DOWNGRADE_PAYMENT_WINDOW_DAYS`) is NEVER refused at the receipt step: InstaPay is transfer first, receipt second, so the money may already be sent (never-reject-an-external-money-fact). The receipt becomes a pending claim flagged `PAYMENT_NOT_DUE` for the operator. Until the window opens the merchant billing page shows NO amount, payee or QR for it (a wait notice instead; `instapay.notDue`). The operator's record-payment refuses it (`409 PAYMENT_NOT_DUE_YET`, the claim stays pending; an explicit period bypasses) until the window; in the window the approval runs the unchanged period rule (EBDA-MONEYA-6): the paid period starts at the CURRENT end, so the review dialog can never show a period a year ahead for an early payer. Residual (accepted): the tier switches at approval, at most 7 days before the current period ends; a prepaid rollover that applies the downgrade exactly at the period end needs a new subscription column and the D1 commit SQL, not done. Upgrades unchanged. Receiver compare: the wrong-account FLAG compares mobiles in full E.164 (`comparableReceiver` via `normalizeEgyptianMobile`), no longer the last 10 digits. Payee: Ebda's InstaPay account has two identifiers, `EBDA_INSTAPAY_HANDLE` and `EBDA_INSTAPAY_MOBILE` (Ahmad: accept the phone too); `instapayPayees()` feeds the receipt check and the merchant page shows both; any other recipient is still RECEIVER_MISMATCH. Evidence: tests/unit/qa123-billing-plan-change.test.ts (5 mutations red) + integration `instapay-receipts` QA-123 case (accepted+flagged, refused early, approved in window at the current end; runs in CI, not run locally: no migrated test DB). Not verified: live (no deploy); production must have `EBDA_INSTAPAY_MOBILE=01207761882` set, otherwise only the handle is accepted. Cost if wrong: a downgrader's early transfer waits in review until the window (the operator can refund it or record it with an explicit period).
