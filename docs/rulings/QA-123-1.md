# QA-123-1

Ruling QA-123-1 - plan change on an active paid period (Amelia, Business yearly, 2026-11-20). (a) "Keep my plan" (`resumeSubscription`) restores plan AND interval: the picker overwrote `billingInterval` with the new plan's interval, so it is re-derived from the paid period's own dates (`intervalOfPeriod`: >= 300 days = yearly); no new column. The current plan card offers the interval switch when the picker interval differs from the store's. (b) A payment for a pending DOWNGRADE (cheaper than the current period's price) is not due, and is refused `409 PAYMENT_NOT_DUE_YET` at receipt submit and at the operator's record-payment (unless the operator gives an explicit period), until 7 days before the current period ends (`DOWNGRADE_PAYMENT_WINDOW_DAYS`, the reminder window). The period rule of EBDA-MONEYA-6 is unchanged (an early payer's time starts at the current end), so the review dialog can no longer show a period a year ahead for a downgrade, and a downgrader no longer loses a paid tier for nothing. Upgrades are unchanged. Payee: Ebda's InstaPay account has two identifiers, `EBDA_INSTAPAY_HANDLE` and `EBDA_INSTAPAY_MOBILE` (Ahmad: accept the phone too); `instapayPayees()` feeds the receipt check and the merchant page shows both; any other recipient is still RECEIVER_MISMATCH. Evidence: tests/unit/qa123-billing-plan-change.test.ts (3 mutations red). Not verified: live (no deploy); production must have `EBDA_INSTAPAY_MOBILE=01207761882` set, otherwise only the handle is accepted. Cost if wrong: a downgrader who truly wants to pay early is told to wait (operator can still record with an explicit period).
