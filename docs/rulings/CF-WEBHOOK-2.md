(3) SWEEP of count-then-insert over capped tables of a DO store: webhook endpoints (atomic, above); InstaPay receipts (`uploadOnDo`, capped in one section); merchant API keys, tax documents, passkeys, staff, wholesale applications and stores are platform (D1) rows, not DO tables; the plan caps on products and orders were counted on the sealed Postgres copy (fixed by CF-CAP-1). Evidence: `tests/unit/merchant-webhooks-routes-on-do.test.ts`, `tests/unit/webhook-service.test.ts`, `apps/api/workers-do/webhook-cap.test.ts`. Cost if wrong: a test event could be queued for an endpoint of another store, or a 21st endpoint admitted.
