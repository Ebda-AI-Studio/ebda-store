# NZ-JOURNEY-1

Ruling NZ-JOURNEY-1 - on the Postgres-less Worker (NZ-D23) the profile of a merchant, staff member or platform admin (the admin namespace, no store scope) is read from the identity service (`getAccountState`) in `AuthService.getProfile`; `avatar`, `createdAt` and `updatedAt` are `null` there because the identity account state does not carry them. Evidence: production 2026-11-10 16:03:54Z `POST /api/v1/auth/session` 500 `POSTGRES_REMOVED ... (user.findUnique)` with `at async e.getProfile` as the last frame (requestId 31b988c0); `workers-test/merchant-journey.test.ts` reproduces it on the built Worker (500 on `/auth/session` and `/auth/me`) and answers 200 after the change. The same PR sets a DO store variant's opening and edited stock through the stock op `setVariantInventory` (the DO refuses stock columns on a row write, Ruling CF-R7-4), with a compensating delete when the opening stock cannot be set, as `ProductService.create` already does for a product. Cost if wrong: a client that renders the profile's avatar or member-since date from `/auth/me` shows it empty for admin-namespace users until the identity service exposes those fields.
