# TEMPLATES-MOCK-5

Ruling TEMPLATES-MOCK-5 - P3 chrome diet. (1) `theme.tabBar` (`always` | `inner` | `never`) decides the phone tab bar; unset = `inner` on a store WITH a look (no bar on the home page, the header bag + menu are the cart entry, as in the mock), `always` on a store with no look (untouched). An explicit saved value is never overridden; checkout/auth/order routes stay bar-less in every mode. The base header shows its cart bag on phones whenever the bar is off on that route, so the cart is never unreachable (Dahab's bag was already always visible). The body's 128 px reserve is dropped while the bar is off (`html[data-tab-bar=off]`). (2) The demo showroom's notice (the `announcementBar` of a `store.isDemo` store, "photos are illustrative") renders as a footer line instead of a strip above every page; a merchant's own announcement bar is untouched. Evidence: `tests/unit/mock-traits.test.ts`, `tab-bar-menu-dedupe.test.ts`, editor-matrix row. Cost if wrong: a look store without a saved choice loses its home tab bar until it picks `always`; one revert PR.
