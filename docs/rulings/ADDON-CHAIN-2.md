# ADDON-CHAIN-2

> Section: 2026-11-15 - Self-service gaps A8 + A9, add-on term chain (#788, #791, follow-up)

Ruling ADDON-CHAIN-2 (2026-11-15, controller) - a payment that is no longer a paid term must not anchor later terms of the same add-on. Access to an add-on is checked by END DATE ONLY (`activeAddons`: succeeded, not refunded, `forPeriodEnd` ahead). Under the first chain design a refunded term kept its slot, so a re-buy made inside the refunded window was placed AFTER the refunded term's end; its own end then sat that many days past `now + 30`, and because access reads only the end date the merchant got the difference as FREE days (the refunded window), not a wait. (An earlier note in #791 called this merchant-unfavourable; that was wrong, the cost is Ebda's.) The chain now takes the next slot under the same predecessor (`addon-after:<store>:<sku>:<pred>:r<n>`) when the slot is held by a dead payment, so the next term starts at `max(now, end of the latest non-refunded term)`: no gap, no free days.

Ruling ADDON-CHAIN-3 - "dead" means `refunded`, or `failed` for good. A payment that ended permanently `failed` AFTER its marker was written (the D1 settle path writes the marker and the term end before it flips the status) must not anchor either, by the same end-date-only argument. NOT dead: a `failed` row that holds a fresh settle lease (`failure_reason = 'settling'`, `updated_at` inside the lease, `settle-lease.ts`) is a late success being applied right now, and a `pending` row may still settle; treating those as dead would let racing payments overlap. Cost if wrong: a dead payment that still anchors gives free days; a live one treated as dead gives overlapping terms (both bounded to 30 days and covered by `apps/api/workers-do/addon-grant-d1.test.ts`).
