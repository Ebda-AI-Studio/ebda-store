Ruling R-MANUAL-UNDERPAY-1 - `recordManualPayment` refuses an amount below the plan price for the chosen interval, VAT included (`chargeFor(price).totalMinor`, the amount a card would be charged today), with 409 `AMOUNT_BELOW_PRICE` (money-abuse audit S14, issue #841). A super admin overrides with `allowUnderpay: true` plus a written `underpayReason` (min 3 chars, else 400 `REASON_REQUIRED`); the reason and the expected price are stored on the ledger row (`raw.underpay`) and the route audit-logs `allowUnderpay`/`underpayReason`. Overpayment is accepted. The admin form shows the override only after the server answered `AMOUNT_BELOW_PRICE`. Evidence: tests/unit/platform-manual-payment-d1.test.ts. Cost if wrong: a legitimately discounted transfer needs one extra tick-and-reason in the admin form, whereas before a 1 EGP transfer could buy a Business year.

Amendment (PR #860 review, CRITICAL) - the price to cover is `chargeFor(price).totalMinor x N`, where N is the number of billing intervals the paid period spans. With no explicit period N = 1. With an explicit `periodStart`/`periodEnd` (the admin form's "Edit period"), N = `intervalsCovered(start, end, interval)` (`apps/api/src/services/billing/period.ts`): the smallest k >= 1 with start + k intervals >= end, counted in calendar months from the start's own day with the same month-end clamp the renewal rules use (Jan 31 + 1 month = Feb 28/29, + 2 = Mar 31), rounded UP, so a second past one interval is two. Chosen over "refuse any period longer than one interval" because a genuine prepaid multi-month transfer stays recordable at full price with no override, while one month's price can no longer buy a long term; chosen over trusting the interval field alone because nothing tied the period to it. The override (`allowUnderpay` + reason, super admin only: the route is `requireRole('super_admin')`) still works for any span, and the ledger row stores `underpay.expectedMinor` (already x N) and `underpay.periodIntervals`. A span over 120 intervals is refused outright (400 `INVALID_PERIOD`, override or not): that is a typo or abuse, never a prepay. The route audit row logs the service's outcome (`underpaid`, `expectedMinor`, `periodIntervals`), never the client's `allowUnderpay` flag, so a corrected amount sent with a stale flag is audited as a normal payment. The admin form drops the override and its reason whenever the plan, interval, amount or period changes, and shows a specific message for 400 `REASON_REQUIRED`. Evidence: tests/unit/billing-period-intervals.test.ts, tests/unit/platform-manual-payment-d1.test.ts (explicit-period block), apps/admin/src/app/billing-events/page.test.tsx. Cost if wrong: an operator recording a deliberate multi-interval prepay below N x price must tick the override and write a reason, whereas without this a 1-month transfer plus an edited end date bought any term.

Amendment 2 (independent money review of 285809a74, BLOCKING) - N is counted from where PAID TIME RESUMES, not from the operator's `periodStart`: `manualPeriodFor(sub, interval, now).periodStart` = max(now, current paid-through date, trial end), read INSIDE the locked transaction (after `lockSubscription`), to `periodEnd`. Access and renewal read only status + `currentPeriodEnd`, so a period 2036-01-15 to 2036-02-15 keeps the store active until 2036; priced from its own start it cost one month. Now a future start or a gap after the paid-through date is charged for the whole span from the resume date, a normal renewal that starts at the paid end is one interval, and backdating cannot lower N below 1. The guard also runs AFTER the idempotency lookup, so a replay of an already-recorded payment returns that record (`duplicate: true`) instead of being refused after a price rise. Evidence: tests/unit/platform-manual-payment-d1.test.ts (future start, gap, renewal, replay). Cost if wrong: a deliberate prepay with a gap, or a future-dated period, needs the override and a reason; without this one month's price bought a term starting years out.
