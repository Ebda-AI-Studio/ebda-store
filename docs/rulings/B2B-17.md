# B2B-17

> Section: B2B wholesale pricing (feat/b2b-wholesale, 2026-10-21)

Ruling B2B-17 (controller, 2026-10-21; final review P1-1; amends B2B-13) - The return adjustment computes tier eligibility the SAME way the tier was awarded at order time: on the quantity of the whole PRODUCT summed across every loose order line of it (variants and personalisation-split note-lines alike, Ruling B2B-4; bundle components excluded, B2B-8b), and for a variant-level tier on the VARIANT's summed lines. The kept aggregate is repriced with the B2B-3b selection re-run on the snapshot, across every line of the product (including lines not on this return, whose kept units are what loses the tier); the product-level `clawback(after) - clawback(before)` is then allocated over THIS return's items of the product by their standard refund, largest remainder, so the integers sum exactly. The floor (never charge the buyer) and the B2B-14 release clamp (never release more than the product's earlier returns retained) apply per product. Splitting one variant into lines can not escape the adjustment. Evidence: 6 S + 6 M at a 10+ tier, return the 6 S -> adjustment 120 (was 0, merchant lost 120); two note-lines 5 + 5, return one -> 100 (was 0). Tests: `packages/shared/src/__tests__/return-adjustment.test.ts`, `tests/integration/b2b-return-adjustment.test.ts`. Cost if wrong: a merchant who meant tiers per order line sees a buyer charged for the units kept on another line; the per-item override (B2B-15) refunds it.
