Ruling QR-1d - scan safety is enforced, not advised: foreground must be darker than the background and at least 4.5:1 (AA); a logo forces error-correction level H and clears at most 16% of the code area; the design is decoded by an independent decoder (`qr/decode.js`) with the logo hole empty AND solid, and again on the real rendered canvas before any PNG/PDF leaves the browser. A failing design cannot be saved or downloaded; merchants still choose any hue that passes. Cost if wrong: a rare scanner stricter than the decoder fails on a design the check passed.
