Ruling DIGITS-1 (Ahmad, 2026-10-26; supersedes the per-store opt-in of NUMERALS-1) — Western digits (0-9) in EVERY Ebda surface and output, in Arabic and in English: admin, storefronts (all templates), orders PWA, marketing, help, docs, status, accounts, emails, notifications, invoices, receipts, packing slips and labels. Arabic-Indic (٠-٩) and Persian (۰-۹) digits are never produced. `storeNumerals` / `effectiveNumerals` always return `latn`, `withNumerals` / `intlLocale` / `formatMoney` / `localizeDigits` ignore any request for `arab`, the "Store numerals" control, its settings-registry entry and messages are removed, and a stored `theme.arabicNumerals` is ignored (the key stays in the type only so old themes parse). Input stays tolerant: the normalisers that READ Arabic-Indic digits typed by a person (prices, phones, search) are kept. Enforced by `tests/unit/digits-1-western-only.test.ts` (no Arabic-Indic digit in any locale file, help/docs article or non-comment source line outside the listed normalisers; no Intl / toLocale call on a bare or variable locale; no `ar` locale literal without `-u-nu-latn`). Cost if wrong: a merchant who liked the Arabic-Indic look on their Arabic storefront loses it; reversible by restoring the flag.
