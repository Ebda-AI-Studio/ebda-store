# B2B-19

> Section: B2B wholesale pricing (feat/b2b-wholesale, 2026-10-21)

Ruling B2B-19 (fix wave, 2026-10-21; final review P2-2) - A partial return's share of the order discount is allocated over the discount-ELIGIBLE gross only (`allocatePartialReturn`: lines with `price_source` 'tier' / 'group' never got the coupon, promotion or loyalty discount under B2B-5 / B2B-M3, so they carry none of it back; null / 'base' lines carry all of it), by the returned gross, not the tier-adjusted gross. `AllocationLine.priceSource` is a required field, so the GL posting, the provider refund, store credit and the backorder cancel can not disagree. The TAX share stays allocated by credited gross over the subtotal (unchanged): in a mixed coupon cart a single partial return's VAT share differs from its exact VAT by at most rate x the discount it does not carry (25 piastres on a 9,000 order with a 200 coupon at 14%), and the cumulative allocation still telescopes to the order's exact tax once everything is returned. Evidence: base 100 with a 20% coupon + tier 10 x 80, return the tier line -> 800 refunded (was 783); `tests/unit/partial-return-allocation.test.ts`, `tests/integration/accounting/accounting-partial-return.test.ts`. Cost if wrong: a per-return VAT share off by a few piastres in a mixed coupon cart, self-correcting on the last return; allocating tax over net-of-discount would fix it and shift existing rounding by a piastre.
