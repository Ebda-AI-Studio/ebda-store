# SWEEP-BOUND-2

> Section: Audit M-149 follow-up (2026-11-12)

**Ruling SWEEP-BOUND-2 - the abandoned-cart sweep is capped per store and per tick; loyalty-daily is page-bounded and idempotent, and its whole-platform tick is an accepted residual until store count makes it matter.** Measured on main 2026-11-12: `abandoned-cart.service.ts` `loadAbandonmentCandidates` (both the Postgres and the DO branch) and the due-reminder reads had no `take`; one 15-minute tick loaded every candidate cart of every store. Now 200 candidates per store (newest abandonment first: a DO store filters reachability in JS after the fetch, so oldest-first could starve behind unreachable carts), 200 due reminders per store oldest-due first, and 500 per tick (`capDueReminders`). Progress is per row (an AbandonedCart record is created, `nextStepAt` advances), so the next tick resumes. `loyalty-daily` pages through points ledgers 500 at a time with a cursor and is idempotent within the year, but one daily tick still walks every loyalty store; a DO store is one section call. Residual: past roughly a few hundred loyalty stores that tick needs a per-store job. Cost if wrong: a tick that exceeds the Worker CPU or subrequest budget stops mid-way and reminders arrive late (abandoned cart) or birthday bonuses are skipped until the next day (loyalty).
