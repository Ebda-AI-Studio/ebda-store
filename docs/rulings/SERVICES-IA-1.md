# SERVICES-IA-1

Ruling SERVICES-IA-1 - Marketing, Design and Events become SERVICE LINES of Ebda AI Studio on ebda.app (`/services/marketing`, `/services/design`, `/services/events`, plus `/en/...`), not standalone subdomain sites (Ahmad 2026-11-09). Addendum to STUDIO-CORE-1: the home stays "AI and software"; `BRAND.services` stays exactly `software`, `ai`, `training`; the lines live in their own list `BRAND.serviceLines`. Information architecture: a Services DROPDOWN in the header (built like Products and Resources: `siteNavServices(lang)` in the brand module, desktop `<details>` panel plus a mobile menu group; order Products, Services, Resources, About, Contact; entries AI & Software, Ebda Events, Ebda Marketing, Ebda Design, All services) leads to the Services index, which lists FIRST the core (the three `BRAND.services` categories) THEN the three lines as cards; each line is ONE page (hero, the line's path as its signature visual, what we do grouped, how we work, why us, FAQ, contact CTA) in the site's tokens with its accent (Marketing silver, Design brass, Events gold; AA text forms on the light canvas: silver `#4f575e` 7.0:1, brass `#7a5524` 6.4:1, gold `#7b6240` 5.5:1; metals on the night hero 8.8:1 and up) and the generated photographs from ebda-sites PR #2, each captioned "illustrative". The pages read as services people deliver on the ground ("tell us what you need and our team does it"); the software is a footnote linking to the related product. The Products area stays software only: Ebda Store and Ebda Chat live, and three COMING SOON software products (also listed, unlinked and marked "Coming soon", in the Products dropdown; the Arabic and English names live in `BRAND.serviceLines` / `BRAND.comingProducts`, the single source) (names confirmed by Ahmad 2026-11-09: Ebda Quote Studio, Ebda Invite (events), Ebda Ads (marketing); the services stay Ebda Design, Ebda Events, Ebda Marketing; Arabic follows the BRAND transliteration pattern: ابدأ كوت ستوديو, ابدأ إنفايت, ابدأ آدز, still DRAFT wording) with a "notify me" CTA that opens the contact form on the new `products` topic; no dates, prices or waitlist form. Contact: topics `marketing`, `design`, `events`, `products` join the contact-enquiry contract and the API enum (the API accepts them always; `homes` still fails); the CTA opens `/contact/?topic=<id>#form`; email ahlan@ebda.app. VISIBILITY: ONE build flag, env `EBDA_SERVICE_LINES` (`off` default, `preview`, `on`), read by `apps/marketing/src/lib/service-lines.ts`. `off` (production): no page is exported (the post-build step deletes the routes' files, so a visit gets the site 404, never a redirect and never a 200), no nav item, no sitemap entry, no llms.txt block, no JSON-LD, no coming-soon cards, no home band, no form topics. `preview`: pages exist, `noindex, nofollow`, still out of nav, sitemap, llms.txt, JSON-LD, search and home; the form lists the new topics. `on`: nav + pages + sitemap + llms.txt + JSON-LD + home band + coming-soon cards appear together. Why 404, not a redirect: the copy is unapproved, so there must be nothing at the URL to find, cache or index. Lastmod: the four pages are dated now (recorded in `page-lastmod.json` from the unpublished render), so going live needs no lastmod run. DRAFT until Ahmad approves the wording (never claimed as confirmed): the Arabic and English hero lines, FAQ answers, process steps and "what you get" lists, "why us" points and "what we will not do" lists, contact "what happens next", closing lines, the Services index copy, the home band, the "our software" footnotes, the coming-soon names and facts, and the per-page meta text. Confirmed by Ahmad: the service lists, benefits and Events scope (corporate events, conferences, exhibitions, launches, seminars and workshops, gatherings; weddings only as support: flowers, photo, video; no birthdays). Evidence: `tests/unit/marketing-service-lines.test.tsx` (flag modes, every surface, post-build, sections, topics, content rules), `contact-enquiry-shared.test.ts`, `contact-enquiry-route.test.ts`, `marketing-page-lastmod.test.tsx`. Help, docs and status render the same dropdown and coming-soon rows through `siteNavHtml` (the same `EBDA_SERVICE_LINES=on` variable; their deploy workflows pass it through, status as a Worker var). Accepted residuals: the lines' pages have no JS-free language switch beyond the header link. Cost if wrong: Ahmad sees wording he dislikes only in a preview build and flips nothing; if the flag is turned on before approval, unapproved copy is public until the next build with it off.
