Ruling CF-JOBS-1 (2026-10-29, INFRA-CF-1 PR E) - background jobs follow the store's backend through one switch, `JOBS_ON_DO`, and a job that still reads or writes a DO store's Postgres tables is REFUSED, never run. (1) `JOBS_ON_DO=1` makes a store flagged in `STORE_DO_ENABLED_IDS` a "DO store" for jobs; unset (today) no store is, and nothing in `lib/jobs/do-guard.ts` changes behaviour. It is the flag's job-side twin until the D1 `storage_backend` reader (PR A) replaces both. (2) Every job id is classified in `lib/jobs/job-data.ts`: `none` (platform tables, logs: 5 jobs), `ported` (store data only through `storeDb`/`storeWriter`: `wholesale-pending-nudge-cron`) or `unported` (the default: everything else). A run whose store (alarm scope or `event.data.storeId`) is a DO store and whose job is `unported` does not start: both runners (queue, Workflow) alert (P0 for the money/stock set `MONEY_JOBS`, P1 otherwise), ack, and skip the job's own `onFailure` (it would write Postgres). A unit test pins that the sets name real jobs, every group 7-money job is in `MONEY_JOBS`, and that a `ported` job's source calls `withStoreDb` and no `prisma`. (3) Sweeps: inside a guarded run `storeDirectory` and `acrossStores` (packages/database `withHiddenStores`) drop the DO stores, so "every store" cannot reach their sealed Postgres rows; an `unported` sweep alerts which stores it skipped, a `ported` one is silent (its alarm owns them). (4) Alarms: `JOBS_CF_ALARMS=all`, but `armStoreSchedule`/`syncStoreSchedules` arm ONLY DO stores and the global cron no longer skips alarm-ready jobs, so a Postgres store keeps today's cron path and a DO store runs once, on its alarm; an alarm that fires after its store went back to Postgres starts nothing. (5) The first ported job (`wholesale-pending-nudge-cron`) reads through `withStoreDb`: the store's DO when flagged, the Postgres tenant scope otherwise. The other 99 jobs read/write through `prisma`/`withStore` with row locks, raw SQL and money models that have no DO method yet (D2a stock, D2b money); porting them is the work of those PRs, not of this one. Evidence: `tests/unit/jobs-do-guard.test.ts` (14), workers-test `store-schedule.test.ts`. Residual: an unported EVENT job whose event carries no `storeId` (payment-webhook-process, review-request, review-moderation, shipment-create, contact-form-send, legal-notice-send, account-erased-propagation, ci-\*) cannot be tied to a store by the guard, so it runs against Postgres; it is closed by the D1 entity index (D2b), and no store may be flagged DO before then. Cost if wrong: a store flagged DO before D2b could have one such event processed against its stale Postgres rows (a missed or duplicated side effect); the cutover runbook flags no store until D2a/D2b land, and the refusal alerts make the unported scoped jobs visible the day it happens.
