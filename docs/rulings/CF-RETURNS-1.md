# CF-RETURNS-1

Ruling CF-RETURNS-1 (2026-10-30, INFRA-CF-1 last-mile R1, controller) - the Return row lifecycle and the partial-return journal move to the StoreDO as named money ops (`returnCreate`, `returnStatus`, `returnTracking`, `returnClaim`, `returnFinish`, `returnRelease`, `journalPartialReturn`, `journalAmountOnlyRefund`; `packages/repo/src/do/returns.ts`), each ONE `transactionSync` section with the optional `_money_ops` marker-row key (CF-MONEY-1). The store-credit wallet credit and the wallet share of a gateway refund are written in `returnFinish`, in the same section as the completion row, the tier items and the restock (security review: a DO wallet credit issued inside an open Postgres transaction double-refunds on rollback plus retry; here the retry replays, keyed on the return id). The advisory locks of `lockOrderReturns` become the claim (`RETURN_ORDER_BUSY` 409); every Postgres option is honoured (return window, bundle groups, gold exchange refusal, over-claim across requests and backorder cancels, B2B tier adjustments and overrides, partial vs full, wallet share by payment mix, store credit with the wallet feature, record-only settlement of open refund claims, exchange never refunds). `PARTIAL_RETURN_JOURNAL_NOT_ON_DO` and the postgresOnly entries "Return row lifecycle" and "partial-return journal" leave `docs/cf/not-on-do.json`; `postAmountOnlyRefundJournal` (which silently skipped a DO order) is ported with it. The engine holds VERBATIM copies of the allocation and tier math, equal to the originals by test. Evidence: `docs/cf/returns-on-do.md`; `packages/repo/test/do-returns.test.ts` (13), `tests/integration/repo-factory-returns-contract.test.ts` (13 scenarios, Postgres 17 vs DO), `tests/unit/do-return-math-parity.test.ts` (4), `apps/api/workers-do/returns.test.ts` (6 on real workerd). NOT verified: the provider call of a gateway completion (covered by the payment-service contract and the plan in the engine test, not by a Return-level contract), and the `order/refunded` fanout for a DO store (jobs port). Cost if wrong: with every flag at `pg` nothing here runs; a wrong DO return is reachable only for a store the operator flipped (one D1 UPDATE back) and the flip gate stays closed until `not-on-do.json` is empty.
