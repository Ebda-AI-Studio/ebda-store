Ruling AI-PLAN-GATE-1 (2026-11-16, money builder, issue #844) - AI spend needs a paid, trialing or comped plan: no free AI slice. `assertAiQuota` (so every spend path that budgets through it, interactive routes and bulk sweeps alike) asks `assertStoreAiPlan` (`apps/api/src/lib/ai-plan-gate.ts`) right after the merchant switch and before any usage count; a store whose effective plan (`getEntitlements`: a canceled subscription already falls back to `free`) is `free`, unless its subscription is trialing or comped, gets 402 `AI_PLAN_REQUIRED` with `details.{messageEn,messageAr}`, and the code is a sweep-stop code so bulk jobs stop instead of failing every product. The AI-first policy puts AI in every paid plan and `free` is the lapsed/demo state; the audit (S23) measured 150 Gemini calls a day for such a store. past_due keeps AI (the dunning grace belongs to the billing job; a billing suspension is #853's separate refusal). The three AI sweeps that pre-filter on `isAiEligiblePlan(stores.plan)` (stock-reorder-scan, stock-deadstock-scan, answer-engine-citation-scan) keep that cheap legacy-enum pre-filter on purpose: every model call they make goes through `assertAiQuota`, so a canceled store whose legacy enum is still paid is refused at the first call (no spend), and an entitlement read per store inside the daily list step would add N subscription reads for no extra protection. Evidence: `tests/unit/ai-plan-gate.test.ts`. Cost if wrong: a merchant we meant to give a small free AI taste gets 402 until they subscribe (reverse by making `assertStoreAiPlan` return early for free), and a canceled store costs one cheap refused call per sweep run.
