# CF-R2-1

Ruling CF-R2-1 (2026-10-30, INFRA-CF-1 PR R2, Ahmad via the controller) - cart rows, fiscal documents and counters, and the order reads move to the StoreDO; it closes CF-ORDERS-CART-1. (1) FISCAL: `issueSaleDocument` runs INSIDE the `createOrder` section (`packages/repo/src/do/fiscal.ts`), so the order and its number commit or roll back together; the counter is ONE upsert on `fiscal_document_counters` in that same section, so numbering is GAPLESS (80 concurrent orders on real workerd get 60 consecutive receipt numbers, the 20 refused ones burn none; a mixed race of orders, cancels and credit notes keeps RCT and RFR gapless). The document, its payload (lines, discounts, seller, buyer only on a tax invoice, totals) and the unbalanced-invoice refusal (`INVOICE_UNBALANCED`) are ports of `FiscalDocumentService`; the contract test compares them field for field with Postgres (receipt, tax invoice VAT-exclusive and VAT-inclusive, coupon discount line, credit notes). A whole-order cancel or refund settles its document in the reversal section (`settleFiscalOnReversal`: no money taken = the sale document is marked cancelled and keeps its number; money taken = one credit note / refund receipt for what is left, keyed `order:<id>:full`); a partial refund event gets its credit note through `OrderService.applyRefundEffects` -> money op `fiscalRefund` keyed on the refund, capped at what is un-credited, the last credit taking the exact VAT left. The `order/refunded` fanout job stays `unported` (refused for a DO store, CF-JOBS-1), so these two doors are how a DO store gets its credit notes. A missing `store_profile` row refuses the order (`STORE_NOT_FOUND`), like Postgres refuses a missing Store. (2) CARTS: `carts`, `cart_items`, `cart_bundles`, `abandoned_carts` and `abandoned_cart_configs` are DO-writable models; `CartService` (every method), the cart email opt-in and restore, the abandoned-cart admin reads (config, stats, list, one) follow the store's backend through `storeRepo`; on Postgres the code path is the unchanged `prisma`. There is no interactive transaction on the DO: the line writers run `writeLine(doTx)` with the same re-read-and-merge retry on P2002 / P2025 the Postgres ambient path uses, and `addItems` first runs a write-less validation pass (so INSUFFICIENT_INVENTORY refuses the whole payload as the Postgres transaction does) and then writes one line at a time. (3) CART CLAIM: `createOrder` takes `cart: { id }` and the engine compares the cart's rows with the ordered lines and boxes and DELETES them in the order's section; the fingerprint claim (`cartClaim`, `_cart_claims`) of CF-FINAL-1 is removed. 40 concurrent submits of one cart on real workerd create one order; a refused order keeps the cart; a keyed retry whose cart was consumed is answered with the first order (not EMPTY_CART). The cart's open abandoned-cart row is marked recovered in the same section; a guest cart matches only its own row (the Postgres path matched `customerId: null`, i.e. every guest's open row: fixed there too). `rollbackForPaymentFailure` gets a DO branch (cancel section with its note, then the cart rebuilt from the order lines, boxes kept). (4) ORDER READS: `getById`, `getByOrderNumber`, `list`, `getCustomerOrders` and the created-order read go through `storeRepo`; `Order.customer` (a platform `User`) is attached after the DO read with the same select and visibility the Postgres join had. Admin order SEARCH on a DO store is refused `ORDER_SEARCH_NOT_ON_DO` 501 (listed in `docs/cf/not-on-do.json`): its customer and guest lookups are Postgres definers over the Postgres `orders` table and a guest's contact is a JSON snapshot prisma-lite cannot filter. (5) STILL ON POSTGRES, accepted and not hidden: cart PRICING (PriceResolver, public gold pricing, `resolveBundle` totals read the catalog through `prisma`), `newsletter_subscribers` of the cart email consent, and the abandoned-cart DETECTION / reminder sweeps and recovery-email jobs (`unported` jobs: refused with an alert for a DO store by the jobs guard, CF-JOBS-1). Evidence: `packages/repo/test/do-fiscal.test.ts` (8), `do-cart-claim.test.ts` (5), `tests/integration/repo-factory-orders-contract.test.ts` (+5 scenarios; every scenario now also compares the fiscal documents and cart rows), `tests/integration/repo-factory-carts-contract.test.ts` (8 scenarios, Postgres 17 vs the DO schema, equal traces), `apps/api/workers-do/orders.test.ts` (+3 on real workerd: gapless x2, 40 submits of one cart). Cost if wrong: with every flag at `pg` nothing here runs; a wrong DO document or cart is reachable only for a store the operator flipped (one D1 UPDATE back) and the flip gate stays closed until `not-on-do.json` is empty; a number burned in error cannot be reused, so a fiscal bug found after a flip needs a credit note, not an edit.
