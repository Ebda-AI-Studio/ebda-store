# B5-20

> Section: 2026-10-22 — Phase B5 Dahab (B5-\*)

Ruling B5-20 — who may write a rate, and the jump gate (Task 10). Who. Rate writes, rate settings, confirming or rejecting a pending feed rate and the bulk mode switch need `requirePermission('store:settings')` (`store_admin` + `super_admin`; `staff` has `products:write` but not `store:settings`, `rbac.ts:61`). The per-product spec and mode follow `products:write`, like the fixed price staff can already edit. Bounds. A rate lies in 10 000..10 000 000 piastres per gram. A change of more than `jump_confirm_bps` (default 500 = 5 %) from the current rate of the same karat needs `confirm: true` in the request, which the admin asks for with "السعر الجديد أعلى من آخر سعر بـ 7٪. متأكد إنه صح؟". So does a 21k/18k rate more than 4 % away from 24k × 21/24 or 18/24 when they are entered together (swapped fields). Feed rates never self-confirm. A feed rate over the gate is stored `pending`, not applied, and the merchant is alerted to confirm or reject it. Cost if wrong: on a genuine crash day (March 2024 float: about −20 % in a day) the feed holds until the merchant taps "تأكيد". That is a stale-rate block rather than a silent sale at a wrong price.
