Ruling CF-SCHEMA-WA-1 (2026-10-27) - the Cloudflare SQLite schema mirrors the WhatsApp contract migrations (#218): StoreWhatsAppLink and StoreWhatsAppConsent are store-do models, StoreSubscription.past_due_since (WA-GRACE-1) is a D1 column kept by three D1 triggers that reproduce the Postgres trigger (set once on the transition into past_due, kept on a retry, cleared otherwise), the 64-hex buyer_key CHECK has a short GLOB translation (workerd caps a pattern at 50 bytes), and fn-map.json maps the two new Postgres definitions. #218 merged before #205 landed its cf-schema package, so main's cf-schema tests were red until this change. Cost if wrong: a D1 subscription that goes past_due without the trigger would not carry the date to WhatsApp.
