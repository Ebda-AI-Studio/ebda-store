# B5-24

> Section: 2026-10-22 — Phase B5 Dahab (B5-\*)

Ruling B5-24 — CSV carries the gold spec, not the price (Task 13). Export and import gain `pricing_mode`, `gold_karat`, `gold_weight_g` (grams, at most 2 decimals, converted to integer milligrams; a third decimal is a row error, never rounded). An imported gold row keeps its making charge and margin when the product already has them and starts at none otherwise; the spec is applied through `GoldRateService.setProductPricing` AFTER the batch commits (the service runs its own money transaction, and a nested one is refused), so a spec that needs a rate that does not exist yet reports "Imported, but gold pricing was not applied" for that row instead of failing the batch. A row can never change a gold product's price (existing B5-13 rule). XLSX import does not carry the columns (CSV only). Cost if wrong: a merchant who imports gold rows before adding a rate gets the products as fixed-price and a row message telling them why.
