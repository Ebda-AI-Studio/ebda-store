Ruling GEIDEA-2 (controller/Ahmad 2026-10-26) - a payment Ebda can never confirm is a money-path fail-open, so a gateway with no verifiable webhook and no remote status API (`capabilities.webhooks` and `remoteStatus` both false; today only Geidea) is NOT offered to buyers at checkout and cannot be created through the factory (`isOfferableAtCheckout`, applied where the factory builds a store's providers). Merchants may still connect it in admin, shown as "pending verification". It is enabled per store, behind `CHECKOUT_UNCONFIRMED_PROVIDERS=geidea:<storeId>`, the moment Geidea confirms its callback format; marketing keeps it out of the available list until then. Evidence: `tests/unit/checkout-offerable.test.ts` (invariant over every catalog gateway). Cost if wrong: Geidea stays invisible to buyers after Geidea confirms, until the flag is set.
