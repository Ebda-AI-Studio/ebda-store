Ruling CF-JOBS-4 (2026-10-30, INFRA-CF-1, first half of the non-money jobs) - the first 32 of the 65 non-money jobs the guard refused for a DO store (alphabetical, `account-erased-propagation` .. `domain-claim-sweep`; the second half is another track) serve a store on its Durable Object, and 1 stays refused with a written blocker. (1) Classes: `account-erased-propagation`, `ci-erasure-second-pass`, `contact-form-send` and `domain-claim-sweep` touch only platform rows (`stores`, `users`, model-split target d1 / identity) or third parties, so they join `NO_STORE_DATA_JOBS`; 28 more join `PORTED_JOBS`: the 16 bulk AI sweeps (`bulk-ai-*`), `bulk-import-products`, `bulk-import-orphan-cleanup`, `ai-autofill-{cleanup,product,sweep}`, `accounting-profit-anomaly-cron`, `analytics-{daily-rollup,rollup-on-demand}`, `answer-engine-{citation-scan-cron,scan-on-demand}`, `agent-write-denied-watch`, `ci-outcomes`. (2) Data: a job reaches its store only through `storeDb` / `storeWriter` / `storeMoney` / `storeRepo`; a Workflow handler resolves them inside the step (`lazyStoreDb` / `lazyStoreWriter`, because a Workflow holds no connection outside `step.run`). Eleven models join `DO_WRITABLE_MODELS` and `WritableModel` (BulkAiJob, BulkImportJob, StoreFaq, StoreHowto, AiRecommendation, FrequentlyBoughtTogether, ProfitAnomaly, CustomerCohortSnapshot, FunnelSnapshot, CustomerRfmSnapshot, AnswerEngineCheck) and every direct reader of them moved to `storeDb` (the CF-NOTIFY-1 baseline shrank by every bulk-ai site; the one that remains, `queue-depth-watch`, is a refused job of the other half). New DO operations: the in-DO procedure `guardedAiWrite` (the guarded AI product write: re-read, per-field compare-and-set, `human` stamps kept, one atomic section, replacing the Postgres row lock), and the money-client ops `accountTotals` (counted journal totals per account in a posted-at window: profit and loss, trial balance, the anomaly scan) and `ciOrderStamp` (Customer Intelligence area / buyer stamp, only while both are null). (3) Schedules: `bulk-import-orphan-cleanup` becomes alarm-ready (`alarm: true` + `ALARM_READY`); `accounting-profit-anomaly-cron` and `analytics-daily-rollup` already were. Three crons cannot be split into per-store alarms without losing what they exist for (a watcher that ranks stores against each other, a leader-locked sweep with one shared AI quota, a weekly scan with one provider budget): they list every store with `allStoresDirectory` / `acrossAllStoresDb` (which step outside the hidden-stores scope) and reach each DO store through its DO from their one cron run; they are listed in `ALL_STORES_JOBS` and are not "skipped sweeps" for the flip gate. (4) Fidelity notes: the co-purchase pairs of `bulk-ai-recommendations` have no raw SQL on the DO, so they are paired in code from the newest 5000 qualifying orders (the Postgres path keeps its SQL); the JSON-path filter of the CI COD-outcome lookup is applied in code on the DO; `bulk-import-products` now starts only a `queued` job, so a second delivery of its event cannot import the CSV twice (also true on Postgres, where `retries: 0` was the only protection). (5) BLOCKER, still refused (P1 alert, listed in `jobsAccepted`): `accounting-expense-ocr-process` creates a draft expense through `createExpense`, which numbers it, looks accounts up and writes the expense on Postgres; `expense.service`, `account.service` and `journal.service` have no DO path, so the expense CRUD routes are Postgres-only for a DO store too.
