Ruling WA-CONSENT-1 (Controller, 2026-10-25; Store side) — abandoned-cart reminders on WhatsApp are MARKETING: `cart.abandoned` is emitted only for a buyer with an active `store_whatsapp_consents` row, checked at SEND time (a revoke between capture and send also stops it). Consent is unticked by default at checkout (`POST /public/cart/whatsapp-optin`, shown only when Ebda Chat is linked), keyed by the suite buyer key (never the phone), stored with time, source and label language, revocable by the buyer (untick), the merchant (`POST /stores/:id/whatsapp-link/consent/revoke`, revoke-only) or a STOP on WhatsApp (`whatsapp.opt_out_changed`, monotonic `optOutVersion`, `optedOut=false` never re-enables), included in the account export and deleted by erasure. Every write is announced to Ebda Chat as `customer.consent_changed` by ONE observer (`lib/wa/consent-state.ts`), the job re-reading the current row. Order, COD and shipping messages are transactional and do not read consent. SECURITY (Controller review): `POST /public/cart/whatsapp-optin` takes NO phone: the body is `{consent, language}` (strict), the caller must be a signed-in customer with a cart in THIS session, and consent is recorded against the number on their own account, audited with the cart id; guests, other sessions and body phones cannot opt anyone in or out; limited per IP+session and per store (`whatsappConsentStoreLimiter`). Second review: granting also needs PROOF the profile phone is the customer's (it must be the number on an order they placed; no OTP-verified phone exists yet), withdrawing needs none; changing the profile phone revokes the old number's consent (source `profile`, announced); the per-store ceiling counts only signed-in grants after auth, never anonymous calls or revocations, so a flood cannot block a withdrawal. Residual: a customer can tick the box on their own account only; the WhatsApp side enforces its own opt-in and STOP. The v1.1 envelope types (`customer.consent_changed`, `whatsapp.opt_out_changed`) are not in the vendored v1 schema until the controller forwards the pinned commit; until then the emitter is built but its envelope fails validation (it throws, retries visibly) and the consumer is not wired. Cost if wrong: a buyer could receive a reminder after withdrawing, or consent could differ between Store and WhatsApp for the retry window.
