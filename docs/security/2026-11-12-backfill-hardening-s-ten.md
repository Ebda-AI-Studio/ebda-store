Ruling SEC-BF-1 - TEN-7 re-checks lazily at delivery instead of pushing removals from the staff-removal path. Evidence: the user DO is the only place that knows which sockets hold which rooms, and delivery is the only moment data leaves. Cost if wrong: a removed staff member's socket stays open (receiving nothing) until it closes, and up to 30 s of events can still reach it after removal.
