## Getting started

### How do I get started with Force AI Store?

Force AI Store is a **managed e-commerce service** — there is no self-serve signup. Every client
goes through a custom proposal and onboarding process:

1. **Request a proposal** — contact us at [hello@forcemediakw.com](mailto:hello@forcemediakw.com)
   or via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a 30-minute call to understand your needs.
2. **Receive and sign a custom proposal** — we send a written proposal within 2-3 business days
   covering setup fee, annual platform fee, and the feature set tailored to your business.
3. **Onboarding begins** — once you sign and pay the deposit, we handle:
   - Store provisioning and theme configuration
   - Brand setup (logo, colors, fonts, hero images)
   - Catalog import (your Excel/CSV → Force AI Store products, bilingual)
   - Payment provider setup (Tap/MyFatoorah/Hesabe/UPayments account connection)
   - Custom domain provisioning
   - 1-hour live training session
4. **Store goes live** — typically 2-4 weeks from contract signing to live store, depending on
   catalog size and brand readiness.

### Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can use Google Docs, you can use Force AI Store admin. The page builder is
drag-and-drop. The product editor has AI buttons for translation and SEO. The payment provider
setup is "paste your API key here."

We don't sell to developers — we sell to merchants who want to sell, not configure.

### Can I import my existing store?

**Products + categories + customers + orders:** yes, via CSV import. Most platforms can export to
CSV; we provide a template that maps standard fields (name, name_ar, price, category, stock, etc.).

**Themes + storefront design:** no automatic migration. You'll pick from our 5 themes and re-build
your visual identity. This usually takes 1–3 hours; we offer a free migration consultation for paid
plans.

**Apps + plugins:** any third-party apps from your current platform need to be re-evaluated. Many
of those apps exist to fill gaps that Force AI Store already covers natively (translation, SEO,
inventory, accounting, reviews).

## Bilingual + Arabic

### How does the Arabic + English support actually work?

Every text field in our system has an Arabic version and an English version. Examples:

- `name` + `name_ar` on products
- `metaTitle` + `metaTitleAr` on SEO
- `question` + `question_ar` on FAQs
- Even customer reviews — when a customer writes a review in Arabic, our AI automatically generates
  an English version (and vice versa)

When a visitor lands on your store, we detect their language preference (from browser settings or a
manual EN/AR toggle in the header). Every page renders in the chosen language with right-to-left
(RTL) layout for Arabic.

**The admin interface** also flips RTL when you switch your admin language to Arabic. Buttons move,
text aligns right, even icons mirror where it makes sense.

### Do I have to write everything twice?

No. Use our **AI translate button** — one click and we fill the other language for you. You can
edit the AI output before saving if you want. Our AI is tuned for Gulf Arabic specifically (not
Modern Standard Arabic), so the output sounds like a Kuwaiti merchant, not a textbook.

You can also run a **bulk translate sweep** that goes through your entire product catalog at once.
Translates 200 products in about 15 minutes.

### What if I only want English (or only Arabic)?

Both languages are always available on the storefront, but you can choose which one is the
**default** (per store). Customers see the default first; they can switch with the language toggle
in your header.

If you have 100% Arabic-speaking customers, set Arabic as default. The English fields stay
populated (AI fills them) for SEO purposes — Google indexes both versions so you appear in both
Arabic and English searches.

## Payments + KNET

### Do you support KNET?

Yes. KNET works through any of these 4 Kuwait payment providers, all configured per-store:

- **Tap Payments** — most popular GCC choice, KNET + Visa + Mastercard + Apple Pay
- **MyFatoorah** — widely used, KNET + cards + Apple Pay + STC Pay
- **Hesabe** — Kuwait-specific, KNET + Visa/Mastercard
- **UPayments** — Kuwait fintech, KNET + digital wallets

You pick **one** provider for your store, paste their API credentials into your Force AI Store
admin, and KNET starts working immediately. No 3rd-party plugin.

If you don't have an account with any of these providers yet, we recommend **Tap** for new
merchants (easiest signup, best documentation, GCC-wide).

### How much do payment providers charge?

Provider fees are paid by you to your provider (not to Force AI Store). Typical 2026 rates:

| Provider   | KNET  | Visa/MC domestic | International |
| ---------- | ----- | ---------------- | ------------- |
| MyFatoorah | 2.0%  | 3.5%             | 3.5%+         |
| Tap        | 2.75% | 2.75%            | 3.25% + FX    |
| Hesabe     | ~2.5% | ~3.5%            | —             |
| UPayments  | ~2.5% | ~3.0%            | —             |

**Force AI Store does not take a cut of your sales.** Your contract is a fixed setup fee + one-time
yearly platform fee — not a monthly subscription. Payment provider fees (2-3.5% per transaction) go
directly from your customer to your payment gateway — not through us.

### Can I use Stripe?

Stripe doesn't work in Kuwait — they don't have a Kuwait merchant program. The 4 providers above
cover everything you need: KNET, Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay, and digital wallets.

### What about cash on delivery?

Yes — toggle COD on per store. We track it as a payment method and your delivery partner collects
on your behalf.

### What is the AI Product Feed?

An individually-priced add-on (`agent_feed`) available to any plan tier — it's not bundled into
Starter, Professional, or Enterprise by default; a super-admin turns it on per store once it's
sold. Once enabled, your store publishes a machine-readable product feed at
`/feeds/products.json` (e.g. `yourstore.com/feeds/products.json`) so AI assistants and shopping
agents — ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other agent crawlers — can read your catalog and cite or
recommend your products directly, following the OpenAI Product Feed field conventions (title,
price, availability, image, category). Stores that haven't purchased the add-on get a 404 on that
URL — the feed's existence isn't revealed until it's activated.

## Compliance + privacy

### Where is my data stored?

- **Database (Neon Postgres):** AWS region `ap-southeast-1` (Singapore) — closest GCC-available
  AWS region with Postgres serverless support
- **Storage (Cloudflare R2):** global CDN — content served from the closest edge to your customer
- **API (Railway):** US East (planned migration to ME region as Railway adds it)

**Data sovereignty for Kuwait:** Kuwait Law 13/2023 (PDPL) doesn't require data residency for SaaS
providers but does require erasure capability — which we provide (`/auth/me/erase` endpoint,
irreversible PII anonymization).

### Are you GDPR / Kuwait Law 13/2023 compliant?

Yes:

- **Art. 17 (erasure / right to be forgotten):** implemented via our customer profile page →
  "Delete my account"
- **Art. 20 (data portability):** customers can export their data as JSON
- **Cookie consent:** every storefront has a consent banner; tracking pixels
  (Facebook/Google/TikTok) only fire after consent
- **Audit trail:** every admin action is logged with user + timestamp + IP

### Is my customer data shared with anyone?

No. We don't sell or share customer data. The only third parties that see customer data are:

- **Your payment provider** (Tap/MyFatoorah/etc.) — they need it to process payments
- **Resend** — our email-sending partner, sees only the order confirmation content
- **Cloudflare** — our CDN, sees encrypted traffic only

We never feed customer data to our AI features (we only translate product catalog content, never
customer info).

### Where's your privacy policy?

Generated per-store at `/privacy` on your storefront. Edit it from the admin → Content → Privacy
Policy. We provide a Kuwait-compliant template (bilingual EN/AR) as the default.

## Migrating from another platform

### Coming from Shopify

Your product catalog, customer list, and order history all transfer via CSV export/import. Your
storefront design doesn't migrate — you'll pick from our 5 themes and rebuild with the page
builder. Most merchants complete the redesign in 1–3 hours.

KNET, WhatsApp notifications, AI translation, and accounting are all included in Force AI Store —
you won't need the separate apps you may have been paying for.

### Coming from Salla

Product import is CSV-based. If you've been using Salla for a Kuwait audience, you'll find
Force AI Store already built around KNET and Kuwait shipping zones — no workarounds needed.

### Coming from WooCommerce

You'll lose some flexibility (no raw PHP, no plugins) but gain peace of mind (no maintenance, no
security patching, no "developer quit, what now?").

If your WooCommerce site has heavy custom code, Force AI Store may not be a fit — talk to us first.

### Coming from Wix or Squarespace

These are great for static brochure sites but weak for transactional e-commerce in Kuwait (no
native KNET, limited multi-language, no inventory module). Force AI Store is purpose-built for what
they're not.
