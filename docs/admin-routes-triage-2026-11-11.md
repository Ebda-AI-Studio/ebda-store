# Admin and merchant routes with no admin caller: triage (F-9-prune)

Date 2026-11-11. Source: `business/ebda/audits/2026-11-04-t2-uncalled-endpoints-report.md` (the 89 "no text caller" routes). Ruling: `docs/rulings/ADMIN-ROUTES-1.md`.

Method: each route was located in `apps/api/src/routes`, its middleware chain read (authenticate / permission or role / store access / Zod on the body or query), and its callers searched again across web, admin, orders, marketing, accounts, help, status, packages, MCP, tests and docs. Guards column: A = authenticated, P = permission or role or store access, Z = Zod parse in the handler; `-` means not seen by pattern, and every `-` was then read by hand (see "Manual review"). Nothing was deleted: `remove (proposed)` is a decision for Ahmad, because the routes are live API surface.

Result: 55 keep, 34 remove (proposed).

## Manual review of routes the pattern check flagged

All of these were read in the handler or its middleware spread (`...adminMiddleware`, `...guards`, `...writeChain`, `...bankRecMiddleware`, `...mw`, `...writeGuard`, `...guard`): they ARE authenticated and store-scoped. `POST /stores/:storeId/orders-app/push/unsubscribe` deletes with `storeId: currentStoreId()`, so it cannot remove another store's subscription. `PUT /admin/stores/:storeId/layout` and `send-notice` parse their input (`layoutSchema`, `sendParams`). Shopper routes (cart, instapay receipt, payments status/retry) are public or customer-owned by design and check ownership in the handler.

**One defect found and fixed:** `GET /payments/methods` (public) read `amount`, `currency` and `provider` from the query with bare casts and no Zod. It now parses them (`methodsQuerySchema`); an unknown provider or currency is a 400 instead of reaching the service. Test: `tests/unit/payments-methods-query.test.ts`.
