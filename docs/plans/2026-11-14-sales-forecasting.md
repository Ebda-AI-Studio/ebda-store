## 2. Method (pure functions, `apps/api/src/services/inventory/forecast.service.ts` and `forecast-math.ts`)

All inputs are integer units or integer piastres; outputs are numbers with explicit units; no Date in pure functions (days are indices from a given start).

### 2.1 Demand series

- Per SKU (variant if the product has variants, else product), daily units, Cairo days, from `order_items` of orders with `status NOT IN (cancelled, refunded) AND payment_status != 'failed'` and, for `awaiting_payment_confirmation`, excluded (hold may expire; Ruling stub FC-DEF-2). This counts COD orders at creation, fixing P1-4's blind spot; a COD order cancelled later drops out on the next nightly run (series are recomputed from scratch each night over the window, not appended, so late cancellations self-correct). Partially returned quantities are netted on the order's original day (simple and stable, an approximation; returns are a separate measured rate).
- Window: up to 365 days back, or since first sale. Missing days are zero ONLY when the SKU was in stock (below).
- **Stock-out censoring:** days where stock was 0 (from `inventory_history` rows type `sale` with `new_stock = 0` until the next restock, plus current `inventory = 0`) are not zeros: they are `NaN`-censored and replaced by the SKU's in-stock trimmed mean for that weekday for fitting, flagged `censored_days`. Without it a SKU that sold out looks like it stopped selling and the forecast under-orders it again.
- **Promotion/outlier days:** days with a coupon or promotion ending the same Cairo day (the promotion tables exist) are not removed in v1; outliers over `median + 6 * MAD` are winsorised for fitting and counted. Merchant-editable outlier/event flags are phase 2 (the audit asks for them): `forecast_events`.

### 2.2 Per-SKU model choice

1. Classify demand (Syntetos-Boylan on the in-stock series): ADI = mean interval between non-zero days, CV2 = squared coefficient of variation of non-zero sizes. Smooth (ADI < 1.32, CV2 < 0.49) and erratic -> SES / seasonal-naive; intermittent or lumpy (ADI >= 1.32) -> Croston-SBA. (Thresholds are the published cut-offs; they are constants in code with the reference comment, and the backtest, not the label, picks the model.)
2. Candidates: `seasonal_naive` (average of the same weekday over the last 4 weeks), `ses` (alpha 0.3 on daily or weekly totals), `croston_sba` (alpha 0.1), `moving_avg_28d` (the safety net). Optional `holt_winters_additive_weekly` for high-volume SKUs (>= 26 weeks, mean >= 5/day), because a trend term over-extrapolates sparse series.
3. **Selection by rolling-origin backtest on the SKU itself:** 4 origins stepping back 7 days, horizon 7 days, loss = WAPE = `sum|a-f| / sum a` (units). The lowest WAPE wins; ties -> simpler model (order above). MAPE is NOT used per SKU: it is undefined at zero actuals and explodes for slow movers (documented in the code and the UI tooltip). For the store revenue series, where actuals are non-zero, both WAPE and MAPE are reported (the audit asks for MAPE/WAPE per store).
4. Cold start: fewer than 14 days of non-censored history -> the old `max(7d,28d)` velocity, `method = 'velocity_fallback'`, `confidence = 'low'` (nothing regresses, as in the inventory audit).
5. Intervals: P50 = point; P80 = P50 + 0.84 \* residual std over the backtest errors scaled to the horizon (sqrt of days), floored at 0. For intermittent SKUs the band is empirical (the 80th percentile of rolling-window sums), not Gaussian, because zero-inflated residuals are not normal. P10-P90 for the revenue band the same way, calibrated and its coverage measured (below).
6. Confidence chip: high = >= 26 weeks and WAPE <= 0.35; medium = >= 8 weeks and WAPE <= 0.6; else low. Constants in `forecast.config.ts` with the date they were set; changed only with measured evidence.

### 2.3 Egyptian calendar effects (`lib/calendar-eg.ts`, one table, no external call)

- Events: Ramadan (month), Eid al-Fitr (3 days + the 10 days before as the shopping lead-in), Eid al-Adha (4 days + 7 before), White Friday (Egypt's Black Friday: the Friday after US Thanksgiving; the usual retail window is the week around it, treated as an event window of 7 days ending the following Monday), back-to-school (about 2 weeks before the school year; start date is a ministry decision that moves yearly), summer (broad window), Sham El-Nessim and Coptic Christmas/Easter (low retail impact; v1 off), plus the official public-holiday days. Hijri events move about 11 days per year and depend on moon sighting (the day can be off by one): the table is generated from the Umm al-Qura calendar (`Intl.DateTimeFormat('en-u-ca-islamic-umalqura')`; **verify it exists on workerd/V8 ICU before relying on it, otherwise ship a static table for 2026-2032 checked against the official dates**) and stored with `approximate: true`; the merchant can shift an event by +-2 days (`forecast_events`) when the real date is announced. White Friday is computed (a rule), not looked up. Back-to-school dates are manual (`forecast_events`) because they are not a rule.
- Payday: the audit asks for it; the monthly salary peak is widely believed to fall around the 25th-1st but the exact days are UNVERIFIED and differ by employer (public sector vs private). So it is NOT hard-coded: the model fits a day-of-month profile per store (mean ratio of demand on days 25-31 and 1-3 vs the store's own other days, over the last 6-12 months) and applies it only when the store's own history shows an effect larger than the backtest noise (ratio >= 1.15 and consistent in >= 4 of the last 6 months); else payday is off for that store. Reported as `payday_effect` on the snapshot.
- Uplift estimation: ratio of the event window's mean daily demand to the surrounding 4-week baseline in the prior year(s) when >= 12 months exist; else a category-level default factor from a small table in code (clearly tagged `estimated_uplift = true`, shown in the UI as "تقدير عام"), capped at 3x (inventory audit). The default factors are placeholders until measured on real stores; they ship at 1.0 (no uplift) unless Ahmad supplies figures, so an unmeasured uplift cannot silently inflate orders (Q2). Hijri events are matched by event, not by calendar date, across years.
- Multi-event overlap (Ramadan and Eid, White Friday in a Ramadan year) multiplies factors but the product is capped at 3x and the cap is flagged.

### 2.4 Store revenue forecast

- Series: daily net sales in piastres under Ruling stub FC-DEF-1: **order total minus tax minus shipping, minus cancelled/refunded orders and minus recorded partial-return amounts**, i.e. the GL-card definition (refund-aware, ex-tax) but on an ORDER-DATE (booked demand) basis so COD is counted when ordered, not when collected. A second series "expected cash" is NOT forecast in v1 (needs COD collection and refusal rates; it is one of the open questions). The dashboard card labels the basis in words: "المبيعات المتوقعة (بعد الإلغاء والمرتجع، قبل الضريبة والشحن)".
- Model: weekly seasonality (day-of-week profile) times a level (SES on weekly totals) times event and payday factors; backtest on the store: rolling origin, 8 origins, horizons 7/30; report WAPE and MAPE (revenue is non-zero) and prediction-interval coverage. Minimum to render: **8 weeks** of history (analytics audit); the audit's "WAPE < 25% on stores with >= 26 weeks" is a TARGET to be measured per store, never a constant: a store whose own backtest WAPE for the 30-day horizon exceeds 0.45 shows the forecast greyed with "الدقة لسه ضعيفة" instead of hiding it, and below 8 weeks shows "محتاجين على الأقل ٨ أسابيع بيانات" (unknown must not look like zero).
- Outputs: 30/60/90-day totals with P10/P90, weekly path for the chart, top drivers (upcoming events inside the horizon, payday effect on/off). Cash runway is out of scope until the cash-flow statement exists (accounting audit rank 6) and plan 0 makes the cash accounts meaningful.

## 3. Using the projection (reorder, stock-out, surfaces)

- `projectedStock = onHand + onOrder - openBacklog - forecastDemand(leadTime + safetyDays)` where `forecastDemand` is P80 for A-class SKUs (top 80% of trailing-90-day revenue, computed in the same job) and P50 otherwise; `stockout_date = today + onHand / forecastDaily` (day-by-day walk through the forecast path, so an upcoming Eid spike moves the date, not a flat rate); `days_of_cover`; `confidence`; `method`. The suggested quantity keeps its existing formula shape but over forecast demand (`max(forecast over lead+safety+14d review period - available, minQty*2)`), and never exceeds the rule's `reorderQty` cap when `onHand >= minQty` (inventory audit test 4).
- Persist: `reorder_suggestions` gets `forecast_30d, forecast_p80, method, confidence, stockout_date, estimated_uplift` (DO migration + `reorderUpsert` op in `packages/repo/src/do/`, Prisma mirrored only if Prisma parity is still required: Postgres is removed in production). `forecast_snapshots(id, store_id, kind 'store_revenue'|'sku', scope_id, asof_date TEXT 'YYYY-MM-DD' Cairo, horizon_days, series JSON, method, params_hash, input_hash, backtest_wape, backtest_mape NULL, interval_coverage, history_days, censored_days, estimated_uplift, created_at)` with UNIQUE `(store_id, kind, scope_id, asof_date)`: one row per store/SKU per Cairo day, so the job is idempotent per (store, day) and re-running overwrites the same row. Row cap and pruning (resource-frugal): SKU rows only for SKUs with sales in the last 90 days, at most 500 SKUs per store, snapshots older than 90 days (kept: the monthly one) pruned by the same job.
- Every table has a UI: reorder page columns (days of cover, forecast P50-P80, stock-out date, confidence chip, method tooltip: "طريقة التوقع: متوسط موسمي"), sortable by stock-out date; `stock/dashboard` card "هتخلص خلال ٧ أيام" (count + top 5) and event banner ("رمضان بعد ١٢ يوم: توقعاتنا بتتغير"); insights Forecast card (30/60/90 totals, band, basis line, confidence, event chips, "ما فيش بيانات كفاية" state); `forecast_events` editor (shift an event, mark a day as outlier) as a small drawer on the card; the history of snapshots + backtest on a "دقة التوقع" panel (so the measurement is visible to the merchant).
- Narration (optional, phase 3): the existing narration service gets the snapshot numbers and writes 2 sentences; a validator asserts every number in the text equals a snapshot number (pattern: `faq-grounding-validator.ts`), else the text is dropped and the numbers show alone; cached by `snapshot id`. Gated by the AI plan guard and the store AI switch; cost <= 1 call per store per week (`AccountingAiFeature` `'sales-forecast-narration'`). The number never comes from the model.

## 5. Accuracy and measurement

- Seeded template data proves the code does not regress versus the old method and that selection works; it cannot prove accuracy on real merchants (synthetic seasonality is the seed's own). Real accuracy is measured per store from `backtest_wape` on every snapshot (stored, visible to the merchant, monitored by a platform-wide weekly rollup of median WAPE) and from a forward check: every snapshot is later scored against realised sales (`forecast_scores` computed by the same job when the horizon has passed; no extra table needed if the snapshot row gets `realised_30d` and `realised_at` filled in).
- Targets: audit targets are weekly-revenue WAPE < 25% on stores with >= 26 weeks, and P10-P90 coverage >= 80% on hold-out. No Ebda store currently has 26 weeks of real history (Ebda is pre-launch; the QA store is test data), so these are `target`, not `measured`, until launch + 26 weeks; before then the claim in docs and UI is "backtest on your own history" only.
- Gate in code: a store-level forecast is displayed greyed (not hidden) when its own 30-day backtest WAPE > 0.45; SKU rows carry the confidence chip; nothing is hidden for a SKU, because a low-confidence stock-out date is still better than the old velocity, but the chip says so.
