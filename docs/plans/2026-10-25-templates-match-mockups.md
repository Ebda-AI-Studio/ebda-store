# Make the real templates look like the Python mock-ups (2026-10-25)

Status: PLAN ONLY. Nothing here is implemented. Ahmad prefers the look of the Python mock-ups (archived at `business/ebda/archive/home-phone-mockups-2026-10-23/`, generators `apps/marketing/scripts/looks/*.py` on main) over the real storefronts, so the fix is to move the real templates toward the mock-ups, not the other way round (MKT-PHONES-1 was reverted).

## Summary for Ahmad / ملخص لأحمد

**English: the 5 biggest reasons the mock-ups look better**

1. **They have a designed brand lockup; the real stores have a plain store name.** Every mock header is a 36-72 px display wordmark with a letter-spaced Latin line under it (`قمر` over `QAMAR .34em`, `دهب` over `DAHAB`, Aref Ruqaa 72 px for Vitrine), centred, on the look's own ground. The real header is the store name in ~22 px body type plus an "En" button, because the demo stores have no logo.
2. **The hero is a tall, editorial picture; the real one is a cropped strip.** Mock heroes are 3/4 to 4/5 portraits (486 px for Atelier, 450 px for Worn, a 4/4.6 framed window for Vitrine). The real hero is capped at `max-h-[45vh]` and shows 293 px of landscape crop (the subject is cut off).
3. **The mock has no chrome; the real store stacks five bars before the content.** Real: 41 px "illustrative photos" strip + 68 px header + 76 px permanent bottom tab bar = 185 px (22% of an 844 px phone) that the mock does not have. Heart buttons, "new" badges, colour dots and tag chips sit on every card on top of that.
4. **Typography has hierarchy in the mock and is flat in the real one.** Mock: headings 26-34 px **bold 700** in the display face, product names 13 px/500, prices 14 px/700 in the accent colour. Real: headings 24 px at **weight 400** (thin display faces), names 16 px/600, prices 16 px/500. Everything is the same size, so nothing leads.
5. **The mock is editorial and dense in the right places; the real one is generic and airy everywhere.** Mock Atelier shows the product grid with a 3 px gutter and square photos; Worn and Gift box use the same 12-22 px rhythm in every section. Real sections use 32-40 px top padding, 12 px gutters and eight to twelve sections per page, so the signature moment of each look (collage, framed window, journey strip, cook portrait) is replaced by a stock layout.

**العربي: أهم ٥ أسباب بتخلّي الموك-أب أحلى**

١. **الموك-أب فيه لوجو متصمّم، والمتاجر الحقيقية فيها اسم المتجر بس.** كل هيدر في الموك-أب كلمة كبيرة (٣٦ لـ٧٢ بكسل) بخط العرض، وتحتها سطر لاتيني متباعد (`قمر` فوق `QAMAR`)، في النص وعلى أرضية الشكل. الهيدر الحقيقي اسم المتجر بخط صغير (~٢٢ بكسل) وزرار «En»، لأن المتاجر التجريبية معندهاش لوجو.
٢. **صورة الهيرو في الموك-أب طويلة ومقصوصة بذكاء، وفي الحقيقي شريط قصير.** الموك-أب ٣/٤ لـ٤/٥ (٤٨٦ بكسل في أتيليه). الحقيقي محدود بـ`max-h-[45vh]` فبيطلع ٢٩٣ بكسل وبيقطع الموديل.
٣. **الموك-أب من غير شريط ولا قوائم زيادة، والحقيقي فيه ٥ طبقات قبل المحتوى.** شريط «الصور توضيحية» (٤١) + الهيدر (٦٨) + شريط التبويبات السفلي الدايم (٧٦) = ١٨٥ بكسل، يعني ٢٢٪ من الشاشة، وفوقهم قلب وشارة «جديد» ونقط ألوان وتاجات على كل كارت.
٤. **الخطوط في الموك-أب ليها تدرّج، وفي الحقيقي كلها بنفس الوزن.** الموك-أب: العناوين ٢٦-٣٤ بكسل **سميكة ٧٠٠**، اسم المنتج ١٣/٥٠٠، السعر ١٤/٧٠٠ بلون التمييز. الحقيقي: العناوين ٢٤ بوزن **٤٠٠** (رفيع)، الاسم ١٦/٦٠٠، السعر ١٦/٥٠٠، فمفيش حاجة بتقود العين.
٥. **الموك-أب عنده لحظة مميّزة لكل شكل، والحقيقي تخطيط عادي.** الكولاج، الفاترينة المؤطّرة، رحلة القطعة، صورة الطبّاخة، شريط السلة. الحقيقي بيستبدلها بأقسام جاهزة بمسافات ٣٢-٤٠ بكسل وفجوات ٣ بكسل بتتحول لـ١٢.

## Method and evidence

- **Mock values**: read from `apps/marketing/scripts/looks/{qamar,souq,jasmine,lamma,dahab}.py`, `catalog.py`, `lib.py` (BASE css, `footer()`), five extraction passes; file:line quoted where the CSS states a number. A phone is 360 px wide; heights marked (calc) are my arithmetic from `aspect-ratio`. The mock's engine CSS (`phone-store.css`) only adds the cart badge, filter bar and overlay layers.
- **Real values**: measured on the live demo stores (`storedemo`, `-souq`, `-jasmine`, `-lamma`, `-dahab`, `?look=<tpl>.<look>`) at **390 x 844**, Arabic, reduced motion, with the demo look bar hidden (computed styles and boxes; raw JSON kept in the session scratchpad). Theme tokens are read from the page's `<html style>`.
- **Side-by-side images**: all 15 old | new pairs were rendered (scratchpad `pack/`); 9 were looked at directly (qamar A, C; souq C; jasmine A, B; lamma A, C; dahab A, B). Findings for the other 6 looks (qamar B, souq A, B, jasmine C, lamma B, dahab C) come from the CSS extraction and the measurements only and are marked **(not viewed)**.
- The real stores were captured with the old bare-brand names ("إبدا ديمو"); they are now named after their template (BRAND-NAME-1). The header gap below is unchanged by that.
- impeccable lens: the critique axes used are hierarchy, rhythm, chrome/noise, focal point and Arabic typography. No impeccable score was run (the skill scans a running page for anti-patterns, and the question here is a match against a reference, not a rule violation).

## Fix plan (ranked by impact per effort; each is a small PR)

Verification for every PR: a **side-by-side harness** (old mock fragment from the archive vs the live demo at 390, per look, two scroll depths; it existed as a throwaway script and is trivially rebuilt), `impeccable` critique on the changed looks at 390/1280 in ar/en, axe, the existing Playwright flow specs. Nothing merges without Ahmad approving the pair images for that template.

1. **P1 Lockup logos (G1).** Generate five lockup logos (display wordmark + letter-spaced Latin line, same fonts as the mock, SVG), upload through the seed (`logo`, `logoAr`), set `wordmarkSize` per look. Biggest single change, touches only seed + header sizing. Needs Ahmad's call on whether the lockups are demo assets or become a template feature (merchants without a logo get the lockup from their store name).
2. **P2 Hero ratio token (G2).** `heroMobileAspect` look token (3/4, 4/5, 1/1.05 ...) replacing `max-h-[45vh]` for looks that set it; `HeroBanner.tsx`, `HeroSlider.tsx`, `looks/{types,registry}.ts`, plus photo crops (`object-position`) per look.
3. **P3 Chrome diet (G3, G4).** Strip: move the demo notice out of the announcement bar (footer line + DemoLookBar). Tab bar: decision for Ahmad: keep for real stores but hide on demo home, or slim to 56 px. Looks with `cartBar: 'total'` already replace it.
4. **P4 Type scale and weight (G5).** Per-look `typeScale` token: heading size/weight, card name size/weight, price weight/colour (accent). Display faces that are single-weight (Lalezar, Kufam, Reem Kufi, Markazi) stay at 400; the others go to 700. Files: `SectionHeading.tsx`, `SectionHeader.tsx`, `card-size.ts`, `StorefrontProductCard.tsx`.
5. **P5 Card chrome token (G6).** `cardChrome: 'bare'` hides heart, badges, dots and tags on home grids (keep on PLP/PDP), and shows the mock's quick-add where the look had it.
6. **P6 Rhythm (G7).** `sectionPadding` / `gridGap` tokens per look from the table above (Atelier 3 px gutters, 22 px row gap; others 10-12 / 16-22).
7. **P7 Signature moments**, one PR per look: Qamar B collage, C framed hero + testimonial; Souq C full-bleed spotlight; Jasmine A split hero + list rows, C hero band; Lamma A pill-on-photo hero, C coloured tiles + delivery row; Dahab B coral CTA / A gold accent.
8. **P8 Palette corrections (G8).** Accent hex fixes in the registry (Dahab x3, Lamma A, Jasmine A/C) after Ahmad picks mock or current per look.

Order of work: P1, P2, P3 on all five templates first (they explain most of the gap and are token-driven), then P4-P6, then P7 per look, re-comparing after each PR.
