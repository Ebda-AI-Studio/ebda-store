**Architecture:** Same shape as B2: Lamma is ONE skin id (`lamma`) that owns chrome only (`[data-pack='lamma']` CSS + `PACK_STYLES.lamma` + a thin header wrapper), Ruling B1-1 / B2-1 repeated. Prep time, portion sizes, serves-N, notes ("بدون بصل"), delivery windows and off-days already exist from B0 (lead time, facts, personalisation note, `checkoutDelivery`, `deliveryOffDays`) and are reused, not rebuilt. Deliberately deferred: multi-select add-ons with price delta, sold-by-weight steppers, gift message/cards, per-day capacity (Ruling B4-5). **No Prisma schema change beyond the enum value.**

**Tech Stack:** unchanged — Next.js 16 (apps/web, apps/admin, apps/marketing), Express on Cloudflare Workers (apps/api), Prisma + Neon (strict RLS + tenant guard), Zod in `@ebda/shared`, Vitest, Playwright + axe-core, R2 `ebda-media` (`https://media.ebda.app`), FLUX.1 schnell on Workers AI (offline), ffmpeg.

## Global Constraints

- **Arabic-first**: every surface designed and shot in Arabic (RTL) first, then English. Every storefront string lives in `apps/web/src/contexts/LanguageContext.tsx` (EN + AR); no hardcoded copy in a skin. **Egyptian Arabic for full sentences** ("اطلب زي المنيو", "هتوصلك النهاردة الساعة ٧", "ده متجر تجريبي", "أضف ٨٠ ج.م وتوصل للحد الأدنى"), **neutral noun/infinitive forms for buttons and labels** ("إضافة للسلة", "اطلب تاني" is a section heading, the button stays "إضافة"). English is sentence case. Western digits by default (NUMERALS-1); the matrix also runs Arabic-Indic. One price format everywhere through the shared formatter.
- **Ebda has no orange** (Ahmad 2026-10-16, extended to palettes and photos by the Souq precedent, B2-2). Forbidden: hue 12°–50° at HSL saturation ≥ 35% in any Lamma look token, any UI colour a look paints, and any generated photo (food-calibrated probe, Ruling B4-3). **Metal is allowed** (taste profile: gold/brass/silver are good): a token declared as `metal` may sit at hue 36°–50° only if HSL saturation ≤ 52% and lightness 45–75%, and is used as text/fill only on the grounds where its contrast is measured (Ruling B4-2). If B2 already shipped a shared metal exemption, reuse it; otherwise Task 1 extracts one helper both gates import (a gate that cannot fail is not a gate: negative controls for saffron `#E0A526` and terracotta `#B5482E` are mandatory).
- **Taste bar** (B0-4 as amended by B0-49, binding): card = at most 5 swatch dots (then `+N`) and at most 2 fact chips below the price, nothing else; no countdowns, no HOT/FEATURED badges, no stock nags, no second CTA; one accent for the primary action; unfilled features render nothing; at most one helper line per control; no gradient text, side-stripe borders, glassmorphism, ghost cards; radius from `var(--radius)` only; PDP fold at 390 = gallery, title, price, first selector, add-to-cart via the sticky bar. ## Review Focus

1. **Orange never creeps in — and food is the hard case.** Tokens, `cardBadge`/sale/OOS chips, focus rings, aisle tints and every generated photo have no forbidden-band pixel/token. Bread, tagines and kunafa are brown/gold by nature, so the probe is calibrated for saturated orange, not brown (Ruling B4-3), and the catalogue avoids orange-coloured subjects (no oranges, carrots, mango, pumpkin, orange sauces as a hero subject). Pinned by Task 1's token gate (with negative controls) and Task 6's image probe; re-checked by Audit-2 on rendered shots.
2. **Notebook on real merchant content.** A dark green look with a merchant's white-background dish photos must not show white rectangles on green (§ 9 rule 1): the dish grid sits on a light band or matte. Pinned by a rendered-band contrast test and a `lamma-white-photos` shot.
3. **Checkout data flow, not component level** (B1's worst miss). The date-chip choice, the dish note and the portion variant must reach the order summary AND the receipt AND the admin notes line through the real cart → checkout → receipt path; a unit test that feeds `OrderSummary` directly does not count. A scenario store places a COD order with a portion-variant dish + note + a picked date and the test asserts all three at the receipt.
4. **Targets ≥ 44px on the rendered DOM**: stepper − / +, tab chips, date chips, reorder add, the cart-bar button. New controls are added to `files` in `purchase-path.json` the moment they exist.
5. **Stepper honesty.** The card stepper appears only for a non-variant, non-required-personalisation, orderable product; otherwise the one CTA routes to the PDP (the existing card-CTA rule). Quantity respects the product max and the cart cap; − at 1 removes the line (with undo, as the cart does).
6. **Date-chip truth.** A chip is offered only if the server would accept it: earliest date = store delivery estimate + max `leadTimeDays` in the cart, business days, skipping `deliveryOffDays`, store timezone, today's windows obey `orderBy`; a stale or hand-edited date is refused with a clear message. A store without `daysAhead` behaves byte-identically to today.
7. **One bottom layer.** The browse-page cart bar REPLACES the bottom tab bar while it shows (C-04, `data-task-bar`); never stacks with the PDP buy bar or the cart total bar; the floating widget lifts correctly (`--sticky-bar-h`).
8. **Look switch keeps content** (TPL-1): Table ↔ Notebook ↔ Market keeps hero text, product refs, maker profile, category photos and logo; only design keys and the look's section mix change. The `reorder`/`on_sale` rows and `maker-profile` survive a switch away and back.
9. **Demo isolation and its limit.** `storedemo-lamma` refuses orders and PII by the same `is_demo` guards (no slug-keyed branch); customer registration is refused on a demo store, so the **reorder row is never visible on the showroom** (shown only in the non-demo scenario store and the matrix) — stated in `demo-store.md`, Ruling B4-9.

- **Ruling B4-3 — food-calibrated orange probe.** Pixel counts as "orange" when hue 12°–45°, HSV saturation ≥ 0.65 and value ≥ 0.60; limit ≤ 4% for product/category photos, ≤ 6% for heroes, page-wide shots ≤ 6%. Browns, crust and caramel (lower saturation or value) pass; a glowing orange (lanterns, oranges, carrots) fails. The Souq probe (strict) is not reused as-is because it would reject any bread. A negative-control fixture must fail. Cost if wrong: a golden-orange dish passes the probe — mitigated by viewing every image and by the Audit-2 rendered-shot check.
- **Ruling B4-4 — the card stepper is a state of the one CTA.** For a non-variant, orderable, non-required-personalisation product already in the cart (`quantityInCart` exists on `ProductCardCta`) the CTA becomes − qty + (≥ 44px each); a first add still uses the one add control. Variant/required-note products keep routing to the PDP. Cost if wrong: a stepper on a card that then needs a choice; mitigated by the gating predicate shared with `canBuyNow`.
- **Ruling B4-5 — deferred from research, each with a reason.** Multi-select add-ons with price delta (changes cart-line identity, order items, invoice, KDS: the B0 personalisation note covers "no onions"), sold-by-weight steppers with 0.5 steps (quantity is an integer end to end; weight is sold as variants "500 جم / 1 كجم" with the unit price shown as a fact), gift message/send date/gift cards (separate order-level data), per-day capacity and slot quotas (needs a counter table; windows + `orderBy` cut-off exist), free-shipping progress (only minimum-order progress ships), a home-page pre-order calendar strip (the dates live at checkout), Notebook's "one-line story" on dish tiles (B0-4 limits a card to price + 2 chips: the story lives on the PDP). Cost if wrong: the Notebook tile is quieter than the direction sketch; Market cannot sell by weight in half-kilos.
