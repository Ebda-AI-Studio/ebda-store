## Taste Rules (from `AHMAD-TASTE-PROFILE.md`, cited by every look brief and every audit)

1. **No orange, saffron or amber anywhere.** Warmth comes from metal (brass, gold, moon silver), never orange.
2. **Deep rich grounds + a light/metallic accent** are premium; flat SaaS is not. (Apothecary is the deep-ground look; Bloom and Clinic are light, so their richness comes from a committed colour and photography, not grey.)
3. **Premium base + playful details**: each look has a fun detail that survives `prefers-reduced-motion` as a static state (Apothecary: the cap "unscrews" and a drop falls on add-to-cart; Bloom: shade dot pops and the page wash shifts a tint on pick; Clinic: a step ticks when its product is in the cart).
4. **Merchandising that sells beats decoration**: skin-type chips, a routine that builds a bundle, rating, bundle saving, a deals row. No countdowns (B0-4).
5. **Egyptian Arabic for full sentences** ("بشرتك دهنية ولا جافة؟ اختار وإحنا نرشّحلك", "ابدأ بروتين من 3 خطوات", "ده متجر تجريبي"); neutral short forms for buttons ("إضافة للسلة", "تسوّق", "بحث").
6. **Photo-led**; text never covers the product on phones (hero anchor + scrim ≤ 40% of the photo, quality-bar § 9).

## Global Constraints

- **Arabic-first**: shot and designed in Arabic RTL first, then English; every string in `LanguageContext.tsx` (ar + en); Western digits by default, matrix also runs Arabic-Indic; one price format via the shared formatter.
- **No orange, in four places, each with a gate that can fail:** (1) tokens: hue 12°–50° at saturation ≥ 35% forbidden on every token and badge colour (B2's gate, negative control `#E68245` and `#C9822B`); (2) **shade swatches**: every demo `swatchHex` passes the same hue test (a "nude" or "peach" lipstick is a rosy-beige at saturation < 35% or a rose/berry/mauve, never a coral or terracotta); (3) generated photos: pixel probe (below); (4) rendered shots: page-wide probe in Task 6.
- **Taste bar B0-4 (binding)**: card = at most 5 swatch dots then `+N`, at most 2 fact chips below the price, nothing else; no countdowns, no HOT/FEATURED badges, no stock nags, no second CTA; one accent for the primary action; unfilled features render nothing; radius from `var(--radius)` only; PDP fold at 390 = gallery, title, price, first selector, add-to-cart via the sticky bar (the rating line and the bundle line must not push it).
- **Quality bar § 1–10 apply.** § 9: Apothecary (dark, committed) puts merchant white-background photos on a light band or matte; over-photo controls take their on-token; a `line` token below 3:1 never draws an interactive boundary; one corner allocator per card. § 10: the whole purchase path shot at 390-ar + 1280-en; ≥ 44px targets measured on the rendered DOM; no unthemed native controls; no colour outside the token set; autoplay pause; titles in the page language; one display face per look.
- **Card paths rule** (CLAUDE.md): the 8 card paths + Dahab + the 4 hand-built mappers. B3 adds NO card path (Ruling B3-1) and **no rating on cards** (Ruling B3-6).
- **Looks are data over the shared section registry** (LOOKS-1). Legacy stores (`look = null`) and every non-Jasmine look stay byte-identical; B1/B2 characterization tests stay green unchanged.
- **Must not change**: checkout, pricing, inventory, permissions, tenant isolation, existing published pages, Qamar/Souq/Dahab looks, the B0/B1/B2 shared fixes.
- **Staleness classes**: the PDP rating line reads `Review` aggregates and the bundle-saving line reads bundle prices. Rating is **BOUNDED-stale** (rides the product's tagged public cache, ≤ 5 min; invalidated by the existing review-moderation and `invalidateStoreProductCaches` paths; class + window commented at the resolver; a test pins that an approved review reaches it). ## Review Focus

1. **Orange never creeps in.** Tokens, swatches, sale/OOS chips, focus rings, photos and marketing captures. The two real risks are _amber glass_ (Apothecary's original scene) and _skin tones / coral lipsticks_ (Bloom): both are kept out of prompts and catalogue, and Task 5's probe has a negative control and a documented skin-tone allowance (see Ruling B3-3).
2. **Apothecary on real merchant content**: a dark look with white-background bottle shots must not show white rectangles on olive; product grids sit on a light band or matte (rendered-band contrast test + `jasmine-white-photos` shot).
3. **Checkout data flow, not component level** (B1's worst miss): every Jasmine scenario store places a COD order through the real cart → checkout → receipt path with a **shade-swatch × size** variant product and asserts the variant names reach the summary and the receipt.
4. **Targets ≥ 44px on the rendered DOM**: concern chips, shade swatches, steps cards, routine "add all", rating link, rail tiles. Every new control's file joins `purchase-path.json` `files` the moment it exists.
5. **Skin-type chips do real work**: tapping a chip lands on a PLP filtered by that tag and shows the count; an empty tag renders no chip (unfilled features render nothing); no chip is a dead end.
6. **Routine honesty**: "روتينك في 3 خطوات" is a REAL sequence (cleanse → treat → protect) backed by a bundle/three products; numbering appears only where order matters; "add all" adds the real products with a real saving, via the existing cart path.
7. **Look switch keeps content** (TPL-1) across Jasmine looks and across templates (Qamar/Souq ↔ Jasmine); only design keys and the look's section mix change.
8. **Demo isolation**: `storedemo-jasmine` refuses orders and PII writes by the same `is_demo` guards (no slug-keyed branch); the bar's template switch lands on the right host with the look kept; catalogues never cross between demo stores.
9. **First screen shows categories or the skin-type chips** in all three looks at 390×844 and 1280×800 (shared bar), even for Apothecary's ingredient hero (hero capped at 78svh).

## Rulings made by this plan

Appended to `docs/RULINGS.md` under `## 2026-10-21 — Phase B3 Jasmine (B3-*)`, each by the implementer in the task that makes it true; Task 6 only checks the set.

- **Ruling B3-1 — Jasmine is one skin, `jasmine`, owning chrome only; cards, PDP, cart, checkout stay shared.** Same evidence as B1-1 / B2-1: every card need (rounded photo, shade dots, 2 fact chips) is expressible by tokens + pack styles. Cost if wrong: a card shape the pack cannot express needs a card-level variant across all card paths.
- **Ruling B3-2 — Apothecary's amber is re-derived to brass; the scene changes from amber glass to jade, clear and cobalt glass with brass caps.** Original: ground `#2E2A22`, text `#F3EFE6`, accent amber `#C9822B` (hue 33°, saturation 65%, inside the forbidden band). New: ground and text kept (ground saturation 15%, text ~25%, both outside the band); **accent = the same brass `#C89B3C` Ahmad chose for Night souq (Ruling B2-18)**, used for primary CTA fill/rules only, text on it `#1E1B14`-class ink (ratio measured in Task 1, ≥ 4.5); the scoped allowlist from B2-18 gains exactly one more entry (`jasmine.apothecary`, same hex, same roles); every other token stays inside the gate unexcepted; real oranges stay banned (negative controls `#C9822B`, `#E68245`). If the allowlist is not on main, the fallback is a bone-brass `#B8A878` (saturation 29%, passes unexcepted). Cost if wrong: one token edit and a re-shoot.
- **Ruling B3-3 — Bloom's and Clinic's colours were already outside the band; two are tightened, and photos avoid skin-close-ups.** Bloom: body `#F6D7DC` (hue 352°), ink `#3A1E2A`, raspberry `#C2185B` (hue 336°): fine as a fill with white text; as _text on blush_ it measures ≈ 4.3:1, so the text role darkens to the AA value Task 1 derives (≈ `#B0124F`), fill unchanged. Clinic: white, ink `#13212B`, aqua band `#E6F5F2` kept; mint `#2FA88F` is ≈ 3:1 on white so it stays as fills/rules and a darker `#1E7F6B` (derived, ≥ 4.5) carries text and the primary button. Generated photos: product-only and still-life compositions; no face or skin close-ups (skin tones sit at hue ~20° and would trip the pixel probe for the wrong reason, and a stock face is a licensing/likeness question). Probe thresholds are the B2 ones (≤ 3%, heroes ≤ 5%). Cost if wrong: a probe false positive forces a re-prompt, not a threshold change.
- **Ruling B3-4 — ingredients, actives, PAO/expiry, skin type and how-to-use are B0 product facts by convention, not columns.** `onCard` facts: Apothecary shows the key ingredient ("زيت الجوجوبا"), Clinic shows the active ("نياسيناميد 10%"), Bloom shows size ("50 مل"). Cost if wrong: no structured ingredient filter or expiry sorting; a typed column can come later without touching the looks.
- **Ruling B3-5 — skin-type and concern chips are a source over store Tags (existing Tag/ProductTag + PLP tag filter), not a new model.** Cost if wrong: a merchant who tags inconsistently gets uneven chips; a typed facet can replace the source later.
- **Ruling B3-6 — star rating shows on the PDP only, not on cards.** B0-4 limits a card to dots + two fact chips; a rating line is a third element on 13 card paths and the research's "per-card stars" belong to the loud retailers (Nice One) the bar rejects. Cost if wrong: Jasmine cards lack the social proof Gulf beauty shoppers scan for; revisit after launch data.
- **Ruling B3-7 — deferred from research, each with a reason.** Subscribe & save / refill reminders (Phase C billing), BNPL line (B2's instalment line already covers it, merchant-configured), gift cards (new model, not B3), review photos (R2 upload + moderation, a feature of its own), flash-deal countdown (B0-4), sample/minis product type, authenticity badge as a feature (the `trust-badges` section already carries "100% أصلي"). Cost if wrong: Jasmine lacks Glossier's subscribe-and-save and review photo strip; both are additive later.
- **Ruling B3-8 — one demo store per template (B2-7 extended).** `storedemo-jasmine`, owner `storedemo-jasmine-owner@ebda.app` (no mailbox, `passwordHash '!seed-no-login'`), R2 prefix `demo/jasmine/`, host in the single `demo-hosts.ts` map. Cost if wrong: one more seed per template.
- **Ruling B3-9 — assets are unbranded.** No logos, lettering or readable labels on bottles (the "ZAS" class: FLUX invents text); packaging is plain matte/glass with blank labels; every file viewed before commit. Cost if wrong: regeneration.
- **Ruling B3-10 — no Ahmad STOPs; the independent audits are the gate (decision 2026-10-21).** Cost if wrong: a taste miss Ahmad would have caught ships; mitigated by the ≥ 28/40, 0 P0/P1 bar and the taste profile cited in both audit briefs.
