# Phase B2 — Souq (general retail & electronics) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax. **LEAN MODE is binding** (contract below); it overrides the B1 plan's heavier loop wherever they differ.

**Goal:** Ship Souq's three looks (Catalogue / Night souq / Showroom) as real storefronts on a new `souq` skin, flip them to `built`, seed a realistic Egyptian electronics-and-appliances catalogue on a second demo store (`storedemo-souq`) reachable from the existing demo bar, and replace the Souq illustrations on ebda.app with captures of that real storefront (Ruling TPL-4). No orange, saffron or amber anywhere in Souq's palettes, photos or UI.

**Architecture:** B1 already built the machinery; B2 adds data and four small platform variants, not new machinery. Souq is ONE new skin id (`souq`) that owns chrome only (a search-first header wrapper + `[data-pack='souq']` CSS + `PACK_STYLES.souq`), exactly the Ruling B1-1 pattern: cards, PDP, cart and checkout stay on the shared components, so B2 adds NO card render path. Character comes from look data (tokens in `packages/shared/src/looks/registry.ts`, a per-look section mix via `LookDefinition.sections`, `lookTag` heroes) plus four platform section variants every skin renders: `category-grid` layouts `rail` and `tiles`, `product-spotlight` layout `stage`, and a `productSource: 'on_sale'`. One platform commerce feature is new: a merchant-configured instalment line on the PDP. Specs, warranty and condition reuse B0's product facts (no schema). The demo grows by one store (per-template demo stores, Ruling B2-7), not by a catalogue-scoping mechanism.

**Tech Stack:** unchanged from B1 — Next.js 16 (apps/web, apps/admin, apps/marketing), Express on Cloudflare Workers (apps/api), Prisma + Neon (strict RLS + tenant guard), Zod in `@ebda/shared`, Vitest, Playwright + axe-core, R2 `ebda-media` (`https://media.ebda.app`), FLUX.1 schnell on Workers AI (offline image generation), ffmpeg.

**Spec:** `docs/plans/done/2026-10-14-templates-and-editor-program.md` (Phase B, Phase S token/registry contract, scenario matrix, review loop, Rulings TPL-1..4). Read with it: `docs/ebda/templates/2026-10-14-directions.md` (Souq A/B/C), `docs/ebda/templates/research/souq.md`, `docs/ebda/templates/looks.md`, `docs/ebda/templates/quality-bar.md` (**§ 9 and § 10 are binding** — they land with B1), `docs/ebda/templates/platform-commerce.md` (B0 taste bar B0-4 as amended by B0-49), `docs/ebda/templates/demo-store.md`, `docs/plans/done/2026-10-15-phase-b1-qamar.md` (structure + Rulings B1-1..B1-23 + amendments C1–C31) and its ledger `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-15-phase-b1-qamar/progress.md` (Rulings B1-24..B1-110: what went wrong).

## Global Constraints

- **Arabic-first**: every surface designed and shot in Arabic (RTL) first, then English. Every storefront string lives in `apps/web/src/contexts/LanguageContext.tsx` (EN + AR); no hardcoded copy in a skin. **Egyptian Arabic for full sentences** (headlines, helper lines, empty states, trust lines: "لقيت اللي عايزه في ضغطتين", "ده متجر تجريبي"), **neutral noun/infinitive forms for buttons and labels** ("إضافة للسلة", "مقارنة", "تسوّق", "بحث"), per Ahmad's decision (e) of 2026-10-15. English is sentence case. Western digits by default (NUMERALS-1); the matrix also runs with Arabic-Indic on. One price format everywhere through the shared formatter.
- **Ebda has no orange** (Ahmad 2026-10-16, extended to palettes: **no saffron, no amber, no brass/gold in the orange family**). Hue band 12°–50° at saturation ≥ 35% is forbidden in Souq look tokens, in the UI the looks paint, and in every generated photo (Ruling B2-2, gate in Task 1 and Task 6).
- **Taste bar** (B0-4 as amended by B0-49, binding): card = at most 5 swatch dots (then `+N`) and at most 2 fact chips below the price, nothing else; **no countdowns**, no HOT/FEATURED badges, no stock nags, no second CTA; one accent for the primary action; unfilled features render nothing; at most one helper line per control; no gradient text, side-stripe borders, glassmorphism, ghost cards; radius from `var(--radius)` only; PDP fold at 390 = gallery, title, price, first selector, add-to-cart via the sticky bar.
- **Quality bar § 1–10 apply**, in particular § 9 (a dark/committed look puts merchant white-background photos on a light band or matte; over-photo controls take their on-token; a decorative `line` token below 3:1 never draws an interactive boundary; one corner allocator per card) and § 10 (the purchase path is shot in full at 390-ar + 1280-en; ≥ 44px targets measured on the rendered DOM; no unthemed native controls; no colours outside the token set on the purchase path; autoplay pause; titles in the page language; one display face per look).
- **Card paths rule** (CLAUDE.md): the 8 card paths + Dahab + the 4 hand-built mappers. B2 adds NO card render path (Ruling B2-1). `stage` spotlight items and the instalment line read the shared price / compare-at / sold-out helpers and `canBuyNow`; a reviewer rejects a task that adds a path or hard-codes `inStock: true`.
- **Looks are data over the shared section registry**, never forked commerce code (LOOKS-1). Legacy stores (`look = null`) and every non-Souq look stay byte-identical; Phase S/B1 characterization tests stay green unchanged.
- **Must not change**: checkout, pricing, inventory (`products.inventory` stays 0 for variant products), permissions, tenant isolation, existing stores' published pages, Qamar and Dahab looks, the B0/B1 shared fixes.
- **Staleness classes** (cache rule): the `on_sale` deals row reads prices, so it declares **BOUNDED-stale**: it rides the same tagged public product caches as every listing (≤ 5 min; checkout reads the authoritative price), is invalidated by the existing `invalidateStoreProductCaches` on every product write path, and states class + residual window in a comment at the source resolver. A test pins that a price edit reaches it through that invalidation. No new cache layer.
- **Migrations**: one, additive, `ALTER TYPE … ADD VALUE IF NOT EXISTS`, single statement, no `lock_timeout` (B0-50). Instalment settings live in `stores.settings` JSON (no migration).
- **Tenant guard**: every Prisma query carries a top-level `storeId` = the ambient store; scripts use `createPrismaClient({ role: 'owner' })`; `withOwnerTx` / `withRlsBypass` stay out of `apps/api/src`.

## Review Focus

1. **Orange never creeps in.** The three Souq palettes, `cardBadge`/sale/OOS chips, focus rings, generated photos (lanterns are the risk: Vitrine's hero measured 61% orange pixels in B1) and the marketing captures contain no pixel or token in the forbidden hue band. Pinned by Task 1's token gate and Task 6's image probe; re-checked by the STOP-2 audit on rendered shots.
2. **Night souq on real merchant content.** A dark look with merchant white-background cut-outs must not show white rectangles on green: grids sit on a light band or matte (quality-bar § 9 rule 1). Pinned by a rendered-band contrast test and a shot of a store whose photos are white-background.
3. **Checkout data flow, not component level.** B1's worst miss: `CheckoutClient` dropped variant fields before `OrderSummary` while the unit test fed the summary directly. Every Souq scenario store therefore places a COD order through the real cart → checkout → receipt path with a variant product (Colour × Storage) and the test asserts the variant names reach the summary and the receipt; the purchase-path shoot (17 screens × 2 viewports × 3 looks) is the gate, not component tests.
4. **Targets ≥ 44px on the rendered DOM** (cart stepper, remove, coupon clear/retry, chips, the search clear button, compare toggle, spec chips that are links). Pinned by B1's `purchase-path-probe` run over every shot; a Souq-specific control added in this plan (rail tiles, search field, stage compare toggle) is added to `files` in `purchase-path.json` the moment it exists.
5. **Instalment honesty.** The line renders only when the merchant enabled it; it is arithmetic over their own settings, named "تقريبي", never a provider logo or brand, never on cards, never in checkout totals; a price edit or a variant change updates it; with settings absent the PDP is byte-identical.
6. **Look switch keeps content** (TPL-1): Catalogue → Showroom keeps the merchant's hero text, product refs, category photos and logo; only design keys and the look's section mix change; the stage section's product ref survives a switch away and back. Reuses B1's keep/reset tests with Souq mixes.
7. **Demo isolation**: `storedemo-souq` refuses orders and PII writes by the same `is_demo` guards as `storedemo` (no slug-keyed branch), the bar's template switch lands on the right host with the look kept, and the Qamar catalogue never appears on the Souq demo or vice versa.
8. **Showroom's first screen shows categories** (shared bar) although its identity is one hero product per screen: the first stage is capped at 78svh and a `chips` category row (B1 variant) sits under the header. Measured at 390×844 and 1280×800.
