# Finance pages — الأرباح والمصروفات (2026-11-04)

The first finance tab is **Profit and expenses / الأرباح والمصروفات**: money in, money out and profit for the period you pick. The accountant tabs (chart of accounts, journal, reports, bank reconciliation) follow it.

- **Expenses**: the row shows the next step (Approve, or Post once approved). **Reject expense** is in the "…" menu, asks first, and the expense stays out of your accounts. A failed action shows a message instead of disappearing.
- **Bank statements**: **Import statement** is the header button. **Delete statement** is in each row's "…" menu and asks first; it removes the statement and every matched transaction.
- **New expense / New journal entry**: **Save expense** / **Post entry** sit in the footer bar with **Cancel** on the other side.
- **Currency rates** (multi-currency): on plans without the feature you see the standard locked-plan card, not a free-text notice.
- **Analytics → cash flow and stock value** are for the store owner on plans that include accounting. Other viewers see a locked card instead of an error.

الصفحة الأولى في المالية هي **الأرباح والمصروفات**: اللي دخل واللي خرج والربح في الفترة اللي تختارها، وبعدها تبويبات المحاسب (دليل الحسابات، اليومية، التقارير، مطابقة البنك). «ارفض المصروف» و«امسح الكشف» في قايمة «…» وبيسألوك الأول.
