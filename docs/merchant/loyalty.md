# Loyalty points

Open **Loyalty** in your store. Turn the program on, set how many points a customer earns per pound spent, the tiers, and how points turn into a discount at checkout.

- **Members** lists every customer who has points, with their tier and balance. It always matches what customers earned from orders and what you awarded yourself.
- **Give points by hand**: open a member and choose _Award points_. Enter a whole number (for example 50). A negative number takes points off. The points can be spent at the next order straight away.
- **Transactions** shows every movement of points across the store, newest first. Filter by type or by customer.
- Staff who place orders for customers cannot change the program; only the store owner can.
