1 · Pricing

Shopify charges Egyptian merchants twice

Shopify Payments does not work in Egypt, so every Egyptian store pays for a local gateway such as Paymob and a Shopify surcharge of 2% on Basic. The subscription is billed in US dollars, which adds about 3% in bank FX charges on an Egyptian card.

What the platform takes each month

Example store: 150 orders a month at EGP 1,000 (EGP 150,000 in sales), paid by card. Each bar is the subscription plus any platform commission. The card gateway fee (about EGP 4,575 on Paymob at 2.75% + EGP 3) is the same on every platform, so it is left out.

Rate used: 1 USD = EGP 51.92 (25 Sep 2026). Shopify Basic = EGP 986 subscription + EGP 3,000 surcharge. YouCan = $29 plan + 1% online commission. Wuilt Business = EGP 10,500 a year. EasyOrders is striped because its pricing page returned 404 and aggregator figures conflict. ExpandCart's EGP prices could not be verified. Salla and Zid are Gulf-first, and their Egypt gateway support is unverified.

Growth is EGP 1,500 a month, about Shopify Basic's $27 sticker price, but with no 2% surcharge, no dollar card and no FX risk. That still leaves the merchant about EGP 2,500 a month better off than Shopify at 150 orders. 0% commission on every plan; gateway fees passed through at cost.

2 · Moving stores off Shopify

No Shopify MCP can export a store. The Admin API can.

Shopify's MCP servers (Dev, Storefront/UCP catalog, Customer Accounts, Checkout) are built for AI shopping agents and coding help. None of them reads a merchant's whole store. The dependable route is the Admin GraphQL API with bulk operations. They export each part of the store as a file in the background, up to 5 at once per store since API 2026-01.

Two hard facts to plan around

Store owners can no longer create custom apps in the admin (since 1 Jan 2026). So the importer has to be our own public Shopify app, which the merchant approves with one click through OAuth.

Customer names, emails and addresses need Shopify's Protected Customer Data approval. Review takes 2–4 weeks. Without it those fields come back empty. Apply early.

Day-one fallback, with no approval needed

The Shopify admin already exports products, customers and orders as CSV. The Pro plan can accept those files on day one while the public app is in review. The public /products.json feed can power a "preview your store here" teaser, but only with the owner's consent. Never migrate a store without it.

  1. Owner connects their store

    "Import from Shopify" opens Shopify's own approval screen. We read nothing until the owner approves.

  2. Export runs in the background

    One bulk export per group of data (Shopify allows up to 5 linked lists and 2 levels of nesting per query). Download links expire after 7 days, so we copy the files straight away.

  3. Theme is translated

    We read the theme's colours, fonts and logo and apply them to one of our themes. Shopify's Liquid template code is kept for reference, never converted automatically.

  4. Owner reviews anything that didn't fit

    Products with too many variants, or data owned by apps, are listed for review instead of being dropped quietly.

  5. Honest report, then cut-over

    A "couldn't be moved" list covers saved cards, original gift card codes and app data. Password-reset emails go to customers. The owner moves the domain themselves when they're ready.

3 · Name, colour, art

The same night as mypovonai and Whisky 11, with one light of its own

Both of your brands are dark-first and nearly monochrome: mypovonai lights its page with a white WebGPU orb, and Whisky 11 allows exactly one cyan call to action per screen. This product keeps that restraint and adds a single signature glow: Antique Gold, the same #B08D57 as Amelia Aurum. It's also the true colour of the Nile at night from orbit, where the city lights burn gold.

The rule carried over from Whisky 11: the glow appears once per screen (the main button, or a store "lighting up"). Everything else stays in white light on the night ground. The platform's colours never appear on a merchant's storefront. Amelia Aurum keeps her wine, cream and gold.

The art: the Nile at night, seen from orbit

From space at night, Egypt is total darkness crossed by one bright ribbon: the Nile valley, fanning out into the Delta. That is the real map of where Egyptians live and trade. In the hero above, each point of light is a store, and gold points are stores opening right now. On the live site this becomes a WebGPU light field in the style of mypovonai's orb, fed by real store counts. No pyramids, no hieroglyphs, and no one else has this image.
