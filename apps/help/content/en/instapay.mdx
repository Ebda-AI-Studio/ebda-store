InstaPay has no merchant API, so Ebda cannot see a transfer on its own. The customer transfers to your InstaPay address or mobile number, and **you** confirm each payment after you see the money in your banking app.

## Turn it on

You can do it in two places:

- **During setup**, on the payment step: switch on **InstaPay** and enter your InstaPay address (for example `yourname@instapay`) or the Egyptian mobile number linked to your account. An account name is optional.
- **Later**, in **Settings > Payments**, open the InstaPay card. There you can also change how long an order is held (default 48 hours) and add a note for customers in English and Arabic.

InstaPay needs an address or a mobile number before it can be turned on. It is in Egyptian pounds only.

## What your customer sees

At checkout the customer picks InstaPay. After placing the order they see your InstaPay address or number, the exact amount, the order number to quote in the transfer note, and the deadline. They can optionally type the transfer reference from their banking app, which you will see on the order.

## What you do

1. The order appears as **Awaiting InstaPay**. Check your banking app for the amount.
2. When the money is there, open the order and tap **Payment received** (in the dashboard or in the Orders app).
3. The order is confirmed and can be shipped.

## The rules

- An order waiting for confirmation is **not shipped** and is **not counted as paid revenue**.
- If you do not confirm it before the deadline, the order **expires**: it is cancelled, the stock goes back on sale, and you and the customer are notified.
- An order can be confirmed **only once**. Tapping twice is safe.
- Only confirm after you have seen the money. Ebda cannot check it for you.
