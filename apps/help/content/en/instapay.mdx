## What is planned

Customers will pay by InstaPay transfer, and you will confirm each payment by hand after you see it in your banking app. The design rules we are building to:

- An order waiting for confirmation is **not shipped** and is **not counted as paid revenue**.
- If you do not confirm it in time, the order **expires**.
- An order can be confirmed **only once**.

## Until then

Use [cash on delivery](/en/orders-and-cod/). We will announce InstaPay in this help centre when it is ready, and the status page will not change for it.
