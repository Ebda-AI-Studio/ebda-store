A store owner adds you from **Team** in their settings. You get an email from Ebda Store.

## If you do not have an account yet

The email has a link to choose your password. Set it, then sign in.

## If your email already has an account

The email has an accept link. Open it, sign in with the invited email, and the page adds you to the team (**Staff invitation**). Then press **Continue**. If it says your account is now a staff account, press **Sign in** and sign in again to open the store.

## If the page shows an error

- **Link missing:** open the link from the email again.
- **Invalid or expired:** ask the owner to send the invitation again.
- **Different email:** sign out and sign in with the email that was invited.
- **Email not confirmed:** confirm it first from the confirmation email, then reopen the link.
- **Account owns a store:** it cannot become a staff account. Ask the owner to invite another email.
- **Team is full:** ask the owner to free a seat or upgrade the plan.

## Good to know

- The link works for 72 hours. A newer invitation to the same address replaces the older one.
- Team members have one fixed staff role. They do not see billing or store settings.
