When you turn the app on, a customer can install your store on their phone screen and open it full screen like a normal app. The store itself is the app, so there is nothing to download from an app store. The page is for the store owner only.

## Plans

This feature is included in every paid plan. If your store does not have it, the page opens locked and names the plan that unlocks it, with an **Upgrade** button. You can change your plan in [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/).

## Turn the app on and set it up

1. From the side menu open **Settings**, then the **Mobile app** card.
2. Switch on **Turn the app on for your customers**. Turning it on or off happens right away, without pressing save.
3. To take the name and logo from your store press **Populate from Store**.
4. In **App Identity**: **App Name** (and the Arabic one), the **Short Name** up to **12 characters** (shown under the icon), and the **Description**.
5. In **App Icons** upload one **square PNG** logo, **512×512** pixels or larger, up to **1 MB**. Keep the artwork inside the central 80% so it is not clipped. Ebda Store generates every size from it (Android, iPhone, browser tab) and shows them under **Generated sizes**.
6. Press **Save the app**.

The fields are disabled until you turn the app on.

## Other settings

- **Features**: **Install Prompt** shows the customer a prompt to add the store to their screen. **Push Notifications** needs extra setup and depends on your plan.
- **iOS Settings**: **Status Bar Style** (default white, black, black translucent) and **Splash Screen Color**.
- **Advanced: the app file (manifest)**: **Theme Color** (browser bar colour), **Background Color** (splash screen), **Display Mode** and **Orientation**. You rarely need to change anything here.

## Common questions

**A customer on iPhone cannot find an install button.** iPhone does not show an install prompt. The customer opens the store in Safari, taps Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

**I uploaded the icon and it was refused.** It must be a PNG, square, 512×512 or larger, and under 1 MB.

**The short name will not stay long.** It is limited to 12 characters.
