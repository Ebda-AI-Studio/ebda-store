Suppliers are the people you buy your goods from, and a **purchase order** is the order you record with them. When the goods arrive you record the receipt on the order, so stock goes up and the Inventory and Accounts Payable accounts are updated.

## Who can do this

Only the store owner.

## Plan

Purchase orders are on the **Business** plan (see [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/)). If your plan does not include the feature, the request is refused with `FEATURE_DISABLED` and names the plan you need. The Suppliers page itself is tied to the basic accounting feature.

## Add a supplier

1. In **Stock**, open **Suppliers** and press **+ New Supplier**.
2. Fill in **Name** (required), **Name (Arabic)**, **Contact**, **Phone** and **Email**.
3. Choose **Payment Terms**: cash on delivery, net 15, 30 or 60 days, or **Custom** (then type **Custom terms days**, from 1 to 365).
4. Type **Net lead time (days)**, from 1 to 365: the days from placing an order until the goods arrive. The system uses it for [reorder suggestions](/en/reorder-and-dead-stock/) when a product has no rule of its own.
5. Add notes in English and Arabic if you like, and press **Create supplier**.

In the suppliers list, the menu next to each supplier has **Edit** and **Deactivate**. Deactivating takes the supplier out of new lists, and existing purchase orders stay as they are.

## Create a purchase order

1. In **Stock**, open **Purchase Orders** and press **New purchase order**.
2. **Select supplier**, and set the **Expected receipt date** if you want.
3. Press **+ Add line item**, choose the **Product** and **Variant** (required when the product has variants), and type the **Qty** and **Unit cost (EGP)**. The subtotal is calculated below the table.
4. Add optional notes and press **Create draft PO**. The order is saved as a **Draft**, and nothing is sent to the supplier.

You need at least one line with a product, quantity and unit cost. Order statuses are: Draft, Sent, Partial, Received and Cancelled.

## Receive goods

1. Open the order from the purchase orders list.
2. Under **Receive items**, type in **Receive Qty** for each line the quantity that actually arrived. The columns show **Ordered**, **Received** and **Remaining**, and you cannot receive more than what remains.
3. Press **Receive**.

If you receive only part, the order becomes **Partial** and you can come back to receive the rest later. When every line is fully received it becomes **Received**. After each receipt stock goes up, and open waiting orders (see [Backorders](/en/backorders/)) are cleared oldest first.

If a product has variants and the line was saved without one, it cannot be received, and you get a message telling you to cancel the order and draft it again for each variant.

## Cancel a purchase order

Cancelling is only available while the order is still a **Draft**, from **Purchase order options** then **Cancel PO**. Quantities already received stay, and you cannot restore the order after cancelling.

## Common questions

**How do I create a purchase order?** In **Purchase Orders** press **New purchase order**.

**I received half the shipment?** Open the order, type only the quantity that arrived in **Receive Qty** and press **Receive**; the order is **Partial** until you receive the rest.
