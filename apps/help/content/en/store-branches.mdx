If you have one or more branches, the **Locations** page in your store shows them to customers with their address, phone, opening hours and map link. Only the store owner can manage them.

## Turn locations on

1. From **Settings** open the **Logo and colors** card, then the **Locations** tab.
2. Switch on **Enable Locations**. It is saved right away.

Locations you add while it is off are saved, but they do not show in your store until you turn it on.

## Add a branch

1. Press **+ Add location**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Country** and **Address**. These are required (up to 200, 100 and 500 characters). The rest is optional: Name, Country and Address in Arabic, **Phone**, **WhatsApp**, **Hours** (Arabic and English), **Maps Link URL**, **Maps Embed URL** and **Position**.
3. Map links must start with `http://` or `https://`.
4. **Published (visible on storefront)**: switch it off if the branch is still a draft.
5. Press **Create**. To change one press **Edit**, then **Save**.

The table shows **Name**, **Country**, **Address** and **Status** (**Published** or **Draft**).

## Delete a branch

Press **Delete**, then **Yes, delete**. Deleting removes the branch for good.

## Show it to customers

- The page appears in your store at `/locations`, but no link to it is added to the menu on its own. Add the link from the editor in the header menu, the footer or the top bar (see [Navigation menu and footer links](/en/menu-and-footer-links/)).
- **Show a Branches column in the storefront footer**: adds a "Branches" column to the footer listing your published branches (name, address, phone). It is off by default. It is saved with the **Save branding** button.

## Common questions

**I added a branch and it does not show.** Check **Enable Locations** is on and the branch is **Published**.

**The branch is wrong on the map.** Edit **Maps Link URL**.
