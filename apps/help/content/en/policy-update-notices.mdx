When Ebda Store changes an important policy, a bar titled **We updated our policies** appears at the top of your store pages in the dashboard. It lists each policy with a short summary and the date it takes effect.

## What to do

1. Read the summary of each policy.
2. Press **Read the policy** for the full text. It opens in a new tab.
3. Press **Got it**. One press confirms every notice shown in the bar.

## See what you accepted

Press the shield icon next to your name at the bottom of the left menu (**Security & 2FA**) and scroll to **Policies you accepted**. Each row shows the document, its version, whether you accepted at sign-up or by pressing **Got it**, and the time in UTC.

## Good to know

- The bar does not block anything. You can keep working underneath it.
- The notice belongs to you, not to one store. Confirm it once and it disappears from all your stores.
- If saving fails you see "Could not save. Please try again." and the bar stays.
- The list of accepted policies is read-only.
