## What works today

Only [cash on delivery](/en/orders-and-cod/) is available for customers. The setup flow shows "Cards and wallets" as "Coming soon".

## What is coming

We are integrating Egyptian payment providers so customers can pay by card or mobile wallet. When a provider is ready you will connect your own account from the dashboard. If one provider is down, only that payment method is affected; checkout keeps working with the others.

We do not list provider names or fees here until they are confirmed.
