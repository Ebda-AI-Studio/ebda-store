## The AI switch

Open **AI usage** (or **AI tools**) in your store and use **Use AI features**. When it is off:

- no AI request is made for your store: translation, SEO text, product tags, campaign drafts, review replies and the page assistant are all paused;
- the automatic fill that runs when you add a product leaves your products alone;
- the switch itself stays available, so you can turn AI back on any time.

Your existing content is never changed by turning it on or off.

## Let the assistant suggest your categories

If your store has **no categories yet**, the **Categories** page offers **Suggest categories**.

1. The assistant reads your product names and descriptions (nothing about your customers or orders) and proposes a grouping.
2. You can rename each category in English and Arabic, remove a category, or untick products inside it.
3. Nothing exists until you press **Create these categories**.

If you already have categories, or no products yet, the suggestion is not offered. Each suggestion counts toward your daily AI limit.

## See what a bulk run will cost before it starts

On **AI tools**, every catalogue-wide run (translate, SEO, tags, FAQs and the rest) first shows how many AI requests it can use and how many you have left today. Products that are already filled are skipped, so the real number is usually lower than the estimate.

If the run needs more requests than you have left, it does not start. The message says which limit you hit: your store's daily limit or the platform's daily limit (both reset at 00:00 UTC), or the monthly limit. Try again after the reset, or run it on fewer products.
