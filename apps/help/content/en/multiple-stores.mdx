One account can own several stores. How many depends on your plan: Basic and Growth allow one, Business allows three.

## See your stores

1. Open **Stores** from the menu. Each store shows its name, address and status.
2. Press a store to switch to it.

## Open another store

1. On **Stores**, press **New store**.
2. Follow the steps. If your plan is at its limit, the screen tells you to upgrade first.
3. Billing for the extra stores is paid on your main store.

## Ask the assistant

Ask "how many stores do I have?" and the assistant lists them. Opening a new store stays on the screen: it is a multi-step set-up.
