Open **Store look** in the left menu. The editor opens with a live preview of your store.

## Pick a page

Use the **Page** menu at the top: **Home**, **Product**, **Collection** (the product list page) or **Cart**. For the product page you can also choose which of your products to preview.

## Change the page

1. In **Sections**, press **Add section** to add one. Use the drag handle, or the move up and move down buttons, to reorder.
2. Select a section to edit its content and design in the settings panel. You can also hide, duplicate or remove it.
3. Switch **Preview size** between **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile** to check each one.
4. Press **Undo** or **Redo** if you change your mind.
5. When you are happy, press **Publish**.

The **Style** tab sets colors and fonts. **History** lists published versions. **Ask AI** edits the page from a plain sentence.

## Good to know

- Your changes save automatically as a draft ("Draft saved"). Customers see nothing until you press **Publish**.
- Some blocks, like the product details or the cart summary, can move but not be removed.
- If someone else edited the same page, you choose **Keep my version** or load the latest one.
- Removing a section asks you to confirm, and **Undo** brings it back.
