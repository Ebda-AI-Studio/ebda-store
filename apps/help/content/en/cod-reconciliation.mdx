Couriers collect cash on delivery and send it to you later, minus fees. This page compares what you should have received with what your courier's statement says.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings**, then **Shipping**, then the **Cash-on-delivery collection** tab (**COD reconciliation**). It is for the store owner.

## Import a statement

1. Under **Import a carrier statement**, choose the **Carrier**: Bosta, ShipBlu, Mylerz, Aramex or manual.
2. Upload a **CSV file** from the courier's portal, or paste its text.
3. Press **Import**. You see how many lines are new, already imported, or match no shipment.

The header needs a tracking number column and a collected or net amount column, in Egyptian pounds. Lines the file cannot read are listed with their line number.

## Read the result

The totals show what was owed, collected, fees, net remitted, **Still to receive** and **Over-remitted**. The table lists each shipment with a status such as **Awaiting remittance**, **Settled** or **Mismatch**. **Lines matching no shipment** shows tracking numbers your store does not have.

## Good to know

- Importing the same statement twice changes nothing.
- One upload takes up to 5,000 lines.
- Importing records the statement only. It does not mark any order as paid.
- If carriers are not included in your plan, the page shows an upgrade card.
