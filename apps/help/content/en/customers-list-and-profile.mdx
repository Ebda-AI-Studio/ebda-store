Open **Sales** in the left menu, then the **Customers** tab. Customers appear automatically after their first order. Guests who checked out without an account are listed too, marked **Guest**.

## Find someone

Type a name, email or phone number in the search box (**Search by name, email, or phone...**). Use the segment pills, such as **All**, to filter by type of customer. The list shows each customer's **Total Orders**, **Total Spent** and **Last Order**.

## Open a profile

Press **View** on a row. The profile shows:

- **Contact** details, **Customer group** and the **SMS offers** choice.
- **Last shipping address**.
- Total orders, total spent, average order value and last order.
- **Orders**: every order with its date, status, payment method and total. Press **View** to open one.

## Good to know

- Staff can browse only the first 500 customers of a view. Search to find anyone beyond that.
- On a very large store the list is partial: it covers the latest orders and newest sign-ups. Export gives a fuller picture (owners only, see [Export orders and customers to Excel](/en/export-orders-and-customers-to-excel/)).
- Customers are not created by hand. They come from orders and sign-ups.
