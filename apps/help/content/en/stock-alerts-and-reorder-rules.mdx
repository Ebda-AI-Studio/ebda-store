## Low-stock alert threshold
Every product has a number: when its stock is at or below it, the product is shown as low on stock and you are alerted. Set it per product, or for a selected group at once, from the stock screens.

## Reorder rules
A reorder rule says: when stock reaches this level, order this many, and the supplier needs this many days.
1. Open **Reorder Suggestions** under stock, then press **Reorder rules**.
2. Add a rule for a product (or one variant): reorder point, reorder quantity, lead time and safety days.
3. Switch the rule off any time without deleting it.

Rules are managed by the store owner.

## Ask the assistant
Say "alert me when the rings drop below 3" or "reorder 20 when this product reaches 5". The assistant shows the old and new values first and waits for you. After you apply it you have **Undo** for 24 hours. Changing more than 20 products at once asks for an extra confirmation.
