The automatic scans run on a schedule. To check right now, open **Stock** and press **Scan now** (or **Re-scan now** on the reorder and dead-stock pages).

Each scan can run once per hour for your store. A scan only reads your stock and raises suggestions. It never sends a message to a customer and never changes a product.
