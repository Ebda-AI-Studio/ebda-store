A store owner can invite you to work with them as staff, and the invitation arrives by email. The link in the email opens a **Staff invitation** page where you accept it.

## Who can accept it

Anyone who has the invitation link, as long as they are signed in with the same email address the invitation was sent to.

## Accept the invitation

1. Open the link from the email. The link is valid for **72 hours**.
2. Sign in with the email address the invitation was sent to, and your email must be verified. The invitation is only accepted after sign-in, and the page keeps the invitation until you sign in.
3. The page says **Accepting your invitation…**, and then:
   - **You now have access**: you were added as staff. Press **Continue** and open the dashboard.
   - **Sign in again to continue**: your account is now a staff account, so you must sign in again. Press **Sign in**.

The invitation changes nothing in your account until this page accepts it. Accepting twice is harmless.

## If a message appears

- **This invitation link is incomplete:** open the link from the email again.
- **This invitation link is invalid or has expired:** ask the store owner to send it again. A new invitation replaces the old one.
- **You are signed in with a different email address from the one invited:** sign out and sign in with the invited address.
- **Verify your email address first:** use the verification email we sent you, then open the invitation link again.
- **This account owns a store:** an account that owns a store cannot become a staff account. Ask the store to invite a different email address.
- **This store has reached its staff limit:** ask the owner to make room or upgrade.
- **We could not accept the invitation:** try again in a moment.

## Common questions

**I got an invitation to join a store, how do I accept it?** Open the link from the email, sign in with the same email address, and the acceptance page appears.

**The invitation link is not working.** It has most likely expired (72 hours) or a newer invitation was sent. Ask the store owner for a new invitation.
