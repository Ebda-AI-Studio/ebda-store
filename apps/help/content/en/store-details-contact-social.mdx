All your store's public details are changed in one place, and only the store owner can save there. **Two-step verification** must be turned on for your account before you can save (see [Account security](/en/account-security/)).

## Open the page

From the side menu open **Settings**. The page groups settings as **Store**, **Selling and payments**, **Visibility**, **Team and account** and **For developers**, with a **Search settings...** box. Open the **Logo and colors** card. It opens **Store Branding** with five tabs: **Branding**, **Theme & Colors**, **Contact Info**, **Social Links** and **Locations**.

## Change the name and description

In **Branding**: **Store Name** (required, up to 200 characters), **Store Name (Arabic)**, **Description** and **Description (Arabic)** up to 1000 characters, plus the logo and **Favicon**.

## Change the phone number and address

In **Contact Info**:

- **Email**. This is where the messages from your "Contact us" page are sent (see [Contact form messages from customers](/en/contact-form-messages/)).
- **WhatsApp Number**: an Egyptian mobile number (010, 011, 012 or 015). A wrong number shows "Enter a valid Egyptian mobile number, e.g. 01012345678".
- **Phone** and **Phone (Arabic Display)**, **Address** and **Address (Arabic)**.
- **Map link**: paste a Google or Apple Maps link, and **Make address clickable on storefront** makes the address open the map.
- **WhatsApp buttons**: a product share or ask button, an "Ask about this order" button and a cash-on-delivery confirmation prompt. They work once you add your WhatsApp number (see [WhatsApp buttons](/en/whatsapp-buttons/)).

## Add your Instagram link

In **Social Links** paste your account link in the right box: Instagram, Facebook, Twitter / X, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, LinkedIn, Wikidata Q-ID and Wikipedia. The link must be a valid web link. Under **Footer icon order** use the arrows to set the order in the footer. Only platforms with a link show.

## Save

Press **Save branding** in the bar at the bottom. All the changes in all the tabs are saved together, and you see "Branding saved". If you see "Access denied" your account is not allowed to change the store details.

## Common questions

**I changed it and the store did not update.** Make sure you pressed **Save branding**.

**The WhatsApp number is refused.** It must be an Egyptian mobile number.

**Currency.** This page has no field to change the currency.
