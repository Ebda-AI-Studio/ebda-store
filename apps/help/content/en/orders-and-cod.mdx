## Cash on delivery

COD is on by default. The customer pays the courier when the order arrives. You can add [InstaPay](/en/instapay/) next to it, and you must keep at least one payment method turned on.

After a COD order your customer can see a **Confirm on WhatsApp** button (see [WhatsApp buttons](/en/whatsapp-buttons/)), which makes it easy to confirm the address and time before you ship.

## Order statuses

Every order has a status you move forward as you work on it:

| Status     | Meaning                                 |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Pending    | New order, not yet looked at            |
| Confirmed  | You have confirmed it with the customer |
| Processing | You are preparing it                    |
| Shipped    | It is on its way                        |
| Delivered  | The customer has it                     |
| Cancelled  | The order was cancelled                 |
| Refunded   | The money was returned                  |

Separately, a payment status shows whether the order is **Paid** or **Unpaid**.

## Message a customer

On an order page you can message the customer on WhatsApp with a prefilled text. It opens WhatsApp for you to send; nothing is sent automatically.
