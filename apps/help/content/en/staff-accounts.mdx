Team members get their own login to your store. How many you can have depends on your plan (2, 5 or 15 seats). A team member uses one fixed staff role: they cannot see billing, store settings or payment settings.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Team** under your store.
2. Press **Add member** and enter their name and e-mail.
3. They get an e-mail with a link to accept. Until they accept, the invitation shows as pending and you can cancel it.

## Remove someone

1. On **Team**, press **Remove** next to their name and confirm.
2. Their access ends at once, pending invitations to them expire and their MCP keys are revoked. To bring them back, invite them again.

## Ask the assistant

Ask "remove the employee ahmed@example.com". The assistant shows who will be removed and waits for your confirmation. Inviting a new member stays on the Team screen.
