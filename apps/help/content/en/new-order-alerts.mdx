So you never miss an order, turn on alerts on the device you carry. As soon as a new order arrives, the device tells you, even when the dashboard is closed. There is also the **orders app** at `orders.ebda.app`, built for the phone and working like any app.

## Who can use it

The store owner and staff members. Each person turns alerts on for their own device.

## Turn on alerts on this device

1. Open the dashboard on the device you want alerts on. A box **Never miss an order** may appear once you have an order or finish setting up the store. Press **Turn on alerts**, or **Later** and it comes back after 14 days.
2. Or open **Marketing and offers**, then **Notifications**. At the top of the page is the **Order alerts on this device** card with the button **Turn on order alerts on this device**.
3. The browser asks you to allow notifications. Allow it. The button changes to **Alerts are on**.

The permission request only happens when you press the button, not when you open the page.

If you see "The browser is blocking notifications", you refused them earlier. Open the site settings in your browser, allow notifications and try again. If you see "Alerts are not available right now" try again in a moment.

The devices you turned on appear under **Notification devices**, and you can remove a device from there. See [Account security](/en/account-security/).

## The orders app (orders.ebda.app)

1. Open `orders.ebda.app` on your phone and sign in with the same email and password as your account. If two-step verification is on, it asks for the code. Customer accounts cannot sign in.
2. Press **Install app** to put it on your home screen.
3. Press **Enable** under **Get new-order alerts** so alerts reach you while the app is closed.

On iPhone: install the app first (Share → Add to Home Screen), then open it and turn on alerts. iPhone does not allow notifications before that.

While the app is open, the list refreshes every 30 seconds, and when a new order appears the app plays a short tone and shows **New order received**. The first time you open the app it does not ring for orders that were already there.

## Plan

The **Notifications** page itself appears when push notifications or SMS are on for your plan (push notifications are on the **Growth** plan and above). If the page sends you back to the store dashboard, use the **Never miss an order** box in the dashboard or the orders app. See [Customer notifications](/en/customer-notifications/) and [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/).

## Common questions

**How do I get a sound alert for new orders?** Turn on alerts from Notifications in the dashboard or from the orders app, and allow the browser permission.

**Is there an app to manage orders?** `orders.ebda.app`. Sign in with your account and install it.

**Alerts stopped.** Make sure the browser has not blocked notifications for the site, and that the device is still listed under **Notification devices**; if not, turn alerts on again.
