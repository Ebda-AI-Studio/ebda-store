Shoppers and store owners are handled differently.

## Shoppers (your customers)

On your storefront, a signed-in customer opens **Account Settings**.

- Under **Privacy & data**, **Download my data** gives a JSON file with their profile, orders, addresses and wishlist.
- Under **Danger Zone**, **Delete Account** hides their name, email, phone and addresses for good and signs them out at once. Their past orders stay, without personal data, for tax rules.

## Store owners and team members

The admin has no button for this yet. To get a copy of your own data, or to close your owner or team account, press **Contact Ebda Store** at the bottom of the left menu and tell us what you need.

Closing an owner account is refused while a store still has a live paid subscription, or while money is still in flight. If erasure is not switched on, you are told plainly that nothing was erased. It never shows a false success.

To export or erase one of your customers from the admin, see [Block a customer or erase their data](/en/customer-block-and-erase/).

## Good to know

- Deleting a shopper account cannot be undone.
- Order records are kept, with personal details removed, because tax law requires it.
- Only the person themselves can download their own data from the storefront.
