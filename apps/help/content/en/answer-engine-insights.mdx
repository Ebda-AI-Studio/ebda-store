When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI summaries about a product like yours, your store may or may not be named as a source. The **Answer-Engine Insights** page shows you which, and gives you tools to fix what is missing. The page is for the store owner only.

## Open the page

From **Marketing and offers** open **Answer-Engine Insights**. The page opens only if the feature is in your plan: it is in every paid plan. If your store does not have it you see a locked page naming the plan required.

## What you will see

- **A summary per engine** and the cited rate (**cited rate**), plus **Cited rate — recent runs** as a chart.
- **Prompts checked (latest run)**: for each prompt a tick if your store was named or a cross if not, with the engine and language. If it was named you see a snippet of the answer. If not you see **Cited instead:** followed by the sites that were named.
- **Last checked**: the check runs weekly, so the page is empty until the first check runs: "No citation checks yet".
- **Google Search Console**: **Impressions**, **Clicks**, **CTR**, **Avg. position** and top queries. If it is not connected yet you see "Google Search Console not connected" and there is nothing for you to do, it appears by itself once Ebda Store's access for your store's domain is set up.

If a note says the result is "Based on model knowledge" or "Search grounding is temporarily unavailable", the number is unverified and does not reflect your store's performance.

## Fix a prompt your store does not appear in

1. Next to the prompt you were not cited in press **Run AI citation fix**. It opens **AI Tools**.
2. You can run a single sweep, or **Run all AEO sweeps** (the **AEO — Answer Engine Optimization** section): drafting FAQs and guides and rewriting meta descriptions. In the GEO section there is **Product citation copy**. A sweep fills empty fields only and does not replace anything you wrote unless you turn on **Replace existing**.
3. Sweeps use your AI allowance, and running them is for the store owner.

## Common questions

**How do I show up in ChatGPT?** Insights measure and suggest. Fix the prompts marked with a cross using the sweeps, and complete your product data and FAQs.

**Why is the page empty?** The check runs weekly.

**Can I change the prompts that are checked?** The page has no option for that.
