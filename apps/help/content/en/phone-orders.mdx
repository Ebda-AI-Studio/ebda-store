When a customer calls or messages you on WhatsApp to order, record the order yourself from the dashboard. It is saved unpaid, and you can issue a payment link to send the customer.

## Who can do this

The store owner and staff. Overriding coupon rules (below) is for the store owner only.

## Create the order

1. Open **Orders** and press **New phone order**.
2. Under **Customer** choose **Existing customer** and search by name, email or phone, or **New / Guest** and type their details. **First name** and **Phone** are required; last name and email are optional.
3. Under **Shipping address** write the **Address line** and choose the **Governorate**, then the **Area**. Once both are chosen the **Calculated shipping fee** appears (or "Free delivery"). If no rate is set for the area a warning says the order will save with 0 EGP shipping.
4. If you agreed a different shipping price with the customer, enter it in **Shipping fee override (EGP)**. Leave it blank to use the calculated fee.
5. Under **Products** search by name or SKU and click the product. If it has variants you are asked to choose one, and out-of-stock ones are marked. Change the quantity, and remove any item you do not want.
6. Optional: enter a **Coupon code** and **Notes**.
7. Press **Place order**. The order details page opens with a message showing the number.

The shipping shown before saving is a preview only; tax is calculated when you save.

## Coupons, with or without their rules

A coupon is applied with its rules (customer group, first order, products, dates, usage limits). Only the store owner can turn on **Override coupon rules**, and must enter a **Reason for the override** (at least 3 characters). Your name and the reason are recorded on the order. A staff account cannot do this.

## Send the payment link

The order is created as **unpaid**. On the order details page press **Payment Link**:

1. The **Issue Payment Link** window opens.
2. Choose **Copy link**. It creates the link with your store's default online payment gateway.
3. Copy the link and send it to the customer on WhatsApp, email or any channel. The store does not send it for you.

When the customer pays, the order's payment status updates by itself. If the order is already paid the button is disabled. You need an online payment gateway enabled in [Payment providers](/en/payment-providers/).

## If you see a message

- **Add at least one product before placing the order.**
- **First name and phone are required.**
- **Pick a size or option for every product that has them.**
- **A size on this order is disabled:** re-enable it on the product page or pick another size.
- **Failed to create order:** the message comes with the reason.

Learn more about order statuses in [Orders and cash on delivery](/en/orders-and-cod/).
