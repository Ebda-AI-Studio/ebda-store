**A protected address.** Some addresses match well-known brand names and cannot be taken by default. If the brand is yours, press **Claim this address**, describe your proof (a trademark, your registered business name), add a link to it if you have one, and send. A person at Ebda Store reviews it and replies by email. Until then, pick another address.

**The store limit.** One account can have up to 3 stores. When you try to create another, Ebda Store tells you the limit is reached and shows a request form. Fill it with the reason and proof, and send it. We review it and, if it makes sense, set a higher limit for your account (up to 20), and you can create the stores. Creating many stores in a single day has its own, separate daily limit; if you hit that one, try again tomorrow.

You can have one open claim per address and one open limit request at a time.
