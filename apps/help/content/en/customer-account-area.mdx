Every store has an account area for its customers at `/account`. It is the customer's page, not your dashboard, and it helps your customer follow their orders without asking you. If a customer who is not signed in opens it, they are sent to the sign-in page and return to the same place after signing in.

## What is in it

The side menu has (named as the customer sees them):

- **Dashboard**: total orders, saved addresses and wishlist items, and recent orders.
- **Orders**: **Order History** with order number, date and total, and for each order **View Details** and **Track Order**.
- **Addresses**: **Saved Addresses**: add, edit, delete, and set the **Default**.
- **Loyalty Rewards**: points, tier and points history. It shows when the loyalty programme is on in your store.
- **Wallet**: store credit balance and history. It shows only if your store offers the wallet (see [Customer wallet and store credit](/en/customer-wallet/)).
- **Wholesale**: shows when wholesale pricing is on.
- **Settings**: **Account Settings**: personal information, **Change Password**, **Download my data** and **Delete Account**.

The last button in the menu, **Sign Out**, signs the customer out.

## The customer cannot see their orders

The page shows only **the orders linked to the customer's account in your store**. Ask them:

1. Did they sign in with the same email they ordered with? An account belongs to one store, so an account in another store does not bring its orders here.
2. Did they order without signing in? An order that is not linked to an account does not appear in **Orders**.
3. You can look the order up from [Orders](/en/orders-and-cod/) in your dashboard by their email or order number, and follow it with them from there.

**If the customer forgot the password:** on your store's sign-in page they press forgot password and follow the link sent to their email.

## Common questions

**The customer says there are no orders yet.** **No orders yet** shows when the account has no orders linked to it. Check the email as above.

**Why is loyalty not showing?** Because the loyalty programme is not on in your store.
