## What it is

The customer wallet holds **store credit**: a balance a signed-in customer can spend on your store. It is money you owe the customer until they spend it, so your books show it as a **liability**, never as income.

Credit gets into a wallet when you:

- complete a return with **store credit** as the refund method;
- give a **manual credit** from the customer's page (a goodwill gesture, a correction). A note is required and the action is recorded.

Customers spend it at checkout: part of an order, or all of it. When the wallet covers the whole order, no payment step is needed.

## What your accounting shows

When accounting is on, every movement is recorded the moment it happens, on the account **2150 Customer Wallet Liability**. The balance of 2150 always equals the total of all customers' wallet balances.

| What happened                                                 | Recorded as                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| You give a goodwill or manual credit                          | Expense **5950 Customer Goodwill** up, 2150 up                                                                       |
| A customer pays for an order from the wallet                  | 2150 down by the wallet part, sales recorded in full. The part paid by card or cash on delivery is recorded as usual |
| You take back part of a manual credit                         | The opposite of the entry above                                                                                      |
| An order paid from the wallet is refunded in full             | The sale is reversed, 2150 goes back up and the customer gets the credit back                                        |
| A balance is written off (the customer deleted their account) | 2150 down, **4100 Unused Store Credit** (other income) up                                                            |

Credit you give from a return is recorded with the return, so it is never counted twice.

## Good to know

- Wallet checkout works with accounting on. It no longer needs accounting to be off.
- Store credit that nobody spends stays a liability until you write it off. Only a written-off balance becomes income.
- Existing stores get the two new accounts (5950 and 4100) automatically the first time they are needed.
