The blog lets you write news, stories and tips that show in your store and help you appear on Google. Posts are managed from **Catalog**, then **Blog**. The store owner and team members can write posts, but turning the blog on is for the store owner.

## Turn the blog on

If the blog is off you see at the top "The blog is off right now: Posts you write will not show to customers until you turn the blog on". Press **Turn on the blog**. Posts you write before turning it on are saved but do not show.

## Write a post

1. Press **Write a new post**.
2. Fill **Title (English)**, **URL slug** and **Content (English)**. These are required. The slug is built from the title for a new post (lowercase English letters, numbers and dashes, up to 80 characters), and you can edit it.
3. Optional: **Title (Arabic)**, **Content (Arabic)** and the **Excerpt** in both languages (one or two sentences shown on the blog list), and a **Cover image** with **Upload cover image**. A blank line starts a new paragraph.
4. **Publish to storefront**: if it is off the post is saved as a **draft**. If you turn it on it shows at `/blog/` plus the post slug.
5. Press **Create post**. To change a post press **Edit**, then **Save changes**.

If a required field is empty you see "Title, slug, and content are required."

## The post list

The list has a **Search posts…** box, the count of published posts and drafts, and each post's status (**Published** or **Draft**). From **Post options** you can edit or delete. Delete asks you to confirm (**Delete the post**) and is permanent.

## Show it on the home page

The **Blog** section in the home page editor shows the latest posts, and you set **Posts to show** up to 20 (see [Home page sections](/en/home-page-sections/)). Links to posts redirect automatically if you change the slug (see [Redirects from old links](/en/old-link-redirects/)).

## Common questions

**How do I publish a post?** Write it, turn on **Publish to storefront** and make sure the blog is on.

**Remove the blog.** The blog page has no button to turn the blog off at the moment. To remove one post use **Delete**.

**The post does not show.** Check it is not a draft and the blog is on.
