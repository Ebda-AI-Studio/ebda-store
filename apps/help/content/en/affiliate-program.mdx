Approve partners, and pay out their commission.

## Steps
1. Open **Affiliates** from the Marketing group.
2. Approve or reject a pending partner; suspend one when needed.
3. Record a payout of a partner's pending balance. This books money as paid out.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can approve a partner or record a payout. The payout amount is always the partner's pending balance shown on the card, and it needs your approval. The assistant previews the change and offers Undo for 24 hours where the change can be undone.
