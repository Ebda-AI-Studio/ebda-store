Two pages under **Stock** help you decide: **Reorder** tells you how much to order and why, and **Dead Stock** shows products that are sitting and not selling.

## Who can do this

Only the store owner, and the owner must have two-step verification turned on (see [Account security](/en/account-security/)). Staff members do not have access to the Reorder and Dead Stock pages.

## Reorder suggestions

Open **Stock**, then **Reorder**. Suggestions are based on how fast the product sells, supplier lead time and open backorders. Each card shows:

- **Reorder qty**.
- **Suggested supplier**.
- **Urgency**, in days.
- **Variant** when the product has variants.

You have two buttons:

- **Draft PO**: creates a **draft** [purchase order](/en/suppliers-and-purchase-orders/) with the suggested quantity and supplier. You see "Draft PO created", and nothing is sent to the supplier.
- **Dismiss**: removes the suggestion.

When there are no suggestions, the page says the next scan runs tomorrow at 06:00 Cairo time. The **Re-scan now** button is not for merchants; only the Ebda AI Studio team can use it.

### Reorder rules

For a product to appear as a suggestion you tell the system when to order it. On the Reorder page press **Reorder rules**, then **New rule**, and fill in:

1. **Product**, and **Variant** if it has variants (or **Whole product**).
2. **Reorder when stock is at or below**: a number from 0 to 1,000,000.
3. **Quantity to order**: from 1 to 1,000,000.
4. **Lead time (days)** and **Safety stock (days)**: from 0 to 365.
5. **Preferred supplier** (optional).
6. **Rule is active** to switch it on.

Then **Save**. Each product (or variant) can have only one rule; adding a second shows that the item already has a rule, so edit the existing one. In the rules list you can **Edit** or **Delete** a rule (deleting does not change stock or purchase orders).

## Dead stock

Open **Stock**, then **Dead Stock**. A product appears when it:

- is in stock and has not sold for **90 days or more** (a product that never sold counts from the day it was added), and
- is worth **EGP 5,000 or more** at cost price. If a product has no cost price its value shows as "Unknown", so add the cost price on the product page.

Each card shows the last sale, the stock value and the suggested discount, with clearance wording. You can:

- **Run Clearance**: creates a percentage discount coupon with code `CLEAR-` plus the product's SKU, active straight away. Review it and set its scope under Coupons before relying on it, because the system does not limit it to specific products.
- **Dismiss**: removes the product from the list.

If a coupon with that code already exists, the system tells you to rename it and try again.

## Plan

These pages are not tied to a plan gate in the code, but the suggestions need your stock and sales data and the rules above.

## Common questions

**How much should I reorder of this product?** Open Reorder and read **Reorder qty**. If the product is not there, add a rule for it.

**What is dead stock?** Products in stock that have not sold for 90 days and are worth EGP 5,000 or more at cost.
