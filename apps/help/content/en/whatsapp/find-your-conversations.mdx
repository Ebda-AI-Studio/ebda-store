The **Conversations** screen lists every customer who wrote to you. The owner and the team both see it.

## Filters
Four buttons sit above the list: **All**, **Unread**, **Mine** and **Unassigned**.
- **Unread:** conversations with messages you have not opened. The total shows next to the title.
- **Mine:** conversations assigned to you (by taking over, or by assignment).
- **Unassigned:** conversations nobody has taken.

A conversation can carry marks: a small dot on the avatar while a cash-on-delivery order waits for the customer's answer ([COD guide](/en/whatsapp/cod-confirmations/)), and **Needs your team** when the customer asked for a person ([AI agent guide](/en/whatsapp/ai-agent/)). The number on the right is the number of unread messages.

## Search
Type in the search box: a customer name, a number or a word from a message. Results appear as you type and your word is highlighted. Move through the results with the up and down arrows, press Enter to open the one you are on, and Esc to clear the search.
- If you see "Search is not working right now, try again", try again in a moment.
- Messages older than your retention period are deleted and cannot be found. You choose the period in the [customer data guide](/en/whatsapp/your-customers-data/).

## Marking as read
A conversation is marked read the moment you open it, and the unread counter drops. There is no separate "mark as read" button, and no way to mark a chat unread again.

## If the list does not update
The list updates by itself ([reading a conversation and live updates](/en/whatsapp/read-a-conversation-and-live-updates/)). If your connection drops you see "You are offline".
