## Open a conversation
Press the customer's name in **Conversations**. Messages are in date order with a separator for each day, and times are Cairo time. Under your own messages you see the status: sent, delivered, read or failed ([failed messages guide](/en/whatsapp/failed-messages-and-sending-limits/)). At the top of the chat you see the time left in the 24-hour window ([templates and the 24-hour window](/en/whatsapp/templates-and-the-24-hour-window/)).

- **Long message:** a message over 1 KB is shown shortened with **Read the full message** under it.
- **Image, file or voice note:** it loads with your sign-in when you open the chat. If it says "File unavailable", Meta no longer provides it (it was deleted there or failed to load). Ask the customer to send it again.
- **Voice note:** with the AI agent on, the text appears under it ([AI agent guide](/en/whatsapp/ai-agent/)).
- **Old messages:** messages past your retention period are deleted. History that comes from the WhatsApp Business app shows here, but it never opens the 24-hour window.

## New messages
You never need to refresh. The app stays connected, so a new message or a status change appears on its own. If the connection drops you see "You are offline", and messages you typed send as soon as the connection is back. Sometimes the app reloads the list by itself after missing many updates; that is normal.

## If messages do not show
1. Check your connection. If "You are offline" shows, wait until it clears.
2. Close the page and open it again.
3. If a customer says they wrote and you cannot see it, search by their name or number ([search guide](/en/whatsapp/find-your-conversations/)); they may have written from another number.
