Ebda Chat tells you inside the app only. **There is no e-mail and no phone push, and no screen to choose your alerts.** So you need to open the app to see them.

## The alerts you will see
- **Package renewal:** a week before your package ends, a bar appears above the conversation list: "Your package ends (date). Renew it so campaigns do not stop", with an **Open the wallet** button ([wallet guide](/en/whatsapp/wallet-top-ups-and-refunds/)).
- **Wallet balance is low**, or **you owe a debt:** the same bar, with the amount when it is a debt.
- **An InstaPay payment was rejected:** the reason shows in the same bar ([wallet guide](/en/whatsapp/wallet-top-ups-and-refunds/), Pay with InstaPay).
- **The weekly summary** in the Wallet ([weekly summary guide](/en/whatsapp/weekly-summary/)).
- **Number quality and sending limit:** shown on the **WhatsApp number** page under the number ([connect your number guide](/en/whatsapp/connect-your-own-number/)).
- **A conversation that needs your team:** it is marked **Needs your team** in the list and inside the chat. No message is sent to your phone, so look at the list from time to time ([AI agent guide](/en/whatsapp/ai-agent/)).

## The "someone removed the app from Facebook" notice
If one of the people who connected your number to WhatsApp removed the app from Facebook, you see a notice on the **WhatsApp number** page. **Nothing was disconnected and your numbers still work.** You have two choices:
1. If that person should no longer be connected, disconnect the number from the same page.
2. If it does not matter, press **Keep numbers connected** and the notice goes away.
This button is for the store owner only.
