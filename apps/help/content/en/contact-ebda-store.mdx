You can write to the Ebda Store team from any page of your dashboard.

1. Press **Contact Ebda Store** at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Pick the topic and write what you need.
3. Press **Send**. A person reads it and replies to the email on your account.

You can send up to 5 requests a day. The request goes to Ebda Store staff only; nothing is sent to your customers.
