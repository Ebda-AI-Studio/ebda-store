Only the owner of a store can delete it. Open **Settings** and scroll to **Delete this store**.

## What is checked first

The deletion is refused, with the reason, while any of these is true:

- there is money in flight: paid orders not delivered, transfers waiting for confirmation, open returns, cash on delivery not settled, or a payment in dispute;
- the store subscription is still active and billed (cancel it from Billing first, see below);
- a customer still holds a wallet balance, or an affiliate payout is still waiting to be paid;
- deletion is not switched on yet for your store on the platform (you see the reason, and nothing is deleted);
- it is your main (billing) store and you have other stores (move billing to another store first).

## Cancel the subscription first, and the paid period

The store cannot be scheduled for deletion while its subscription is billed. Cancel it from **Billing**. A paid plan is cancelled at the end of the period you already paid for, so the subscription keeps counting as billed until that date: you can schedule the deletion once the paid period has ended (or at once if it is past due or you are on the free plan). We do not refund the unused part of a paid period, and deleting the store does not change that.

## Confirm, then wait 14 days

Type the store address exactly, then press **Schedule deletion**. Nothing is deleted yet. The store is deleted 14 days later, and we email you when you schedule it, if you cancel it, and when it is deleted. The same checks run again on that day; if one blocks the deletion, the store stays scheduled and Settings shows the reason.

## Change your mind

Until the 14 days end, open **Settings** and press **Cancel the deletion**. Nothing is lost.

## What happens to your data

The store, its products and its customers' personal data are removed. Orders keep the tax and payment records the law requires, without the customer's identity. To keep a copy first, see **Export your store data**.
