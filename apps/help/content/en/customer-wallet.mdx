The customer wallet keeps a store-credit balance for each customer. It is included in the **Growth** and **Business** plans.

## What you can do

- **Refund as store credit.** When you complete a return, choose store credit and the amount lands in the customer's wallet.
- **Adjust a balance.** Open a customer, find the wallet card, and add or remove credit with a short reason.
- **See every movement.** Sales, then Wallet, lists all credits and debits.

## What customers see

Signed-in customers see their balance and history under Account, then Wallet.

## Paying with the wallet

Customers can pay part or all of an order from their balance at checkout. This is not available while accounting is switched on for your store.

## If you do not see Wallet

Your store needs the Growth or Business plan. On Basic, the Wallet page shows an upgrade prompt.
