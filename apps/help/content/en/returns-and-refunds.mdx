## Complete a return and refund the customer

1. Open **Returns & Exchanges** and open the return. It must be **Received** (or **Inspecting**).
2. Press **Complete & Process Refund**.
3. Choose how the money goes back. The dialog shows the exact refund amount before you confirm. **Original payment method** is shown only when the payment provider can take the refund.
4. Confirm. The refund cannot be undone.

Refunding to the original payment method or as store credit needs the owner's permission; team members without it cannot do this step.

## Set the return policy

1. Open **Shipping** and find **Return policy**.
2. Fill **Return window (days)**. Leave it blank to declare no policy, or enter 0 for "returns not accepted".
3. Choose **Return shipping paid by**: **Customer** or **Merchant (free returns)**.
4. Press **Save return policy**.

The policy is shown on your Returns, Terms and FAQ pages and is enforced on return requests, counted from the delivery date.

## Ask the assistant

- For a refund, the assistant shows a card with the return, the method and the exact amount in EGP, which the store works out from the return. You press the confirm button yourself; it cannot be undone.
- For the return policy, the assistant previews the old and new values and offers **Undo** for 24 hours.
