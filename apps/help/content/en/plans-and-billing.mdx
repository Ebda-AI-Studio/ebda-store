Every paid plan includes all supported payment methods and a custom domain. Cash on delivery and InstaPay work from day one; card and wallet gateways (Paymob, Kashier, FawryPay) need your own account with the provider.

**Paying for your plan:** you pay Ebda AI Studio by InstaPay transfer. Your plan starts as soon as our team confirms the transfer by hand. Paying for a plan by card is not available yet. WhatsApp is a `wa.me` chat button on every plan; automated WhatsApp messages are not part of any plan.

After you pass your monthly order cap you get seven days to upgrade; after that new orders are blocked. Existing orders stay available. When a trial ends without a paid plan, new orders are blocked too.

The listed prices are final amounts. Ebda AI Studio is not registered for VAT and charges none; if that changes, VAT is introduced only with at least 30 days of written notice and never applies retroactively. Taxes on your own store are configured separately.

## Ask the assistant to change your plan

- **Downgrade:** the assistant shows a card with what changes (limits you lose) and starts the new plan at the end of the period you paid for. Nothing is charged.
- **Upgrade:** the assistant shows the price before VAT, the VAT and the total, then gives you a link to the payment screen. You confirm the charge there. The assistant never charges your saved card itself.
