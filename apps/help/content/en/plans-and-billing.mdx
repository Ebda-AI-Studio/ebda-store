## The plans

| Plan    | For                | Highlights                                                                                      |
| ------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Starter | Your first store   | A store on your `.ebda.app` address, cash on delivery, WhatsApp button, basic analytics         |
| Growth  | When sales pick up | Everything in Starter, your own domain, Ebda branding removed, more payment methods, team seats |
| Pro     | Busier stores      | Everything in Growth, advanced analytics, higher order volume, priority support                 |
| Scale   | High volume        | Everything in Pro with higher limits                                                            |

## Billing

Plans will be billed in Egyptian pounds. We will publish prices on the website and here before any charge is made. Some limits (for example how many products you can add) depend on your plan, and the dashboard tells you when you reach one.
