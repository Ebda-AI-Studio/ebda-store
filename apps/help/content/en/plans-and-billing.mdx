Try Growth for 14 days. There is no public free plan. Platform commission is zero; payment gateway fees are separate.

| Plan | EGP/month | EGP/year | Staff | Orders/month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Basic | 1,490 | 13,410 | 2 | 300 |
| Growth | 3,490 | 31,410 | 5 | 3,000 |
| Business | 9,990 | 89,910 | 15 | Unlimited |

Annual billing saves 25%. Every paid plan includes unlimited products, a custom domain, and all supported payment methods. After exceeding your monthly order cap, you have seven days to upgrade before new orders are blocked. Existing orders remain available.

Invoices use the listed flat amounts until Ebda registers for VAT. Merchant store taxes are configured separately.
