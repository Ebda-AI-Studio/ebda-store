Try Growth for 14 days. There is no public free plan. Ebda takes no commission on your orders; payment gateway fees are separate, and cash on delivery never carries a fee.

|                                     |     Basic |    Growth |  Business |

| EGP / month                         |     1,490 |     3,490 |     9,990 |
| EGP / year (25% off)                |    13,410 |    31,410 |    89,910 |
| Staff seats                         |         2 |         5 |        15 |
| Orders / month                      |       300 |     3,000 | Unlimited |
| Stores                              |         1 |         1 |         3 |
| Products                            | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Custom domain                       |       Yes |       Yes |       Yes |
| Abandoned-cart recovery             |        No |       Yes |       Yes |

| API access                          |        No |        No |       Yes |
| Analytics                           |     Basic |  Standard |  Advanced |
| Support                             |     Email |      Chat |  Priority |

Every paid plan includes all supported payment methods and a custom domain. WhatsApp is a `wa.me` chat button on every plan; automated WhatsApp messages are not part of any plan.

After you pass your monthly order cap you get seven days to upgrade; after that new orders are blocked. Existing orders stay available. When a trial ends without a paid plan, new orders are blocked too.

Invoices use the listed flat amounts until Ebda registers for VAT. Taxes on your own store are configured separately.
