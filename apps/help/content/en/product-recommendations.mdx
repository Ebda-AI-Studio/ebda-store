You can link each product in your store to products that are suggested with it, so shoppers buy more. There are two kinds: **Frequently Bought Together** (cross-sell, like a case with a phone), and **Upsell Alternatives** (pricier or better products suggested as an alternative). The feature is available on every paid Ebda Store plan, and the store owner sets it up.

## Where to find it

1. From **Products** open the product you want to set suggestions for.
2. On the edit page open the **More product options** menu (the three dots) and choose **Recommendations**.
3. The **Recommendations: product name** page opens.

## Choose the products

The page has two sections:

- **Frequently Bought Together**: products customers commonly buy alongside this one. Up to **8** products.
- **Upsell Alternatives**: higher-value products to suggest as alternatives or upgrades. Up to **4** products.

In each section:

1. Type the product name in **Search products** and pick it from the results. If there is no match you see **No products found**.
2. A product added in one section is not offered in the other, and the product itself is never suggested.
3. Remove any product with the remove button next to it.
4. When you reach the limit a message appears, so remove one first to add another. The current count shows like **3 / 8**.
5. Select **Save Recommendations**. You will see **Saved**. **Back to Edit** takes you back to the product.

The products you picked by hand are what shoppers see as suggestions with the product.

## AI suggestions

Below the two sections there is **AI suggestions** for the product. These come from the last **AI recommendations** sweep that you run from AI Tools. They show to your shoppers automatically in the "You might also like" section **when you have not picked a cross-sell by hand** for that product.

- **Promote** adds one suggestion to your hand-picked cross-sell list.
- **Promote all** adds all the new suggestions (up to the limit).
- **Demote all** removes the ones promoted from AI from the list, and the store goes back to showing AI suggestions.
- **Refresh** loads the latest suggestions.

If there are none yet you see **No AI suggestions yet for this product**. Run the **AI recommendations** sweep from **AI Tools**.

## If something is not working

- If the page is locked, your plan or store does not have the feature. You see an upgrade card. See [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/).
- If you see **Failed to load existing recommendations.** try refreshing the page.
- If saving fails you see **Save failed** with the reason.

## Common questions

**How do I show related products?** Open **Recommendations** for the product, pick the products and save.

**"People who bought this bought that"?** That is the **Frequently Bought Together** section. You pick it by hand, or it comes from AI suggestions.

**What is the difference between cross-sell and upsell?** Cross-sell products complete the product, and upsell products are pricier or better alternatives to it.
