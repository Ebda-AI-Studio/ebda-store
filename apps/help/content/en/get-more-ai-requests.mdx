Every store has a daily number of AI requests. You can see how many are left today on **AI usage** and on **Add-ons**.

When you reach the limit, buy a pack:

1. Open **Billing**, then **Add-ons**, and go to **More AI requests** (or press **Get more requests** on AI usage).
2. Choose a pack and read the exact total. Nothing is charged yet.
3. Press **Yes, charge** and pay by card.

The new daily limit applies as soon as the payment is confirmed and lasts 30 days. Packs add up while they run and they do not renew by themselves. The platform-wide limits still apply to every store.
