The hero banner is the first thing a shopper sees in your store. The old standalone banner page was retired and the banner is now a section inside the **visual editor**. If you open its old link you land in the editor. Only the store owner can use the editor.

## Change the image or add a slide

1. In the side menu open **Store look** (the editor opens on the home page).
2. In the **Sections** tab choose **Hero banner**.
3. Under **Content** pick the **Layout**: **Single banner** or **Slideshow**.
4. Each slide has: **Image**, **Mobile image**, **Image focus** (centre, top or bottom), **Image description**, **Headline**, **Button text**, **Button link**, and **Whole-slide link** if you want the whole slide to open a link.
5. To add a slide use **Add Slide** under the section. You can have up to 10 slides.
6. Press **Publish** so visitors see the change.

The headline, button text and image have an Arabic version and an English version. Links must start with `/` or `https://`, otherwise the editor rejects them.

## Background video

On a slide choose **Background video (optional)** and upload an **MP4** or **WebM** file up to **6 MB**. The video loops with no sound. **Mobile video** lets you use a different video on phones. If the file is over 6 MB or the wrong type, the editor tells you why and does not upload it.

## How it is shown

From the section settings:

- **Slide duration (ms)**: 1000 to 30000.
- **Presentation**: full bleed, framed, or collage (3 photos).
- **Text position**: centre or bottom corner. Some store looks fix the text position and hide this option.
- On phones and tablets the text sits under the photo in the page colours, so the headline colour applies only on large screens.

The banner height on phones is set from **Colours and fonts** (see [Fine-tune your store look](/en/store-look-controls/)).

## Undo

Edits are saved as a **draft** until you publish, and **Undo** works. After publishing you can go back to an older version from the **History** tab (see [Page versions and restore](/en/page-versions-and-restore/)).

## Common questions

**The image is cropped on phones.** Add a **Mobile image** and adjust **Image focus**.

**I changed it but visitors do not see it.** You must press **Publish**. Changes before that are a draft.

**Publish is refused.** The editor lists what to fix first. Your draft is kept.
