Ebda Store adds free **click-to-chat** buttons. A button opens WhatsApp on the customer's phone with a message already written, addressed to your number. No WhatsApp business account or API is needed, and nothing is sent automatically.

## Turn it on

1. In your store, open Branding, then Contact info.
2. Enter your WhatsApp number (an Egyptian mobile). The buttons stay off until a valid number is saved.
3. Switch on the buttons you want:
   - **Product share / ask**: a button on every product page, prefilled with the product name, price and link.
   - **Ask about this order**: lets customers message you from the order page.
   - **Cash-on-delivery confirmation**: after a COD order, shows a Confirm on WhatsApp button and adds it to the order email.

## Where else it shows up

- The setup guide has a button to share your store link on WhatsApp.
- On an order page you can message the customer with a prefilled text.

Messages are in Arabic or English following your store's default language.

<Callout title="Automation is separate">
  Sending order updates and templates automatically through the WhatsApp Business platform is a
  different, paid feature and is not part of these free buttons.
</Callout>
