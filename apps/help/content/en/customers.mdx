Customers appear in your store automatically once they place their first order. From the Customers page you find them and see their orders, and if a customer asks you to delete their data you do it from their profile.

## Find a customer and read the list

1. From your store, open **Customers**.
2. Type a name, email or phone number in the search box (**Search by name, email, or phone...**).
3. Each customer shows **Total Orders**, **Total Spent**, **Last Order** and **Avg order**.
4. Above the list are store-wide figures: **Customers**, **Repeat rate**, **Avg order value** and **Total spent**.
5. Badges describe the customer, such as **VIP**, **Loyal**, **At Risk**, **New** and **Guest**, and you can filter the list by segment.
6. Press **View** on a customer to open their profile.

A **Guest** is someone who bought without an account. They still show in the list, grouped by email or phone.

If your store is very large, the list is partial and a notice says so. If a search matches too many customers it shows the first batch, so type more of the name, phone or email. Team members (staff) see the list capped at 500 rows and without segments.

## The customer profile

The profile shows **Contact** details (name, email, phone, **Customer since**), the **Last shipping address**, statistics (total orders, total spent, average order value, last order), and an **Orders** list with each order's status and payment. Press **View** on an order to open it. It also shows whether the customer opted in to **SMS offers**.

If the **customer wallet** is on, you also see a **Wallet** section. See [Customer wallet](/en/customer-wallet/). To put the customer in a group, pick one under **Customer group**. See [Customer groups](/en/customer-groups/).

## Download a customer's data

In the **Privacy & data** section of the profile, press **Export**. You get a JSON file with everything tied to the account: profile, orders, addresses and wishlist.

## Erase a customer's data

In the same section press **Erase customer**. The **Erase customer data** window opens. Type the customer's email (or phone) to confirm and press **Erase customer**.

- The customer's name, email, phone and addresses are anonymised permanently. This cannot be undone.
- Order records are kept, with the personal data removed, for tax-compliance retention.
- If the customer holds wallet credit, a warning tells you the balance will be written off.
- Afterwards you may see a notice about your **customers list**: if their record there did not match the email or phone, or more than one record matched, it is left unchanged and you delete it by hand.

The privacy section is shown for registered customers only, not guests.

## Who can do this

- List, profile and search: the store owner and team members.
- The Excel export of all customers (**Export Excel**), exporting one customer's data and erasing it: the store owner only.

There is currently no button in the admin to "block" a customer or to delete one in the ordinary sense. The permanent removal is **Erase customer** above.

## Common questions

**Who are my best customers?**
Filter the list by **VIP** or **Loyal** and look at **Total Spent**.

**How many times did this customer order?**
Open their profile. **Total orders** and the **Orders** list answer it.

**A customer asked me to delete their data.**
Follow **Erase customer** above. The tax records of their orders stay, without their name.
