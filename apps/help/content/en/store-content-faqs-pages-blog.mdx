## FAQs
Open **Content** then **FAQs**. Add a question and its answer (and the Arabic version), set the order, and use the switch to show or hide an entry.

## Size charts
Open **Size charts** and press **New chart**. Give it a name, choose cm or inches, set the columns and fill each row with one cell per column. Link it to a product or a whole category.

## Policy pages
Open **Content Pages** and choose Privacy, Terms, Shipping or Returns. Write the text in English and Arabic, then save. These are your own store pages; the platform's notices are separate.

## Blog
Open **Blog** and press **Write a new post**. A post stays a draft until you publish it.

## Ask the assistant
"Add an FAQ about delivery times", "add a size chart for abayas", "rewrite my returns page in 3 short points", "draft a post about Eid gifts". The assistant shows the old and new text side by side, you press Apply, and you can press **Undo** for 24 hours (for new size charts and blog drafts, delete them from the screen if you change your mind). It only writes drafts for the blog and never publishes. Legal wording is yours to check before you rely on it.
