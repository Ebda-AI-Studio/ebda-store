AI shopping assistants read a short guide about your store (llms.txt) and your product feed.

## Create the llms.txt
1. Open **AI tools** in your store.
2. Start the **llms.txt** job. It runs in the background and fills the file where it is empty.

The agent key that lets an assistant place orders is created only on the **Agent key** screen. It is shown once and the assistant never sees it.

## Ask the assistant
Say "generate llms.txt". The assistant shows a card, you confirm, and it queues the job and gives you its id. It never overwrites text you wrote.
