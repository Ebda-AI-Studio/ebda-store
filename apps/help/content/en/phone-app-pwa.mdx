Shoppers can add your store to their phone's home screen like an app.

## Set it up
1. Open **Phone app** in your store and switch it on.
2. Set the app name and the short name (12 characters at most), the description and the colours.
3. Upload a square PNG icon of at least 512 by 512 pixels.

On an iPhone shoppers use Share, then Add to Home Screen. Your plan must include the phone app.

## Ask the assistant
Say "change my app name to Nour". The assistant previews old and new values and offers Undo for 24 hours. Icons stay on the screen because they need an image upload.
