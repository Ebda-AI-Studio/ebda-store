<Callout title="New connections: we watch the first parcel">
  Ebda Store builds each carrier from the carrier's official documentation, and your own account is
  what proves it works. When you save your keys we check them straight away. The first real parcel booked
  through a carrier is watched by the Ebda Store team, so if anything is off we see it immediately and fix it.
</Callout>

## What is available

**Manual delivery** is always available: on an order, type the courier's name and the tracking number
they gave you, then move the order along yourself. See [Shipping](/en/shipping/) and
[Orders and cash on delivery](/en/orders-and-cod/).

## How connecting works

You use your OWN account with the carrier: the parcels, the courier fees and the cash collected on
delivery are between you and the carrier, not Ebda Store. Connecting is only a credentials step in
**Settings > Shipping carriers**:

1. Open the carrier's card and pick **Sandbox** (test) or **Live**. Each mode keeps its own keys, so
   test keys never reach the live system.
2. Paste each value. Every field has a **Where do I find this?** note that names the exact place in the
   carrier's dashboard. Secrets are saved encrypted and are never shown again: the page only shows
   "Saved ••••" and the last 4 characters.
3. Press **Save**. Ebda Store immediately makes one harmless call to the carrier with your keys (the mode you
   just saved); nothing is booked. You see **Connected**, **Credentials wrong** (check them and save again)
   or **Carrier not reachable** (your keys are saved; try **Test connection** again later). **Test
   connection** re-runs the check any time.
4. Once the check passes, switch the carrier **on** and, if you use several, choose your **default**.
   Changing a key later turns the carrier off until it is checked again.
5. Fill in your **pickup address** and the carrier's settings on the same card.

When an order is ready, **Ship** books the parcel with your default carrier. The cash to collect is the
order's unpaid cash-on-delivery amount, sent in EGP. Printing the label, tracking, cancelling and
requesting the courier pickup are on the order.

## Courier pickup

Once a parcel is booked with Bosta, Aramex or Mylerz, the order's shipment card has **Request pickup**:

1. Choose the **pickup date** (today up to 14 days ahead, Cairo time).
2. **Aramex:** choose the window the courier can come (from / to). **Mylerz:** choose one of Mylerz's own
   time slots. **Bosta:** Bosta assigns the time slot itself and the card shows it after the request.
3. Press **Send pickup request**. The courier comes to the **pickup address** saved on the carrier's card.

The card then shows the request (Requested, Failed or Cancelled), the carrier's reference and the slot.
Pressing the button twice, or again while a pickup is already requested, never books a second courier.
If the carrier does not answer, the request shows **Failed** with a note: the pickup may still have been
booked, so check the carrier's dashboard before asking again.

**Cancel pickup** is on the card for Bosta and Aramex. Mylerz has no cancel in its API: cancel it in your
Mylerz portal. ShipBlu requests the pickup itself when the parcel is booked, so its card only shows that.

## Bosta

- **API key:** Bosta dashboard > Settings > API Integration > Request OTP > Create API key. Bosta shows
  the key once. Give it **Read/Write** access, or **Full** access if you want Ebda Store to cancel deliveries.
- **Pickup location ID:** Settings > Pickup locations. Empty uses your default location for parcels;
  **Request pickup** needs it filled in.
- **Tracking:** Bosta pushes every status change to Ebda Store automatically. Nothing to paste: Ebda Store gives
  Bosta the callback address and a secret header with each delivery. If you use a firewall, Bosta calls
  from 34.89.199.241 and 35.246.223.19.
- Cash on delivery up to 30,000 EGP per parcel. The customer's governorate and area must be one Bosta
  serves; otherwise the order asks you to fix the address.

## ShipBlu

- **API key:** ShipBlu merchant dashboard > Integrations tab > the (+) button. For a sandbox key, email
  tech@shipblu.com with your business account name.
- **Parcel size:** pick Small, Medium, Large or Extra large in the card; ShipBlu prices by size, not weight.
- **Pickup:** ShipBlu keeps a new order on hold until a pickup is requested, so Ebda Store requests it as soon as
  the parcel is booked. If ShipBlu refuses, the order shows a note: request the pickup in your ShipBlu
  dashboard.
- **Tracking:** ShipBlu has no push notifications, so Ebda Store checks your open parcels about every 30
  minutes.
- **Cancel:** ShipBlu does not document a cancel call. Cancel the order in your ShipBlu dashboard, then
  use **Force cancel** on the Ebda Store order to stop tracking it.

## Mylerz

- **Username and password:** issued by your Mylerz account manager (the same login as the Mylerz
  merchant portal).
- **Pickup warehouse name:** Mylerz merchant portal > Warehouses, the "Name" column. It must match
  exactly. Test connection lists the names Mylerz knows for you.
- **No public sandbox:** Mylerz publishes no test environment, so connect your real account in **Live**
  mode. The check on save only reads (it lists your warehouses), so nothing is booked.
- **Tracking:** polled about every 30 minutes. Cancel works before pickup.

## Aramex

- **Username, password, account number, account PIN, account entity and country code:** provided by
  Aramex when your shipping API access is set up. Ask your Aramex account manager; the Aramex developer
  site lists the services.
- **Product type:** Aramex wants a domestic product for your account. Ask Aramex which one, then enter it
  in the card's settings.
- **Cash on delivery in EGP:** tick the box on the card only after Aramex confirms in writing that your
  account collects cash on delivery in Egyptian pounds. Until then Ebda Store books prepaid parcels only and
  tells you when an order needs cash collected.
- **Tracking:** polled about every 30 minutes.
- **Cancel:** Aramex's API has no "cancel shipment" call. Ask Aramex support (or use your Aramex portal)
  to cancel, then use **Force cancel** on the Ebda Store order.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The address could not be mapped":** the customer's governorate or area is not one the carrier
  recognises. Correct the order's address (governorate, then area) and press **Ship** again.
- **"Credentials wrong" or "not reachable" on save:** the first means the carrier refused the keys (check them
  and save again), the second means the carrier did not answer (your keys are saved; press **Test connection**
  again later). Use manual delivery meanwhile.
- **The carrier is not answering:** the order is untouched and booking is retried automatically.

## Need another carrier?

Ebda Store supports Bosta, ShipBlu, Mylerz and Aramex, plus manual delivery. If you use another courier, use
manual delivery, or use the **Tell us** link at the bottom of the Shipping carriers page.
