Open **Analytics** in the left menu (the **Analytics** tab). Pick a period at the top: today, yesterday, the last 7, 30 or 90 days, this or last month, this year, or a custom range.

## What you see

- **Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Customers, Avg Order Value**, each compared with the previous period.
- **Sales Overview**: a chart of revenue or orders, daily, weekly or monthly.
- **Inventory snapshot** and **Cashflow**.
- **Top Products**, the **Latest 5 orders** and **Low stock alerts**.
- **Wholesale vs retail** and **Searches with no results** (last 7 days).

## Good to know

- Profit shows **Unknown** when some sold items have no cost price. Add cost prices to see it.
- **Cashflow** is for the store owner and needs a plan with accounting. Without it you see "Cash flow is locked on your plan".
- **Latest 5 orders** and **Low stock alerts** ignore the period you picked.
- If one number fails to load, a red notice appears with **Retry**. Do not trust the figures below it until it loads.
- Searches appear only if shoppers searched the same word at least twice.
