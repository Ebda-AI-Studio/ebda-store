See who subscribed to your newsletter, export the list, and send a short broadcast.

## Steps
1. Open **Newsletter** from the Marketing group.
2. Browse the subscribers; use Export to download the list as a spreadsheet.
3. Use **Broadcast** for a short message to up to 400 subscribers; larger lists use Email campaigns.

## Ask the assistant
Ask how many subscribers you have, or ask it to send a broadcast. The broadcast card shows the audience size and the exact text and needs your approval. The assistant previews the change and offers Undo for 24 hours where the change can be undone.
