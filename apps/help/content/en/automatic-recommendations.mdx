## What shoppers see

A product page shows up to **8** recommended products. They are filled in this order:

1. **Your own picks** (the cross-sell and upsell products you choose on the product's Recommendations page). They always come first.
2. **Bought together**: products that other customers of your store ordered together with this one.
3. **Similar products**: items from your store that are closest in meaning to this product (name, description, category).

Only products that are published and in stock are shown, and only from your own store. A product is never recommended next to itself.

The cart page uses the same rule: your picks first, then products bought together with what is in the cart.

## Turn the automatic part off

Open a product's **Recommendations** page and use the **Automatic recommendations** switch at the top. It applies to the whole store. When it is off, shoppers see only the products you picked yourself. Your picks are never changed by this switch.

## Good to know

- Bought-together lists are recalculated every day from your store's orders, and need a pair to appear in at least two orders. No customer details are used, only which products were in the same order.
- Lists can be up to 5 minutes behind a stock or publishing change. The cart still checks stock when someone adds an item.
- If similar products cannot be found at the moment, the page simply shows your picks and the bought-together products. It never blocks the page.
