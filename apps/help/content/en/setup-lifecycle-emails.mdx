See the welcome and tips emails Ebda Store sends you and whether each one went out.

## Steps
1. Open **Setup emails** from the Marketing group.
2. Each email shows sent, skipped (with the reason), failed or pending.

## Ask the assistant
Ask which automatic emails are sent; the assistant reads the same list. It is view-only. The assistant previews the change and offers Undo for 24 hours where the change can be undone.
