A product bundle sells a fixed group of products as one item at one price, like a kit or a gift set. The shopper adds it to the cart once, and stock is taken from the products inside it, so nothing is counted twice. The store owner creates them.

## This feature is turned on for your store

Product bundles are **not part of any plan**. They are turned on for each store separately by the Ebda Store team. If you open the **Product bundles** tab and it is locked behind a lock card, reach us from the **Talk to us** button or through support and we will turn it on for your store.

## Create a bundle

1. From **Marketing and offers** open the **Product bundles** tab.
2. Select **Add a set** (or **Add your first set**).
3. Enter the **Name** and **Name (Arabic)**, the **Slug** that goes in the link, the description, and upload a picture with **Upload bundle image**.
4. Under **Items** search for products and add them, and set the **Quantity** of each. If a product has variants (size or colour) you choose the variant. The limit is **50 products** per bundle, and each quantity is from 1 to 1000.
5. Under **Pricing** pick the **Pricing type**:
   - **Fixed price**: enter the **Bundle price (EGP)**.
   - **Percentage off**: enter the **Discount (%)** off the components total.
   - **Fixed amount off**: enter the **Discount amount (EGP)** off the components total.
6. Optional: **Start date** and **Expiry date**.
7. Keep **Bundle is active** ticked if you want it on sale straight away, and select **Save**.

As you edit, the page works out the **Components total**, the **Bundle price** and **Can currently make** (or **Unlimited** if the products do not track stock).

## Price rules

The page stops you from setting illogical prices and tells you why:

- The bundle price cannot be more than the total of its products.
- A discount cannot equal or exceed the products total (the bundle would be free).
- A bundle priced at 0 is not sold when its products have a price.
- If the price cannot be split evenly across the items, the page suggests the nearest price that splits and you can take it with the **Use** button.

## Stop selling a bundle

From the bundle options menu choose **Deactivate**. The bundle stops selling, but existing carts and orders keep their reference to it. You can **Edit** any bundle at any time. A bundle's status shows **Active** or **Inactive**, and if it cannot be bought right now you see **(not purchasable)** next to it.

## If saving fails

If you see **Could not save the bundle.**, check that there is at least one product and that the price follows the rules above. If the store is suspended for billing, saving fails.

## Common questions

**How do I make a bundle at a reduced price?** Choose **Fixed price** and enter the price, or **Percentage off**.

**How is stock taken?** From the products inside the bundle, by the quantity you set for each.

**How is it different from a promotion?** A bundle is a new item at one price. A [promotion](/en/promotions/) is a discount on products that are sold on their own.
