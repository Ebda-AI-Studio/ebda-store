Your store has fixed pages such as **About Us**, **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**. You can write their text yourself in Arabic and English. The pages are for the store owner only, and **two-step verification** must be on (see [Account security](/en/account-security/)). The old "Pages" screen in the editor was retired, and pages are edited from here.

## Open the pages list

From **Catalog** open **Pages**. **Content Pages** opens with:

- **Standard pages**: About Us, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Shipping Policy, FAQ.
- **Custom pages** (the ones you added with **Add a page**).

Each page carries a tag: **Custom content** if you wrote its text, or **Using default copy**, together with **Published** or **Draft**. A page with no content written shows the store's default text.

## Write or edit a page

1. Press **Edit** next to the page.
2. Write the **Title (English)** and **Title (Arabic)**, and add a **Cover image** if you like.
3. Build the text from blocks: **Heading**, **Paragraph**, **Bullet list**, **Numbered list**, **Image** and **Button**. Press **Add block** under each column. The English and Arabic columns are independent, and there is a **Preview**. The maximum is 200 blocks per language.
4. **Show on storefront**: if you turn it off the page is unavailable in the store, and the default text is not shown in its place.
5. Press **Save changes**.

## What shows in the store

The **About Us**, **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** pages show the text you wrote. The **Shipping Policy** page shows your shipping zones and prices from [Shipping](/en/shipping/), and the **FAQ** page shows your questions from [FAQs and how-to guides](/en/faqs-and-how-to-guides/). So editing the text of those two pages does not change what is in the store.

## You cannot delete a page

The privacy and terms pages are protected: you edit their text, and deleting a page is for the platform team. If you do not want a page to show, turn off **Show on storefront**.

## Common questions

**Where is my About page?** In **Catalog** then **Pages**, the first one under **Standard pages**.

**How do I write a returns policy?** There is no standard page with that name in the list.

**I edited the privacy policy and it did not show.** Make sure **Show on storefront** is on and you pressed **Save changes**.

**What about a custom page?** It is saved under the slug you type (lowercase Latin letters, numbers and dashes).
