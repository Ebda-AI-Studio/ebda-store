A promotion is a discount shoppers get automatically at checkout, with no code to type. It is different from a coupon, which needs a code.

## Create one
Open **Promotions** in your store menu and press the add button.
1. Name it, choose **percentage** (a whole number from 0 to 100) or **fixed amount** in EGP.
2. Choose what it covers: certain products or categories, or the whole store.
3. Optionally set a minimum order, a maximum discount, first order only, and the start and end dates.
4. Save. It applies to every matching shopper straight away, so check the scope and the dates first.

Use the switch on the list to turn a promotion off and on without deleting it.

## Ask the assistant
"Take 20% off all abayas for Eid." Because a promotion changes the price every shopper pays, the assistant never applies it on its own. It shows a card with the exact discount, what it covers and the dates, and you press **Confirm** (the confirmation is valid for 15 minutes). You can undo it for 24 hours, which switches the promotion off.
