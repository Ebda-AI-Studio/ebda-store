## Coupons
Open **Discount Coupons** in your store menu.
1. Press **Create Coupon**.
2. Enter the code shoppers will type, a name, the type (**percentage** is a whole number from 1 to 100, **fixed** is an amount in EGP) and the value.
3. Optionally set a minimum order, a maximum discount, how many times it can be used, and the start and end dates.
4. Save. The coupon is active straight away. Use **Deactivate** to stop it without deleting it.

## Product sets
Open **Product sets**, press **Add a set**, name it, add the products and their quantities, then choose the pricing: a fixed set price, a percentage off, or a fixed amount off. The set price must be possible to split across its products; the page tells you the nearest price that works.

## Recommended products
Open a product, then **Recommendations**. Pick the products shown as related and the ones offered as an upgrade. Your own picks always come first.

## Ask the assistant
Ask in your own words: "make a 10% coupon for Eid", "switch off coupon EID10", "make a set of these two products for 150 EGP". The assistant shows a preview of exactly what will change, you press Apply, and you can press **Undo** for 24 hours. Anything that changes a price asks you to confirm first. It cannot delete coupons or sets; do that from the screen.
