Some features are add-ons: they are not part of your plan and you can add them yourself. You do not need to ask anyone.

1. Open **Billing**, then **Add-ons** (or press **Get this add-on** on a locked screen, which takes you to the right card).
2. Press **Buy** next to the add-on. You see the exact total first, and nothing is charged yet.
3. Press **Yes, charge** and pay by card on the secure page.

The add-on turns on by itself as soon as the payment is confirmed. One payment covers 30 days. It then renews every 30 days on your saved card for the same price until you stop it, and we email you before each renewal. If the price changes, or your plan starts to include the feature, we do not charge you and tell you instead.

To stop it, press **Stop renewing**. It stays on until the 30 days you paid for end. If a payment does not go through, nothing is added and nothing is charged. Only the store owner can buy or cancel add-ons.

Each add-on shows where it stands: not added, waiting for payment, active, ending soon, ended, refunded, or already in your plan. Under the list, **Your add-on payments** shows every payment with its receipt. Add-ons are paid by card for now; InstaPay is not available for them yet.
