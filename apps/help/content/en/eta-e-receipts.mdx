## Who this is for

Only for stores that are **verified for VAT** (see [VAT registration](/en/vat-registration/)) and that the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) requires to issue electronic receipts. The ETA decides who must issue them; ask your accountant. Ebda Store does not decide that for you, and it does not file your VAT returns.

The feature is **off by default**. Ebda Store support switches it on for your store. Until then nothing is sent to the ETA.

## What you need from the ETA

1. Register Ebda Store as a **POS / ERP system** in your ETA profile. The ETA gives you a **client id**, a **client secret** and a **POS serial number** (and a pre-shared key where your profile has one).
2. Register at least one **item code** (an EGS code, in the form `EG-<your registration number>-<your code>`) and wait for the ETA to approve it. Ebda Store uses this code on every receipt line.
3. Know your **branch code** and your **activity code**.

## Connect

Open your store, then **Tax & VAT**. Once your VAT is verified and support has switched the feature on, an **ETA e-receipts** card appears under the VAT registration card. Fill in the form and press **Save**. Start with **Test (PreProd)** to try the ETA test system; switch to **Production** only when the test works. Press **Check connection**: it only signs in, it sends no receipt.

Your client secret and pre-shared key are stored encrypted. They are never shown again, only the last four characters.

## What happens to an order

When a VAT order is placed, Ebda Store builds the receipt, sends it, and then asks the ETA for its verdict. The order page shows the status:

- **Queued / Retrying**: waiting to be sent, or the ETA was busy and we will try again.
- **Submitted**: the ETA received it and is checking it.
- **Accepted**: valid. The receipt QR code and link appear on the order.
- **Rejected / Failed**: the ETA refused it or it could not be built. The reason code is shown. Press **Issue again** after fixing the cause.
- **Late**: the ETA allows 24 hours. After that you must raise a Late Submission Request in the ETA portal.

A refund sends a **return receipt** automatically: a full refund returns everything not already returned, and a partial return (some items from the order) returns just those items. A refund that is only a price adjustment, with no items sent back, waits on the order page until you choose the **ETA return reason** (International return receipt, or Sales issued before adoption). Choose the one that is true for the sale; Ebda Store then sends a return receipt without reference for the refunded amount. If the ETA refuses it, ask your accountant to issue the credit.

## Good to know

- Receipts are currently sent **unsigned**: the ETA documents that signature checking is not switched on for receipts yet.
- Invoices between businesses (B2B e-invoices) are not sent by Ebda Store yet. They need a signature from your ETA eSeal on a USB token, which only you can use.
