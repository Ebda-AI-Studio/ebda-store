A backorder lets a customer buy a product that is out of stock, and you supply it when the goods reach you. These orders collect on the **Backorders** page.

## Who can do this

Only the store owner can open the Backorders page and cancel from it. Staff members can turn backorder on or off from the Inventory page.

## Plan

The feature is on the **Growth** plan and above (see [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/)). If your plan does not include it, the request is refused with `FEATURE_DISABLED` and names the plan you need.

## Turn backorder on

1. In **Stock**, open the **Inventory** tab.
2. Press **Inventory options** at the top, then **Enable backorder on all products**.
3. Confirm. You see "Backorder enabled on N products".

This applies to **every active product** at once. Draft and archived products are skipped. The **Allows Backorder** column in Inventory shows which products have it on. To turn it off, the same menu has **Disable backorder on all products**, after which out-of-stock products can no longer be ordered.

## Track waiting orders

Open **Stock**, then **Backorders**. The table shows the order number (it opens the order), the customer, the product, the quantity and the days waiting. When you receive goods against a [purchase order](/en/suppliers-and-purchase-orders/), waiting orders are cleared oldest first (FIFO).

An empty page means there are no open backorders.

## Cancel a backorder

1. Press **Cancel** next to the order.
2. Write the **cancellation reason**. It is internal, kept on the order's history, and not sent to the customer.
3. Press **Cancel line & reverse the sale**.

<Callout title="Cancelling does not refund money">
  Cancelling a backorder cancels the line and reverses its sale in your books, but it **sends no money to the customer**. If the customer already paid, the window shows how much you still owe, and you refund it yourself at your payment provider. A cancel cannot be undone.
</Callout>

If the order was paid and this line has an amount to give back, the customer receives a notice that the refund was recorded, so make sure you actually refund them.

## Common questions

**A customer ordered an out-of-stock item. What now?** If backorder is on, it joins the Backorders list and clears automatically when stock arrives. If you cannot supply it, cancel it and refund the customer.

**Can I enable backorder for one product only?** On the current screen, backorder is switched for all active products at once.
