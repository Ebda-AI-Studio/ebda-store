Your template ships looking like its design mock-up. Every part of that look has a control in the visual editor, so you can keep it, soften it, or go back to the plain version. Open **Visual editor, Colours and fonts**, and change a control: it is saved in your draft and goes live when you press **Publish**.

A control you never touched follows the look. Pressing **Reset** next to an edited control puts it back to the look's own value. Switching to a different look with "reset my design" clears all of them.

## How the panel is organised

Open **Visual editor, Colours and fonts**. The first screen has what most people want:

- **Colour palettes**: six ready palettes. Press one and your page, text and brand colours change together, as a draft.
- **Three colours**: the main colour, the page background and the text colour, each shown as the colour itself.
- **Spacing between sections**: Compact, Regular or Airy. It scales every section whose own spacing you did not set.
- **Corners** (Square, Soft, Round) and **Font**.

Below that, the groups **Header and navigation**, **Buttons and cards**, **Images** and **All colours** open one at a time. **Advanced** holds the font library, the text styles for every part of the page and the rarely used switches. The search box at the top finds any setting by name. **Reset all changes** puts everything back to the look.

## Spacing of one section, per device

Select a section, open **Design**. **Spacing** is the first block: choose **Above** and **Below** from None, Tight, Normal, Wide, Wider or Widest, with the real height shown next to it. The hero has it too. By default **Same on all devices** is on. Turn it off to set Desktop, Tablet and Mobile separately: the device switch at the top of the editor decides which one you are editing, and a dot marks a device that differs. The gap between two sections is the bottom of the first plus the top of the second.

## Store name without a logo

Group **Header and navigation**, control **Store name without a logo**.

- **Designed wordmark** (the default): your store name set large in the look's display face with a small spaced Latin line under or beside it, for example قمر over QAMAR.
- **Plain name**: just the store name in the normal header text.

If you upload a logo, the logo is always shown and this control does nothing.

## Hero height on phones

Group **Images**, control **Hero height on phones**.

- **Tall (4:5)**: a portrait picture that fills most of the first screen, like the design mock-up.
- **Square (1:1)**: shorter, leaves room for your first products.
- **Wide strip**: the classic landscape banner.

Until you pick one, the look decides. Phones only: on a computer the hero looks the same as before.

## Bottom bar on phones

Group **Header and navigation**, control **Bottom bar on phones**.

- **Not on the home page** (the default): the home page has no bottom bar, and the cart bag and menu sit in the header, like the design mock-up. The bar shows on every other page.
- **On every page**: the bar you had before (home, wishlist, cart, search, menu).
- **Never**: no bottom bar anywhere; the header keeps the bag and menu.

Checkout and sign-in never show the bar.

## Section heading weight and price colour

Group **Advanced**, controls **Section heading weight** and **Price colour on cards**.

- **Section heading weight**: Bold (the design mock-up) or Regular. A few display fonts have one weight only, so the look keeps those on Regular; picking Bold there just thickens the same letters.
- **Price colour on cards**: the look's accent colour (the default, darkened or lightened until it is easy to read on your page) or your normal text colour.

If you set a colour or weight for a heading or price in the zone settings, that choice always wins.

## Card details on the home page

Group **Buttons and cards**, controls **Wishlist heart**, **Colour dots** and **Tag chips** on home cards.

The design mock-up shows bare cards: a photo, the name and the price. Each of the three details is hidden by default and you can show any of them on its own. They only change the cards in the home page sections; the product list, the product page and the wishlist keep their hearts, colours and tags.

## Space between home cards

Group **Advanced**, control **Space between home cards**.

- **Tight**: a hairline between the photos, like a lookbook (the Atelier look's default).
- **Regular**: the standard spacing.
- **Airy**: generous space around every card.

It changes the product grids in the home page sections only; the product list keeps its own "Space between cards" setting.
