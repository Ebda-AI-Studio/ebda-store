Your template ships looking like its design mock-up. Every part of that look has a control in the visual editor, so you can keep it, soften it, or go back to the plain version. Open **Visual editor, Theme**, and change a control: it is saved in your draft and goes live when you press **Publish**.

A control you never touched follows the look. Pressing **Reset** next to an edited control puts it back to the look's own value. Switching to a different look with "reset my design" clears all of them.

## Store name without a logo

Group **Header**, control **Store name without a logo**.

- **Designed wordmark** (the default): your store name set large in the look's display face with a small spaced Latin line under or beside it, for example قمر over QAMAR.
- **Plain name**: just the store name in the normal header text.

If you upload a logo, the logo is always shown and this control does nothing.

## Hero height on phones

Group **Background and rhythm**, control **Hero height on phones**.

- **Tall (4:5)**: a portrait picture that fills most of the first screen, like the design mock-up.
- **Square (1:1)**: shorter, leaves room for your first products.
- **Wide strip**: the classic landscape banner.

Until you pick one, the look decides. Phones only: on a computer the hero looks the same as before.

## Bottom bar on phones

Group **Header**, control **Bottom bar on phones**.

- **Not on the home page** (the default): the home page has no bottom bar, and the cart bag and menu sit in the header, like the design mock-up. The bar shows on every other page.
- **On every page**: the bar you had before (home, wishlist, cart, search, menu).
- **Never**: no bottom bar anywhere; the header keeps the bag and menu.

Checkout and sign-in never show the bar.
