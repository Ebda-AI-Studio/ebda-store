Add your Meta, TikTok and Google pixels by their public ids.

## Steps
1. Open **Marketing pixels** from the Marketing group.
2. Turn a platform on and paste its public id.
3. Access tokens for server-side tracking are pasted on this screen only, never in the assistant.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can turn a pixel on or off and set its public id, with a preview and Undo for 24 hours. It never accepts an access token. The assistant previews the change and offers Undo for 24 hours where the change can be undone.
