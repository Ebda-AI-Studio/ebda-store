FAQs and how-to guides show to your customers in the store and help Google and AI assistants understand your store and answer with it. The page is for the store owner only, and **two-step verification** must be on (see [Account security](/en/account-security/)). Questions for one product have their own page: [Product FAQs](/en/product-faqs/).

## Open the page

From **Catalog** open **Pages**, then press the link "Frequently asked questions live here". The **Questions and answers** page opens with two parts: **Frequently asked questions** and **How-to guides**.

## Add a question

1. Press **Add FAQ**.
2. Write the **Question** and **Answer** (in English). The question is up to 500 characters and the answer up to 5000. There are also **Question (Arabic)** and **Answer (Arabic)**.
3. Press **Save**.

Each question has **Active** or **Inactive** (an inactive one does not show), **Move up** and **Move down** to reorder, and **Delete**. Delete happens right away.

## Add a how-to guide

1. Press **Add Guide**.
2. Write the **Title** (up to 200 characters) and the **Description** (up to 2000) in English and Arabic.
3. Under **Steps** press **Add Step** and write the **Step Name** and the **Instruction**. At least one step is required, up to 20.
4. Press **Save**.

## Let AI draft them

From **Marketing and offers** open **AI Tools**, and in the **AEO — Answer Engine Optimization** section:

- **Draft FAQs with AI**: up to 8 questions about delivery, payments, returns and care, in Arabic and English.
- **Draft How-To Guides with AI**: up to 3 guides.
- **Draft Product FAQs with AI**: up to 5 questions per product.

What you edited by hand is kept and not removed. A run uses your daily AI allowance, and only the store owner can run it.

## Common questions

**How do I add FAQs to my store?** Open the page and press **Add FAQ**.

**The question did not show in the store.** Make sure it is **Active**. The store's FAQ page shows your questions, and if you added none it shows a ready-made general set.

**The page says I must turn on two-step verification.** Turn it on from [Account security](/en/account-security/).
