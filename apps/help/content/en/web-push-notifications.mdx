Send a push notification to every shopper who allowed notifications on your storefront.

## Steps
1. Open **Notifications** from the Marketing group.
2. Write the title and body in English and Arabic, and an optional https link.
3. Send it. Only shoppers who allowed notifications receive it; it cannot be recalled.

## Ask the assistant
Ask it to send a flash-sale push. The card shows all four texts and needs your approval. The assistant previews the change and offers Undo for 24 hours where the change can be undone.
