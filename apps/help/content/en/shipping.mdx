## What works today

You set **shipping zones and fees** yourself, telling customers where you deliver and how much it costs. You can enter area fees one by one, or export the template, fill in the fees and import it as CSV or XLSX. You then hand the order to your own courier.

<Callout type="soon" title="Coming soon: carriers">
  Connecting a carrier account (Bosta and Aramex are the first planned) to create shipments, print
  labels, track parcels and reconcile cash collected on delivery is not available yet.
</Callout>

## What to expect

When carriers arrive, each order will get a tracking status automatically and the cash a courier collected will be matched against your COD orders. Until then, move orders to **Shipped** and **Delivered** yourself. See [Orders and cash on delivery](/en/orders-and-cod/).
