Promotional SMS only goes to customers who agreed. The choice belongs to the shopper, not to you or your team. There is no personal SMS setting in the admin.

## What the shopper sees

- In **Account Settings** on your storefront there is an **SMS offers** card. A shopper with a phone number ticks **Send me offers by SMS**. Later they can press **Stop SMS offers**.
- Every promotional SMS carries an opt-out link. Opening it and pressing **Stop SMS offers** stops promotional SMS to that number from your store, with no sign-in.
- Order and delivery messages are not affected.

## What you see

Open **Customers**. A customer who agreed has an **SMS offers** badge in the list. On the customer's profile, the **SMS offers** field shows **Opted in**, **Opted out** or **Not given**, with the date and where it happened: at checkout, from the account, or via the message link.

## Good to know

- The card only shows when your store can send promotional SMS (SMS is on with working credentials and a promotional message enabled), or when the customer had already agreed.
- A shopper with no phone number on their profile is asked to add one first.
- You cannot opt a customer in for them. Withdrawing always works.
