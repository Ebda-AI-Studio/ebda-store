Every store has a list of features. Some come with your plan; a few are switched on by the Ebda team.

## See what is on

1. Open your store and go to **Features**.
2. Features that are on show a tick. A locked feature tells you why: your plan does not include it, or the Ebda team has to enable it.

## Get a locked feature

- If your plan does not include it, open **Plans and billing** and upgrade.
- If it says the Ebda team must enable it, contact support. There is no add-on checkout yet, so nobody else can turn it on for you.

## Ask the assistant

Ask "why is this feature locked?" and the assistant reads your feature list and tells you the reason. It cannot turn a feature on. It never changes anything without a preview, and you can undo changes for 24 hours.
