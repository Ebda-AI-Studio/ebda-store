Every store has a list of features. The Business plan includes all of them; Basic and Growth include part of the list.

## See what is on

1. Open your store and go to **Features**.
2. Features that are on show a tick. A locked feature tells you which plan includes it.

## Get a locked feature

- Open **Plans and billing** and upgrade to the plan the card names (the Business plan has every feature).
- On a Business store nothing is locked. If a feature is switched off for your store, contact support and we will turn it on.

## Ask the assistant

Ask "why is this feature locked?" and the assistant reads your feature list and tells you the reason. It cannot turn a feature on. It never changes anything without a preview, and you can undo changes for 24 hours.
