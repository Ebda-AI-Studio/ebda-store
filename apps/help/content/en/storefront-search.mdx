Every storefront search box shows matching products while the shopper types, starting from the **first letter**. One Arabic letter is already a word, so there is no minimum length. This works in the header of every template and on the search page.

## What a shopper sees

- A short list of products with photo and price under the box. Matching letters are highlighted.
- **Enter** opens the full results page. **Esc** closes the list.
- If nothing matches, or the connection drops, the box says so in plain words instead of staying blank.

## It understands Arabic spellings

The same product is found whichever way it is typed: with or without the dots and the hamza on alef (ا أ إ آ), ي and ى, ة and ه, and with or without diacritics. A shopper typing `شنطه` finds `شنطة`.

## It understands plurals and prefixes

On the storefront, `شنط`, `شنطة`, `الشنط` and `شنطتين` all find the same bags, and `كتب` finds `الكتب`, `كتابين` and `مكتبة`. Products that contain the exact word the shopper typed are always listed first; products found only through another form of the word come after them.

## Add the words your shoppers really use

Open a product, then **Search words**. Write the other names people use, separated by commas: `حقيبة, شنطة يد, bag, handbag`. These words are never shown in the store. Leave the field empty and AI Autopilot suggests them; words you write yourself are never replaced.

<Callout title="Keep it short">
  A handful of real words works better than a long list. Add what a shopper would type, not
  everything that is vaguely related.
</Callout>

## Keyboard and screen readers

The box is a proper combobox. **Down** and **Up** move through the suggestions (and wrap around), **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last, **Enter** opens the highlighted one, **Tab** and **Esc** close the list. A screen reader announces how many results there are as the list changes. In Arabic the list opens on the right edge, mirrored from English.
