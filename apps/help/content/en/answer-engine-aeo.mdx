Open **Answer-Engine Insights** to see whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews mention your store. The page fills in after the weekly check runs. It needs a plan that includes answer-engine insights.

## The description they read
In **Google visibility** you can write a short, conversational description of your store in English and Arabic (up to 400 characters). Write it the way you would answer "what does this store sell?".

## Ask the assistant
Say "write my answer-engine description: ...". The assistant previews the old and new text and offers **Undo** for 24 hours. Reports and bulk AI sweeps stay on the Answer-Engine screen.
