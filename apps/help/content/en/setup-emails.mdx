Ebda Store sends you, as the store owner, a few emails that help you get your store ready and win your first order, and they go out based on what has actually happened in your store, not on the date alone. This page is a log of those emails and what happened to each.

**Important:** these emails are for your own inbox, **not for your customers**. Customers only get the emails about their own orders, and for marketing to them use [Email campaigns](/en/email-campaigns/). The page is view-only, and there is nothing to edit on each email.

## Where to find it

From **Marketing and offers** open the **Setup emails** tab.

## The emails that are sent

The table lists each email with the columns **Email**, **Status**, **When** and **Note**:

- **Add your first products** (day 1, if no products yet).
- **Cash and shipping ready?** (day 3, if not set).
- **Order from your store like a customer** (day 5, if no order yet).
- **Put the link in your bio** (day 7).
- **Trial recap** (4 days before the trial ends).
- **First order: ask for a referral** (after the first order).

## What the status means

- **Not sent yet**: its time has not come.
- **Sent**: it reached you.
- **Not needed**: Ebda Store saw you already finished that step, so it did not send it. The reason shows in **Note**, like **You already added products**, **Payment and shipping were already set** or **An order was already placed**.
- **Failed to send**: the email did not go out, and the reason is in **Note** (like **The email could not be queued**).

Other reasons you may see are **Not on a trial**, **Store not active** and **No owner email**.

## Stop these emails

Every email has an unsubscribe link. If you select it, no more setup or trial emails are sent to you, and the page shows **You unsubscribed**. Order and billing emails are not affected. You unsubscribe from the link in the email, not from the page.

## If the page does not open

If you see **Could not load the email log.** try refreshing the page. The page needs no particular plan.

## Common questions

**What emails are sent to the customer?** This page is not about customers. These are emails to your own inbox. A customer's emails go out with their order status.

**Why is an email marked "Not needed"?** Because you finished the step before its time, so there was no need to send it.
