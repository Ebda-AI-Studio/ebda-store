Product FAQs answer your customer before they ask: material, size, warranty, how to use it. They appear on the product page and are also sent to Google as FAQ rich results.

## Who can do this

The store owner and staff members who can manage products.

## Add a question yourself

1. From **Products** open the product to edit it.
2. Scroll to the **FAQs** section, which comes after **Search appearance**.
3. Press **Add FAQ**.
4. Write the **Question (EN)** and the **Answer (EN)**; both are required. You can also write the **Question (AR)** and **Answer (AR)** so Arabic shoppers see Arabic text.
5. Press **Save**, or **Cancel** if you change your mind.

To change an existing question press **Edit** next to it, change the text and press **Save**. To remove one press **Delete**; a "Question deleted" message appears with an **Undo** button in case it was a mistake.

A question can be up to 500 characters and an answer up to 5000 characters.

## Let AI draft them

1. In the **FAQs** section press **Generate with AI**.
2. You get about 5 drafts, each marked "AI draft — review before saving".
3. Review each one, edit what you need and save them one by one. Or press **Save all AI drafts**, or **Discard AI drafts** if you do not like them.

Nothing shows to your customer until you save it. The AI writes from your product's details, so check the answers, especially any number or warranty.

## If something goes wrong

- **AI generation failed:** the AI usage quota may be used up or the service busy. Try again in a little while, or write the questions by hand.
- **Failed to save FAQ:** make sure the English question and answer are filled in, then try again.
