When we make a material change to the terms or the privacy policy, we tell you. The notice appears inside your dashboard, and it does not lock you out.

## What appears

A banner **We updated our policies** appears at the top of your store pages. It has a line for each updated policy: its name, a summary of the change and **Takes effect** on a given date, plus a **Read the policy** link that opens the full text in a new tab.

## What to do

1. Press **Read the policy** and read the update.
2. Press **Got it**. This records that you received every notice in the banner, and the banner disappears.

The banner does not stop you working: the pages underneath work normally until you decide. The notice is on **your account**, not on the store, so if you have more than one store and confirm from one, it disappears from the others.

If you see "Could not save. Please try again." try again. If the notice was already confirmed elsewhere the banner goes away by itself.

## Who sees it

The store owner and staff members, each on their own account. No particular plan is needed.

## See the policies you accepted

Open **Security & 2FA** from the menu and scroll to **Policies you accepted**. You see each document, its **Version**, how it was recorded (**At signup** or **Acknowledged the notice**) and when, in UTC time. The section is read-only.

## Common questions

**I got a policy update notice, what do I do?** Read the policy with **Read the policy**, then press **Got it**.

**The banner will not go away.** Make sure you pressed **Got it**. If a save error appears try again, otherwise refresh the page.

**I want to know what I agreed to.** Go to **Security & 2FA**, section **Policies you accepted**. See also [Account security](/en/account-security/).
