Your store always works at `<your-address>.ebda.app`. A custom domain such as `yourshop.com` is an extra, available on plans that include it (see [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/)).

## How it works

1. **Enter your domain** on the Domain page of your store. This is a claim. It is not live yet and does not change where customers land.
2. **Add the DNS records** shown on the page at your domain registrar. The status reads _Pending DNS verification_ while we wait.
3. **Verified.** When your DNS is confirmed the status becomes _Verified_, an SSL certificate is active and your store is reachable at `https://yourshop.com`.

## If it does not verify

- If the status is _Verification failed_, check the DNS records and save the domain again.
- A claim that is **not verified within 72 hours is released automatically**. This stops anyone holding a name they do not own. You can claim it again at any time.
- A domain that is already active on another store cannot be claimed.

DNS changes can take a while to spread. Keep sharing your `.ebda.app` address in the meantime.
