Warehouses let you keep stock in more than one place, for example Cairo and Alexandria. They are part of the Business plan.

## Add or edit a warehouse
1. Open **Stock** then **Warehouses**.
2. Press **Add warehouse**, type a name and a short code such as `CAI` (letters, digits, - and _ only).
3. Higher **priority** is picked first when an order is routed. Mark one as the **default** warehouse.
4. To stop using a warehouse, switch it off. The stock stays; you cannot switch off your last active warehouse.

## Change the units in a warehouse
Open the warehouse and add or remove units of a product. The count never goes below zero, and every change is kept in the stock history.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can prepare this for you. It always shows a preview first, and you press the button yourself. Say, for example, 'add a warehouse called Alexandria with code ALX' or 'add 20 units of this product to the Cairo warehouse'. Undo is offered for 24 hours.
