You can have a live store in a few minutes. No card is needed to sign up.

## 1. Sign up

Open the dashboard and choose **Create your store**. Enter your name, email, an optional Egyptian mobile number and a password (at least 8 characters with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol). Use a permanent email address: temporary email services are not accepted.

## 2. Verify your email

We email you a verification link. It works once and is valid for **24 hours**. If it expires, ask for a new one from the same page. You cannot continue setting up until the email is verified.

## 3. Name your store and pick its address

Choose a store name and an address. Your free address is `<your-address>.ebda.app`.

- Use lowercase Latin letters, digits and single hyphens, between 3 and 40 characters.
- The page tells you immediately if an address is taken or reserved.
- Names that match a protected brand are held back. Contact support to claim one that is really yours.

## 4. Pick a look

Choose one of the templates: Modern, Classic, Minimal, Bold or Elegant. You can change it any time later.

**Numbers on your store.** Prices, quantities and dates always show Western digits (123), in Arabic and in English — for example `450 ج.م`. Order numbers and your dashboard use the same digits.

## 5. Add your first product

Enter a name, a price in EGP, the quantity in stock and optionally a photo. You can add the rest from the dashboard afterwards, or [import many products at once](/en/products-and-csv-import/).

## 6. Choose how customers pay

**Cash on delivery** is on by default. You can also switch on **InstaPay** here (see [InstaPay](/en/instapay/)); cards and wallets (Paymob, Kashier, FawryPay) connect with your own account in **Settings > Payments** (see [Payment providers](/en/payment-providers/)). Keep at least one method on. See [Orders and cash on delivery](/en/orders-and-cod/).

## 7. Go live

The last step shows a checklist of what you set up and publishes your store at your address. Then share the link with customers on WhatsApp. The **setup guide** on your dashboard keeps going: logo and colours, shipping zones, payment methods, your own domain, a test order and sharing your link.

## 8. Messages from your contact page

Your store's **Contact** page has a message form. Messages go to the email address you show on that page (Branding → Contact Info). If you leave it empty they go to your account email, so set it to the inbox you actually read. Reply to the email to answer the customer directly.

<Callout title="Limits">
  There is a limit on how many stores one account can create per day and in total. If you reach it
  you will see a message with what to do next.
</Callout>
