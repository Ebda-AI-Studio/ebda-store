## Hero text
The hero is the first block of your home page. Its title, subtitle and button text (in English and Arabic) and the button link are edited from **Settings > Hero Section**, which opens the visual editor. Hero images and slides are changed in the editor's layout.

## Header menu and footer links
Open the visual editor and choose the header or footer. Use a store path such as `/products` for your own pages, or a full `https://` link for outside pages. The menu holds up to 12 links.

## Ask the assistant
Say "change the hero title to ..." or "add an About link to the menu". The assistant previews the change and offers **Undo** for 24 hours. It cannot upload or change hero images.
