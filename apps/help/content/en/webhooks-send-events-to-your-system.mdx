A webhook sends a signed message to your own server every time something changes in your store, for example to feed an ERP or accounting tool.

## Set it up

1. Open **Settings**, then **For developers**, then **Webhooks**.
2. Press **Add endpoint**. Enter a public HTTPS address, an optional note, and tick the events you want: order placed, paid, shipped or delivered or cancelled, refunded, product added, changed or deleted, or stock is low.
3. Press **Save**. Copy the signing secret now. It is shown only once.
4. Press **Send test event** to check your server answers.

Customer name, email and phone are left out unless you turn on that option for the endpoint.

## Afterwards

- **Delivery log** lists recent sends with the result. **Resend** repeats one.
- **New secret** replaces the secret and the old one stops at once. Update your server first.
- **Turn off** or **Delete** stops the events.

## Good to know

- Webhooks are on the Growth and Business plans, and only the store owner can use them.
- Up to 10 endpoints per store. Local and private addresses are refused.
- If your server is down, each event is tried up to 6 times over about a day.
- After 10 failed events in a row the endpoint turns itself off.
- The log keeps the last 50 sends per endpoint, for 30 days.
