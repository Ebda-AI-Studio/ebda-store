Your store has a **Contact us** page (`/contact`) with a simple form. There is no message inbox inside the dashboard: every message is sent to you **by email** and is not stored in Ebda Store.

## What the customer sees

The customer fills **Name**, **Email Address**, **Subject** and **Message** and presses **Send Message**. The name is up to 100 characters, the subject up to 200 and the message up to 5000. Next to the form they see your email, phone and location from your store details.

## Where the message goes

It goes to the **Email** written in **Contact Info** (see [Store details, contact and social links](/en/store-details-contact-social/)). If there is no email there, it goes to your owner account email. An address from Ebda Store itself is ignored in either place.

Important: if neither has a usable email, the message **reaches nobody**, and the customer is still told it was sent. So always keep the email in your store details correct.

## Make sure it reaches you

1. Open **Settings**, then **Logo and colors** and the **Contact Info** tab.
2. Enter an email you actually read in **Email** and save with **Save branding**.
3. Send a test message from the **Contact us** page in your store, and check Spam if it does not arrive.

## Limits and protection

- There is a per-store limit of **20 messages per hour**. Messages after that are not delivered, and the customer still sees the normal success message.
- A demo store refuses messages.
- The form has spam protection, so automated messages are dropped without reaching you.
- If sending fails temporarily it is retried automatically up to 3 times.

## Common questions

**Where are my customers' messages?** In your store's email. There is no messages page in the dashboard.

**A customer says they wrote and it never came.** Check your contact email, your Spam folder, and that you did not hit 20 messages in an hour.
