## Block or reactivate a customer
Open the customer's page and change the account status. A **blocked** customer is signed out at once and cannot log in; set it back to active to allow them again. Only the store owner can do this.

## Erase a customer's personal data
When a customer asks you to delete their data, open their page and choose **Erase personal data**. Their name, phone, email and addresses are anonymised. Orders stay in your accounts without their details. This cannot be undone.

## Export a customer's data
From the same page you can download everything stored about the customer as a file.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can prepare this for you. It always shows a preview first, and you press the button yourself. Blocking and erasing always need your confirmation on a card that names the customer. Erasing cannot be undone; a block is reversed by asking to activate the account again.
