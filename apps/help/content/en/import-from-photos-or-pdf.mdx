## How it works

Open **Bulk tools** and choose **From photos or PDF**. Upload up to 4 files at a time (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC or PDF, 6 MB each, PDFs up to 10 pages). We read the products and prices and show them for you to check.

**Nothing goes live on its own.** You review the products, create drafts, and publish only the ones you checked. Drafts are hidden from shoppers until you publish them.

## Review what we read

Every product shows a label: **Clear**, **Check** or **Unsure**. Edit any field in place: names in Arabic and English, price, category, tags and options. Edits save when you leave the field.

- **Accept** the products that are right. **Accept all clear products** accepts the ones with no warnings.
- Some warnings need a tick before you can accept: a price that starts from, a price per kilo or unit, text that looks odd, or a product that looks like one you already have.
- **Open original** shows the file you uploaded, so you can compare.

Prices are read as Egyptian pounds. A price in another currency, a range such as "100 to 150", or no price at all is left empty for you to fill in.

## Create drafts and publish

1. Click **Create drafts for accepted**. Each accepted product becomes a draft. Stock starts at 0.
2. Open the **Drafts** tab, select the drafts that are ready and click **Publish selected**.
3. A draft without a price cannot be published. A draft without a photo can, but add photos first.

Search words, SEO text and missing translations are added to your drafts afterwards by the smart tools, within your daily limit.

## Limits and privacy

- Each plan includes a number of **pages per month**. The page count is shown on the upload tab. Basic 100, Growth 400, Business 1,500. Each page you retry that is read again counts again.
- Up to 30 pages per import, 60 pages per day, and 2 imports running at once.
- Phone numbers, emails and links in a photo are **not** copied into your products.
- Uploaded files are private to your store and deleted after 30 days.

## If something fails

A file that could not be read is listed with the reason and a **Try again** button. A file can be tried up to 3 times. If the daily limit is used up, the remaining files wait and you can retry them tomorrow.
