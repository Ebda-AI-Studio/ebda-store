Ebda Store makes QR codes you can print on a shop sign, a packing slip, a flyer or a product tag. A shopper points their phone camera at the code and lands on the right page.

## Make a code

1. In your store, open Settings, then QR codes & barcodes (the QR codes tab).
2. Choose what the code opens: your store, one product, one category, or your WhatsApp chat.
3. Pick the look: dot style, corner style and an optional frame with a line of text under the code (Arabic or English).
4. Download it as **PNG** for screens and social media, **SVG** for sign makers, or **PDF** for print. The PDF is 300 dpi at the size you choose (5, 10, 15 or 20 cm wide).

The preview updates as you change things, and it is the exact image you download.

## Colours and your logo

Pick any colours you like, with two rules that keep the code scannable: the code must be **darker than its background**, and the two colours need a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1. If the colours are too close, the page tells you why and switches off saving and downloading until you fix them.

You can place your store logo in the middle. The code then uses the strongest error correction (level H) and keeps a clear space around the logo. Add your logo in Branding first.

## Every download is checked

Before a file is created, Ebda Store reads the finished code back the way a phone would. If it cannot be read, the download stays off and the page says so. Test the printed code with a real phone before you print a big batch.

## Counting scans

The code holds a short link on your store address, for example `yourstore.ebda.app/q/p/ring`. When someone scans it, Ebda Store counts the scan and sends the shopper to the page with `utm_source=qr` added, so Google Analytics and your ad pixels can tell QR visits apart. The QR codes page lists scans per code for the last 30 days. Only a count per day is kept: no names, phone numbers or devices.

Because the link is short and goes through your store, a printed code keeps working if you rename a product, and the WhatsApp code always opens your current number.
