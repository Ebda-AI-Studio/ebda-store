Every public page of your store has one address per language: `yourshop.ebda.app/ar/products` in Arabic and `yourshop.ebda.app/en/products` in English. This applies to the home page, products, categories, blog, About, FAQ, Contact and the policy pages. Cart, checkout and your customer account keep their normal addresses.

## What happens to my old links

Nothing breaks. An old link such as `yourshop.ebda.app/products` sends the visitor to your store's **default language** version (the language set in your store settings), and Google follows that redirect and learns the new address. Links you already shared on WhatsApp or Instagram keep working.

## What visitors see

- A visitor on `/ar/...` sees the page in Arabic, with the layout right-to-left. On `/en/...` they see English.
- The language switcher moves them to the same page in the other language.
- Their choice is remembered, so the cart and checkout open in the same language.

## What Google sees

Each page tells Google about its twin in the other language, and your sitemap lists both. You do not need to do anything. If you use Google Search Console, you can ask it to re-read `yourshop.ebda.app/sitemap.xml` so it picks up the new addresses sooner.

## Changing your default language

Change it in your store settings. Old links then redirect to the new default language, and Google re-learns it over the following days.
