A 3D model lets a shopper turn the product around on its page, zoom in, see it change when they pick a colour or a metal, and on a phone place it in their room ("View in your space"). It is included in every plan.

## Prepare the file

- Format: **one `.glb` file** (binary glTF 2.0) with the textures inside it. `.gltf` with separate files, `.obj` and `.fbx` are not accepted.
- Size: up to **15 MB**. Smaller loads faster on mobile data; aim for under 5 MB.
- Compression: **meshopt** for the mesh and **WebP** for textures work. Draco and KTX2 are not supported yet.
- Give each part that changes colour its own **named material** in your 3D tool (for example `Band` and `Stone`). That name is what you pick when you map options.

If your file is too big or uses Draco, this one command fixes both:

```bash
npx @gltf-transform/cli optimize in.glb out.glb --compress meshopt --texture-compress webp
```

## Upload it

1. Open the product in your dashboard (Products, then the product).
2. In the **3D model** card, choose **Upload .glb model**.
3. Check the preview. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
4. Add a **poster**: either **Use current view as poster** or upload an image. Shoppers see it while the model loads.
5. Write a short description for screen readers in English and Arabic, then **Save**.

## Colours and metals

If the product has options (for example *Metal: Gold, White gold, Rose gold*), the card lists every option value. For each one:

1. Pick the **part of the model** it changes (a material, or a variant already inside the file).
2. Pick a **finish**: Gold, White gold, Rose gold, Silver, Platinum, or a custom colour.
3. Press **Preview** to see it, then **Save mapping**.

On your store the model changes as soon as the shopper taps the option, even before they choose a size. Options with a colour also get a small colour dot.

## AR ("View in your space")

- **iPhone / iPad**: works out of the box. The phone builds the AR file from your model with the finish the shopper picked. If you have your own `.usdz` file, upload it in the card and it is used instead.
- **Android**: opens Google's AR viewer with your model (it shows the file's default colours).
- Computers do not show the AR button. You can switch AR off for a product in the card.

## If the 3D model card says it is switched off

3D models are on by default for every store. If the card says they are switched off, Ebda Store staff turned them off for your store for a reason, for example a problem with your models. Press **Contact Ebda Store** in the card to ask why, or to ask us to turn them back on. You can still remove a model you already uploaded.
