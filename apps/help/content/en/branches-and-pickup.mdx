Branches appear on your store when you turn locations on.

## Add or change a branch
1. Open **Branding**, then **Locations**.
2. Add a branch with name, country and address, and optionally phone, WhatsApp, opening hours and a Google Maps link.
3. Use the published switch to show or hide it.

Deleting a branch removes it for good.

## Ask the assistant
Say "add a branch in Maadi" or "change the Maadi branch hours". Add and edit show a preview with Undo for 24 hours; deleting asks for confirmation and cannot be undone.
