The Ebda Store assistant answers your questions about your store in plain language and shows you the screen you need. Until it is switched to suggestion or action mode for your store it only answers and explains; it does not change anything.

## How to open it
1. Press the **Assistant** button in the side rail, or press `Ctrl` + `J` (`⌘` + `J` on a Mac).
2. Type your question in your own words, in Arabic, Franco-Arabic or English. For example: "How do I connect a domain?" or "Where is the shipping page?".
3. When the answer lives on a specific screen you get a **Show me where** link that takes you straight there.

## Where its answers come from
- The official help guides; it names the guide under each answer.
- Your store's data: products, orders, customers and figures. It reads them with exactly your permissions, so a team member without access to something cannot see it through the assistant.
- When there is no confirmed answer it says so plainly and offers to connect you with the support team.

## What it never does
- It never asks for a password, a verification code, a key or card details, and it does not accept them if you type them. You enter those on the secure screen only, and it shows you where.
- It never says it did something it did not do. A change only happens after you press the button on its preview.
- It cannot see other stores. It works on your store only.

## When it prepares a change
- Ask in your own words, for example "make the announcement bar say free shipping this week". The assistant prepares a **preview** with the old and the new value side by side. Nothing changes yet.
- In suggestion mode you see the preview only. In action mode you press **Apply change** and it is done with exactly your permissions: a team member can only do what that role may do.
- After it is applied you can press **Undo** for 24 hours. If you edited the same thing by hand in between, the assistant shows you the new situation instead of overwriting your edit.
- Prices, money, deleting, messages to customers, more than 20 items at once and anything customers see at checkout always wait for your explicit confirmation.
- It never overwrites text you wrote yourself unless you asked it to in that conversation.

## Privacy and limits
- Phone numbers, e-mails and card numbers are masked before any text is stored or sent to the model.
- Conversations are kept masked for 90 days and are deleted with the store if it is closed.
- The assistant is free up to 150 messages per store per day. When you reach the limit it tells you and you can continue the next day.
- If you turn off **AI features** in AI tools, the assistant turns off too.

## If you do not see the assistant
It is switched on store by store, in stages. If the button is missing, it is not on for your store yet; it appears by itself when it is.
