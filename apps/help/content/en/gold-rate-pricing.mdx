## Set the rates
1. Open **Gold rates**.
2. Type the price of one gram in EGP for each karat you sell (24, 21, 18) and save.
3. Every product priced by the gold rate is repriced immediately, so shoppers see the new price. A very large jump compared to the last rate asks you to confirm first.
4. The rate history is kept, so you can see who changed what and when.

The price of a gold-priced product is worked out from the rate, the weight and the making charge; you do not type it on the product.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can prepare this for you. It always shows a preview first, and you press the button yourself. Say 'set 21 karat to 4100 EGP per gram'. The card shows the old and new rate per karat and warns about a big jump; confirming changes what shoppers pay, and Undo puts the previous rate back.
