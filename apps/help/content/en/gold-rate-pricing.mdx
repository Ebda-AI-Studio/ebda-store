If you sell gold or jewellery you can price each piece from its weight, its making charge and today's gold rate per gram, so the price updates by itself when the gold rate changes. The page is in **Catalog** under the **Gold rates** tab.

## Who can do what

The store owner changes rates, settings, automatic-rate decisions and the payment policy. Team members can open the page and view it, and set a piece's pricing from the product page.

## Turn gold pricing on

1. Open **Catalog**, then **Gold rates**.
2. In **Settings** switch on **Price products by gold** and save with **Save settings**.
3. Under **Rate per gram** type today's rate for each karat (**24 karat**, **21 karat** and **18 karat**) in EGP per gram, with up to two decimals. **Fill 21 and 18 from 24** works the other two out from the 24 karat rate. Then **Save rates**.

Every product priced by gold updates the moment you save the rate. If the new rate is far from the last one you see **This rate is far from the last one** with the numbers: check them and press **Apply the rate** or **Go back**.

## Automatic rate

Switch on **Automatic rate** in **Settings** and the rate is fetched a few times a day. A big jump waits for your confirmation: **A new automatic rate is waiting for you** shows and you press **Confirm** or **Reject**. If the two automatic rate sources disagree you pick **Apply first source**, **Apply second source**, **Enter my own rate** or **Keep current rate**. If you type your own rate while a disagreement is open, that counts as your decision.

## Other settings

- **Rate premium (%)**: between -10 and 10, added to the automatic rate.
- **Rate expires after (hours)**: from 1 to 26. After that gold products **cannot be bought** until you update the rate.
- **Round prices to**: 1 piastre, 1 pound, 5 pounds, 10 pounds.
- **Show the breakdown to shoppers**: shows the gold value and the making charge on the product page.

## Price a piece

Open the product and the **Gold pricing** card, and switch on **Price this product by gold**. Choose the **Karat**, type the **Weight (grams)**, the **Making charge** (**Per gram** or **Fixed per piece**) and the **Margin (%)**, and press **Save gold pricing**. The current price is worked out and cannot be typed. If variants weigh differently enter that under **Variants**. To switch many products, in the product list select them, open **Mass Edit**, then **Price by gold** or **Fixed price**.

A product inside a bundle, or with quantity price breaks, cannot be gold-priced. It also needs a current rate for that karat.

## Payment

Orders with gold are paid in full online. Cash on delivery and installments are hidden when the order has gold. Under **Payment for gold** you can allow them: tick **I understand and I confirm.** then **Save**, and the choice is your responsibility.

## Common questions

**Gold products stopped selling.** The rate is out of date or missing. Add a new rate under **Rate per gram**.

**Does the price update by itself?** Yes if you turned on **Automatic rate**, otherwise you type it. Every rate is kept in **Rate history** (automatic or manual, applied, waiting or rejected).
