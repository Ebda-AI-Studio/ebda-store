When a shopper types their email in the newsletter box in your store footer, they become a subscriber. This page shows you the subscribers, lets you download the list and lets you send them a quick message. This list is the same audience your [email campaigns](/en/email-campaigns/) reach. The store owner opens it.

## See your subscribers

From **Marketing and offers** open the **Newsletter** tab. The page is called **Newsletter Subscribers** and it shows the subscriber count and a table with: **Email**, **Language**, **Source**, **Status** (**Active** or **Unsubscribed**) and **Joined**.

If there are many subscribers, the page shows the first batch and tells you "Showing the first N of N subscribers. Export to download the full list."

If there are none you see **No subscribers yet**.

## Download the list

Select **Export CSV** or **Export XLSX**. Both files have the same columns. An export covers up to 50,000 rows.

## Send a quick message

1. Select **Send Newsletter**.
2. Enter the **Subject line** and the **Body (HTML)**.
3. Select **Preview email** to see the look. The content is placed inside your store's email template automatically.
4. Select **Send to all subscribers**.

The message goes **to active subscribers only**, and every message has an unsubscribe link. If the content has scripts, embedded frames or `javascript:` links, the page warns you and removes them before sending. After sending you see **Sent to N of N subscriber(s)**.

A quick send is limited to **400 active subscribers** at a time. If you have more, use [Email campaigns](/en/email-campaigns/).

## Plan

Viewing subscribers and downloading the list need no particular plan. **Send Newsletter** needs the **Growth** plan or higher (the same requirement as campaigns). If your plan does not include it you see an upgrade message. See [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/).

## Common questions

**Who subscribed to the newsletter?** Open the tab. The table shows each subscriber, where they came from (**Source**) and when.

**I want to download my subscribers.** **Export CSV** or **Export XLSX**.

**Why did fewer people get the email than the total?** A send goes to active subscribers only. Anyone who unsubscribed shows **Unsubscribed** and does not receive it.
