When your store is suspended, a banner on every dashboard page tells you why.

1. Read the reason in the banner.
2. Press **Appeal this decision**, explain what happened, and press **Send**.
3. A person at Ebda Store reviews it and replies by email. If they agree, they switch your store back on and the appeal closes.

You can have one appeal open at a time. If the suspension is about billing, the banner says so: settle the invoice instead, or use **Contact Ebda Store**.
