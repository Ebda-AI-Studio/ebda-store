After you create your store ([Create your store](/en/create-your-store/)), the dashboard shows a **Setup guide** card with 7 steps. The card tells you what is still missing to win your first order. The store owner sees it.

## The card

The card says **Finish these steps to get your first order.** It shows a progress bar and **N of N done**. Each step gets a tick when it is finished. The steps are:

| Step | Counts as done when |
| --- | --- |
| **Add a product** | there is at least one product in the store |
| **Set your logo and colors** | you add a logo, pick a look, or set a main colour |
| **Set shipping zones** | you add a shipping zone or an area shipping rate |
| **Choose payment methods** | you turn on a payment method other than cash on delivery, or select **Mark as done** |
| **Connect your own domain** (**Optional**) | you connect a domain, or select **Mark as done** |
| **Place a test order** | there is any order in the store |
| **Share your store link** | you select **Open** and share the link on WhatsApp |

Optional steps do not count toward the required progress.

## Finish each step

- Select **Open** next to the step, and you go to the right screen: [Products](/en/products-and-csv-import/), Branding, [Shipping](/en/shipping/), payment settings ([Payment providers](/en/payment-providers/)), or the [Domain](/en/custom-domain/).
- **Place a test order**: **Open** opens your store, so order a product as if you were a customer. The step completes with any order in the store.
- **Share your store link**: **Open** opens WhatsApp with a ready message holding the link, and the step completes as soon as you select it.
- Cash on delivery is on from day one, so the payment step does not complete with it alone. If cash is all you need, select **Mark as done**.

## Steps that complete themselves and ones you tick

Most steps complete **from your store's real data** and cannot be ticked by hand, so the list never claims you have a product when you do not. The ones you can tick by hand are **Payment** and **Domain**, with the **Mark as done** button, and you take it back with **Undo**. The **Share your store link** step is ticked when you open the share.

## The shipping warning

If you have not set delivery rates yet, a **Delivery rates are not set yet** notice shows above the card: customers will not see a delivery fee at checkout. Select **Add delivery rates** before you take orders.

## Hide the card or bring it back

Select **Hide guide** to hide it, and it stays hidden. When you want it back select **Show the setup guide**.

## If the card does not load

If you see **We could not load the setup guide.** refresh the page.

## Common questions

**What is left before my store is ready?** Open the dashboard, and the steps without a tick are what is missing.

**Why can't I tick the product step by hand?** Because it completes when a product actually exists. Add a product and it completes.

**Hide the setup checklist?** **Hide guide**, and bring it back with **Show the setup guide**.
