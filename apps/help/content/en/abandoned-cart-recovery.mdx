Open **Abandoned Cart Recovery** in your store menu. The settings panel controls the reminders.

## What you can set

- Up to three reminder steps, each on or off, with its own delay in minutes.
- The customer e-mail, on or off, and how long after the cart is left it goes out (60 to 1440 minutes).
- An automatic coupon: on or off, its percentage, an optional EGP cap and how many hours it stays valid.

Turning a reminder or the customer e-mail **on** means the store sends e-mails to shoppers who left a cart, on that schedule, only to those who agreed to receive them.

## Ask the assistant

Two kinds of request, handled differently:

- **The automatic coupon** ("give a 10% coupon with the reminder"): the assistant shows the settings before and after, and because a coupon is a discount it asks you to confirm.
- **Reminders and the customer e-mail** ("turn on the cart e-mail after 2 hours"): turning these on makes the store e-mail shoppers who left a cart, automatically. The assistant shows exactly what will switch on and when, and always asks you to confirm first. E-mails already sent cannot be recalled.

Confirming is valid for 15 minutes. You can press **Undo** for 24 hours to put the settings back.
