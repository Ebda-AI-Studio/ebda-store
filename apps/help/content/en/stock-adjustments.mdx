Any manual change to stock (a count, damaged goods, goods you found) is recorded as a **stock adjustment** with a number, a type and a reason. When you approve it, quantities really change and an entry is posted to your accounts.

## Who can do this

Only the store owner.

## Plan

Bulk stock adjustments are on the **Business** plan (see [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/)). If your plan does not include the feature, the request is refused with `FEATURE_DISABLED` and names the plan you need. The **Quick Adjust** link from Inventory opens this same screen.

## Make a manual adjustment

1. In **Stock**, open **Adjustments** and press **New stock adjustment**.
2. Pick the **Adjustment type**:
   - **Cycle Count**: sets stock to the quantity you actually counted.
   - **Damage**: units damaged and no longer sellable.
   - **Theft**: stock lost to theft.
   - **Loss**: stock lost or misplaced for no clear reason.
   - **Write-off**: deliberately removed (expired or obsolete).
   - **Found Stock**: missing stock located, so the quantity goes up.
   - **Return to Supplier**: sent back to the supplier, so the quantity goes down.
3. Write the **Reason** in English and Arabic (optional), and optionally an **Evidence URL** (a link to a photo or file that proves the reason).
4. Press **+ Add product**, choose the product and type the **New quantity** (the final quantity after the change, not the difference). You need at least one product.
5. Choose one:
   - **Save as draft**: saved in the adjustments list as a draft, and stock does not change.
   - **Approve adjustment**: asks you to confirm, then stock changes and the entry is posted to your accounts.

## Import adjustments from a CSV

For hundreds of products, use the **CSV Import** tab on the same page:

1. Press **Download template (.csv)** and fill it in.
2. Each row needs a **SKU** or **Barcode**, plus **New Quantity** (the actual count) or **Delta** (positive or negative). Reason is optional. Arabic column headers are also accepted.
3. Choose the adjustment type and upload the file (drag it in or click to choose).
4. Review the preview: you see the first 50 rows, how many rows are valid and how many have errors. Rows with errors are skipped, with the reason for each (**Show row errors**).
5. Press **Import & Post (N rows)**. The import approves the adjustment straight away, with no draft step. You see the adjustment number and how many rows were imported and skipped.

<Callout title="Approval cannot be undone">
  Once approved, an adjustment is never edited again and it posts an accounting entry. If you made a mistake, create a new adjustment that reverses it. If you want to review first, save the manual adjustment as a draft before approving.
</Callout>

## The adjustments list

The table shows the number, type, status (draft or posted), date and value impact in EGP, and it has search. A manual draft appears here, but the current screen has no button to approve a draft after saving it, so use **Approve adjustment** if you want the change applied.

## Common questions

**How do I adjust stock from a CSV?** Use **CSV Import** with the **Cycle Count** type and a **New Quantity** column.

**Only one product's quantity is wrong?** In the **Inventory** tab press **Quick Adjust** next to the product. It opens a new adjustment with that product selected.
