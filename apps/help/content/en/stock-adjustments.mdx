A stock adjustment is a document that explains why your stock changed, so the numbers and your accounts agree. It is part of the Business plan.

## Make an adjustment
1. Open **Stock** then **Adjustments** and press **New adjustment**.
2. Pick the reason: cycle count, damage, theft, loss, write-off, found stock or return to supplier.
3. Add the products and type the counted total of each. The screen shows the difference and what it does to your inventory cost.
4. **Post** it. Stock and the accounts update. A posted adjustment cannot be edited; reverse it with another adjustment.

You can also adjust many products at once from a CSV file on the same page.

## Counting with a scanner
On **Stock** then **Scan** open a count session, scan or type each product with the quantity you counted, then close and reconcile it. The differences are posted as one count adjustment.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can record an adjustment or a count for you from the numbers you give it. It shows the quantities and the cost impact first, and a count can be undone by counting the old numbers back.
