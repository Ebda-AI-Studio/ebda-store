Three small things that make a product page easier to find and to buy from.

## Tags
Tags are the filter chips shoppers see on the store. You can rename a tag or give it an Arabic label on the **Tags** page. To put tags on products, ask the assistant ("add the tag Sale to these 25 products") or use the **tags** column when you import a CSV.

## Questions and answers (FAQs)
1. Open the product and go to its FAQs section.
2. Write the question and the answer. The Arabic versions are optional.
3. Save. The question shows on the product page right away.

## Variants (size, color)
1. Open the product and go to **Variants**.
2. Pick the variant, then change its price, compare-at price, SKU, or switch it off so shoppers cannot buy it.
3. Leave the price empty to use the product price. Stock is changed from the stock screens, not here.

## Ask the assistant
Tell the assistant what you want, for example "add a question about delivery time to this product" or "make the large size 350 EGP". It shows a preview of exactly what will change and waits for you. After you apply it you have an **Undo** button for 24 hours. A price change always asks for an extra confirmation, and so does a change to more than 20 products.
