Set how customers earn points and give points to customers.

## Steps
1. Open **Loyalty** from the Marketing group.
2. Turn the program on and set the points per EGP and the bonuses.
3. Open a member to give or take back points.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can change the settings (Undo restores them) and give points to customers. Points have value, so giving them needs your approval, and more than 20 customers is flagged as a bulk change. The assistant previews the change and offers Undo for 24 hours where the change can be undone.
