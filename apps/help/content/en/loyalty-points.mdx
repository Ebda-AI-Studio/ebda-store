The loyalty program gives your shopper points on every order they receive, and they can redeem the points for a discount on a later order. The feature is available from the **Growth** plan upward, and the store owner sets it up.

## Turn the program on

1. From **Marketing and offers** open the **Loyalty** tab. The page is called **Loyalty Program**.
2. Next to **Program Status** there is a button that shows the current state (**Enabled** or **Disabled**). Select it to switch the program to **Enabled**.
3. If you turn it off a confirmation window appears: customers cannot earn or redeem points until you turn it on again.

## Set the rules

From the **Settings** tab:

1. **Point Earning**: **Points per 1 EGP Spent** (1 to 100), **Signup Bonus**, **Referral Bonus**, **Review Bonus** and **Birthday Bonus**.
2. **Membership Tiers**: **Bronze**, **Silver**, **Gold** and **Platinum**, each with a **Min Points** and a **Multiplier** (it increases the points the shopper earns).
3. **Redemption**: **Point Value (EGP)** (the page tells you "1 point = X EGP"), **Min Points to Redeem**, **Max % of Order**, and **Expiration (months)** (0 means points never expire).
4. Select **Save Settings**.

## When points are earned and spent

- Points are earned when the customer's order reaches **Delivered**, not when they place it.
- A shopper redeems points while paying, and only if they are signed in (a guest cannot redeem). The discount is taken from what is left after any coupon or promotion, and within **Max % of Order**.

## Members and points

The numbers on top: **Total Members**, **Points Issued**, **Points Redeemed**, **Outstanding** and **Tier Breakdown**.

From the **Members** tab you see **Member**, **Tier**, **Points Balance**, **Total Earned** and **Total Redeemed**. To award or deduct points by hand:

1. Select **Award Points** on the member's row.
2. Enter the **Points**: a positive number to award, or a **negative** one to deduct. The limit is 100,000 points per action.
3. Enter the **Reason** (optional) and select **Award Points**.

Under **Transactions** you find every movement: **Earn** (purchase, sign-up, referral, review, birthday, manual), **Redeem**, **Expired**, **Adjustment** and **Tier Changed**, and you can filter by type, date and customer ID.

## Plan

The feature is on the **Growth** plan. On a lower plan the page shows a locked upgrade card. See [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/).

## Common questions

**How do I set up loyalty points?** Turn the program on and set **Points per 1 EGP** and the **Point Value**.

**Give a customer points.** From **Members** select **Award Points** on that customer.

**When do points expire?** By **Expiration (months)** in Settings. Zero means they never expire.
