The navigation menu and footer links are edited in the **visual editor**. The old "Navigation" page was retired, and its old link takes you to the editor. Only the store owner can edit them.

## Header menu

1. Open **Store look** from the side menu.
2. In the **Sections** tab choose **Header**, then open **Content**.
3. Under **Menu links** each link has **Name (Arabic)**, **Name (English)** and **Link**.
4. Use **Add a link** to add one, **Move up** and **Move down** to reorder, and **Remove** to delete. The maximum is 12 links.
5. Press **Publish**.

If your store has not changed the menu yet you will see the default links: Products, Categories, About and Contact.

## Top bar

In the same place **Show the top bar** turns it on or off, and **Top bar links** holds up to 8 links.

## Footer links

Choose **Footer** and open **Content**. There are three columns: **Shop column**, **Support column** and **Company column**, each with up to 12 links. **Show newsletter signup** shows or hides the sign-up box in the footer.

## Link format

A link must be a path like `/about`, or a link starting with `https://`, `mailto:` or `tel:`. Anything else shows "Use a path like /about…" under the field and the link is not saved to the draft.

The logo is changed from Branding, and a **Branches** link is one you add here yourself after you turn branches on (see [Branches and pickup locations](/en/store-branches/)).

## Common questions

**I linked a page and it shows "not found".** Usually the page has **Show on storefront** switched off. Open it from [Store pages and policies](/en/store-pages-and-policies/).

**The change did not show.** Links are saved in the draft and customers see them when you press **Publish**. You can go back with **Undo** or from **History**.

**The current links did not load.** The editor says it could not load the current links. Refresh the page and try again.
