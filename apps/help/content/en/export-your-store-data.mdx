Only the owner of a store can export it. Open **Settings** and press **Download my data**. The file includes your customers' names, phones and emails, so the download needs a fresh confirmation, and we email you every time an export is made. If that was not you, change your password and contact support.

## What you get

One zip with a file per table (products, categories, orders with their items, customers, coupons, reviews, returns and expenses) in NDJSON, one record per line, plus a `manifest.json` that lists the tables and row counts. API keys and payment provider credentials are never included.

## Limits

You can export once an hour for each store. If your store has more than 20000 rows in total, the export is refused with a clear message; export orders, products and customers from their own pages instead.

## Close your account

Closing your own account is in your account settings on the web app, and it is refused while you own a store with a live subscription or money in flight. To delete a store, see **Delete your store**.
