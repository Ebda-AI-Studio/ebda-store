Link your store to Ebda Chat and withdraw a customer's WhatsApp marketing consent.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings** and find the Ebda Chat card at the top.
2. Choose **Connect Ebda Chat**. This only creates the workspace: no charge, no plan change, no message is sent. Connect the store (needs your plan to include it and your two-step sign-in).
3. To stop marketing messages to one customer, withdraw their consent by phone number. Only the customer can grant it again.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can tell you if WhatsApp is linked and withdraw a customer's consent (with your approval). Connecting is done on the screen. The assistant previews the change and offers Undo for 24 hours where the change can be undone.
