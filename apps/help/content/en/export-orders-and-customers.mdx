You can download your orders or your customers as an Excel (XLSX) file to open on your computer, analyse, or share with your accountant.

## Who can do this

The store owner only. These files contain customer names, phone numbers and addresses, so staff accounts do not see the export button.

## Export orders

1. Open **Orders**.
2. Choose the period and the status, or type a search, to narrow the orders.
3. From the **More order options** menu press **Export orders**.
4. Wait for "Exporting..." and the file downloads.

The file follows the filters on screen: period, status and search. It has two sheets: **Orders**, one row per order with the newest first, and **Line Items**, one row per product in an order.

The limit is 20,000 orders per file. If you have more, a note row says the file was cut and it holds only the most recent orders.

## Export customers

1. Open **Customers**.
2. To narrow the list use the search or the segment filter.
3. From the **More customer options** menu press **Export Excel**.

The file follows the same search and segment as the screen, and has the sheets **Customers**, **Purchases** and **Summary**. Customers are built from order history. If there are very many orders a note row says the file was cut.

## If the export fails

You see "Export failed" for orders or "Failed to export customers". Try again in a little while. The export is rate-limited, so do not press the button several times in a row.

<Callout title="Sensitive data">
  The file holds customers' personal data. Keep it somewhere safe and share it only with people who need it.
</Callout>
