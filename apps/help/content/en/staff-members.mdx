You can add staff to work with you on the store without giving them your own account. A staff member manages orders, products and inventory, but cannot change store settings, payment configuration or financial records. The store owner adds and removes them.

## Where to find it

From **Settings** open the **Team** card (Add staff with limited permissions). The page is called **Staff Members**.

## Add a staff member

1. Select **Add Staff Member**.
2. Enter the **Email**, **First name** and **Last name**.
3. Select **Add Staff Member**.

One of two things happens next:

- If the email has no account, the person is **added straight away**, you see **Staff member added**, and they can sign in.
- If the email already has an account, an **invitation** is sent: you see **Invitation sent**, and they get an email to accept. Nothing changes on their account until they accept. The invitation is valid for **72 hours**.

Invitations that have not been accepted show under **Pending invitations** with their expiry. To cancel one select **Revoke** and confirm, and the link stops working at once. If an invitation expired, invite them again with the same email, and the new invitation revokes the old one.

## What staff can and cannot do

- They can manage **orders, products and inventory**.
- They cannot change **store settings**, **payment configuration** or **financial records**. The Finance and Settings sections are hidden from them.
- There is **one fixed staff role**. There are no custom permissions, so you cannot set something different for each staff member.

## The team list

The table shows **Member**, **Status**, **Added** and **Last login**. If there is nobody you see **No staff members yet.**

To remove a staff member select **Remove** on their row and confirm. They lose access immediately.

## The limit by plan

The number of staff is limited by your plan:

| Plan | Limit |
| --- | ---: |
| Basic | 2 |
| Growth | 5 |
| Business | 15 |

If you reach the limit and try to add someone, adding stops with an upgrade message. See [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/).

## Important conditions

- Two-step verification must be on for your account to add or remove staff. See [Account security](/en/account-security/).
- An account that owns a store, a customer account or a platform admin cannot be added as staff.
- An invited person must verify their email first, and sign in with the invited email to accept.

## If something is not working

- **This invitation link is invalid or has expired:** send a new invitation.
- **This store has reached its staff limit:** remove a staff member or upgrade the plan.
- **A staff member cannot see profits:** that is expected. Finance and Settings are hidden from staff.

## Common questions

**How do I add a staff member?** **Add Staff Member**, enter the email and name.

**I want a staff member to see profits or change settings.** That is not available right now. A staff member has one fixed role.

**Remove a staff member who left.** **Remove** on their row, and they lose access at once.
