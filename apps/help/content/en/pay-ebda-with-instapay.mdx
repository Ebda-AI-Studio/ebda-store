You can pay your Ebda Store subscription by InstaPay. This works whether you are trialing, active, overdue, or your subscription has ended. Upgrade, downgrade, cancel and resume all work with InstaPay.

## Steps

1. Open **Billing** for your store and pick a plan (monthly or yearly). The new plan applies when your payment is confirmed.
2. Send the transfer to Ebda Store's InstaPay address, with the payment reference shown on the page.
3. Take a screenshot of the receipt in your banking app.
4. Upload it on the Billing page. The AI reads it and fills in the details.
5. Check and correct the details:
   - amount
   - reference number (12 digits)
   - date and time
   - sender name, exactly as shown (keep any `*`)
   - sender InstaPay address or mobile
   - bank
6. Submit.

**If the receipt matches, your plan is confirmed within minutes. Otherwise a person at Ebda AI Studio reviews it.** A receipt matches when the amount is exactly the amount shown, the money went to Ebda Store's InstaPay address, the transfer is less than 48 hours old, and the receipt was never used before. Anything else waits for a person, and nothing activates until it is confirmed. You get a notification when it is confirmed, or rejected with the reason (then upload the right receipt). You also get one when a paused store comes back.

## If the money did not arrive

If the transfer does not show up on our side, the payment is marked **not received**. Your plan goes back to unpaid and you have **48 hours** to upload a correct receipt or pay by card before paid features pause. Your products, orders and settings are never deleted.

## First payment limit

The first automatic confirmation covers **one month** of the plan you chose. A bigger first payment, such as a yearly one, waits for a person. After one clean payment there is no such limit.

## Good to know

- **Paying early does not lose days.** The new period starts when your current period (or trial) ends.
- **A transfer reference can be used only once.**
- **Several stores, one paused?** If your plan allows fewer stores than you have, the extra store is paused. Upgrade your **main (oldest) store**. Paying for the paused store does not bring it back.
- **Subscription ended?** The store does not take new orders until you choose a plan and pay. Your data is kept.

## Refunds

Ebda Store refunds by InstaPay, outside the app, and records it on your billing history.
