A campaign is a marketing email you send once to your newsletter subscribers, like an Eid offer or a new product. You write it in Arabic and English and test it on yourself before sending. The feature is available from the **Growth** plan upward, and the store owner sends it.

## Who receives it

A campaign goes **only to newsletter subscribers who opted in**. There is no other audience source. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and anyone who unsubscribes gets nothing after that. Before sending, the page tells you **N people will receive this campaign**. If nobody has opted in, nothing is sent. See [Newsletter](/en/newsletter/).

## Create and send a campaign

1. From **Marketing and offers** open the **Campaigns** tab.
2. Select **New campaign**. A draft is created and the editor opens.
3. Enter a **Campaign name** (for your own use).
4. Write the message in **English** and **Arabic**: the **Subject** and the **Body (HTML)**. You must write a subject and body in at least one language. Select **Preview email** to see it.
5. If you want help, select **AI draft**, describe the email in **Describe the email you want** (for example "Announce our Ramadan offer with 20% off"), and select **Generate**. Review and edit the result, because it is only a draft.
6. Choose the **Language sent to each subscriber**: **Each subscriber's own language** (it falls back to the other language if theirs is empty), **Always English**, or **Always Arabic**.
7. Select **Send test to me** to get a copy at your account email and check the look. A test send does not count toward the sending limit.
8. Under **Send** choose:
   - **Send now**: a confirmation window shows the number of people.
   - **Schedule**: pick a time in the future, within 30 days.
9. Confirm with **Send**. After you confirm there is no undo, but you can cancel before it finishes (see below).

## The daily sending limit

To protect sender reputation there is a limit on messages in the last 24 hours: **1000** on the Growth plan and **5000** on the Business plan. The page shows **N of N more can be sent in the next 24 hours**. If a campaign would go over the limit you see a message, and you should schedule it for another day or reduce the audience.

## After sending

Open the campaign from the list. The list shows the **Status** (**Draft**, **Scheduled**, **Sending**, **Sent**, **Cancelled**, **Failed**) and the **Recipients**, **Sent** and **Failed** counts. On the campaign page:

- **Recipients** with each one's status: **Queued**, **Sent**, **Failed**, **Bounced**, **Unsubscribed**, and you can filter by status.
- **Cancel campaign** stops it before it starts, or before the next batch if it is being sent. It cannot be resumed after cancelling, you have to send it again.

## Editing and deleting

- Only a **draft** can be edited. A campaign that was sent can no longer be edited.
- **Delete draft** works on a draft. A cancelled or failed campaign can be deleted too. One that already sent mail is kept as send history and cannot be deleted.

## If something is not working

- If your plan does not include campaigns: the page is locked behind an upgrade card with **This feature isn't enabled for your store**. See [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/).
- **Nobody to send to:** **No subscribers have opted in yet**. Only people who sign up from the store footer count.
- **Could not queue the campaign:** nothing was sent. Try again.
- **Send is disabled:** add a subject and body in at least one language.

## Common questions

**I want to send an Eid offer to all my subscribers.** Make a campaign, write the message, test it on yourself, then **Send now** or **Schedule**.

**How is it different from "Send Newsletter"?** The [Newsletter](/en/newsletter/) page has a quick send for up to 400 subscribers. Campaigns are for larger lists and have scheduling and recipient tracking.
