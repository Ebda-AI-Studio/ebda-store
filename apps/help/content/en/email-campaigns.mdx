Write an email campaign, send yourself a test, then send it to every subscriber who opted in.

## Steps
1. Open **Email campaigns** from the Marketing group.
2. Create a campaign: name, subject and body (add Arabic too if you want).
3. Send a test to your own email first.
4. Press **Send** (now or scheduled). The audience size is shown before you confirm; an unsubscribe link is added automatically.
5. Sent email cannot be recalled. A scheduled campaign can be cancelled before it starts.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can write the text for you and send it to your subscribers. The card shows the audience size and the exact text, and sending needs your approval; it cannot be undone. The assistant previews the change and offers Undo for 24 hours where the change can be undone.
