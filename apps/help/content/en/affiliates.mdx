The affiliate program lets partners or influencers recommend your store with a referral code of their own, and earn a share of the orders that come through them. The feature is on the **Business** plan, and the store owner manages it.

## Where to find it

From **Marketing and offers** open **Affiliates**. The page is called **Affiliate Program**, and it has two tabs: **Affiliates** and **Configuration**.

## Set up the program

From the **Configuration** tab:

1. **Commission & Payouts**: **Default Commission Rate (%)**, **Cookie Duration (days)** (1 to 365), and **Min Payout Amount (EGP)** (default 500 EGP).
2. **Program Options**: **Auto-approve Applications** approves any new partner without your review, and **Public Signup** lets partners sign up themselves through the public page.
3. Select **Save Configuration**.

## Add a partner

1. From the **Affiliates** tab select **Invite Affiliate**.
2. Enter the **Full Name** and **Email**, and optionally **Phone**, **Instagram**, **TikTok** and **Internal Notes** (nobody outside sees these).
3. Select **Add Affiliate**. They are added as **Approved** straight away and get a referral code they can share immediately.

If the email is already registered as a partner, adding fails.

## Approve, reject or suspend

Each partner has a status: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Suspended**. You can filter by it and search by name. The buttons in a partner's row are:

- When **Pending**: **Approve** or **Reject**.
- When **Approved**: **Payout** and **Suspend**.
- **View events** is always there, and opens the partner's history (creation, approval, commissions and payouts).

Approve and reject work on **Pending** partners only.

## How commission is calculated

When an order that came through a partner's link reaches **Delivered**, the commission is recorded and added to the partner's **Pending Balance** (at their own rate if one is set, otherwise the default rate). If the order is refunded or fully cancelled, the commission is taken back off their balance. The top of the page shows **Total Affiliates**, **Active Referrals**, **Conversion Rate** and **Total Paid**.

## Record a payout to a partner

1. On the row of an **Approved** partner with a pending balance, select **Payout**. The button is disabled when their balance is zero.
2. In **Create Payout** enter the **Amount (EGP)** (no more than the **Available balance**), choose the **Method**: **Bank Transfer**, **InstaPay**, **Mobile wallet**, **Cash** or **Manual**, and optional notes.
3. Select **Confirm Payout**.

**Important:** Ebda Store records the payout and takes its amount off the partner's balance, but it **does not move the money**. You have to make the actual transfer yourself by the method you chose, and a payout cannot be undone from the screen. Check the amount before you confirm.

## Plan and who can use it

The feature is on the **Business** plan. On a lower plan the page shows a locked upgrade card. See [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/).

## Common questions

**How do I set up a commission system for partners?** Turn on **Public Signup** or add partners yourself, set the **Default Commission Rate**, and approve applications.

**Why is there no commission on an order?** A commission is recorded only when the order is **Delivered**, and it must have come through an **Approved** partner's link within the **Cookie Duration**.

**A partner is not working out.** Select **Suspend**. A partner who has earnings or payouts is never deleted, only suspended.
