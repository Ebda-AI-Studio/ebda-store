Customers write reviews from the product page, and you decide what shows in your store. From the Reviews page you approve or unapprove a review, delete it, and reply to it.

## Who can write a review

Only a customer with a **delivered** order that contains the product, and once per product. A review is 1 to 5 stars and the text must be at least 20 characters. That is why every review shows **Verified Purchase**.

## Review the reviews

1. From your store, open **Reviews**.
2. Use the filters: **All Reviews**, **Pending Approval**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Spam**. There is also a **Search reviews…** box.
3. On a new review press **Approve**. It is then visible to customers.
4. To hide a review that is live, press **Unapprove**.
5. **Delete** removes the review for good after a confirmation. It cannot be undone.

After a customer submits a review, the system checks it automatically. If it sees spam signals it moves the review to **Spam** and shows the **Spam signals** under it. It also translates the review, and you can press **Show original** or **Show translation** to switch. The system does not detect a fake review from a real buyer who truly purchased, so unapprove or delete what you do not trust.

## Reply to a review

1. Press **Draft AI response** on the review.
2. You get an English reply and an Arabic reply. Read and edit both.
3. Press **Save response**.

The reply is published only when you press save, and customers see it under **Owner response**. Read it carefully before saving, especially for a negative review.

## Summarise a product's reviews

Press **Summarize reviews** on any review to see the **AI Review Summary**: **Top positives**, **Top negatives** and **Overall sentiment**, based on that product's verified reviews.

## Who can do this

The store owner and team members. Reviews are available on every plan. If an AI request fails you see "AI request failed. Please try again."; try again. See [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/) for your plan.

## Common questions

**Why do I see no reviews?**
Reviews appear after a customer writes one, and the customer must have received the order first.

**A customer says their review is not showing.**
It may be under **Pending Approval** or **Spam**. Open that filter and press **Approve**.
