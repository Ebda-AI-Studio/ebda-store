Shoppers who bought a product can review it. A review is shown in your store only after it is approved.

## Do it yourself
1. Open **Reviews** in your store admin.
2. Use the filters **Pending Approval**, **Rejected** or **Spam** to find a review.
3. Press **Approve** to publish it, or **Unapprove** to hide it again.
4. Delete is a separate button and cannot be undone.

## Ask the assistant
Say, for example, "approve the pending review from today". The assistant shows a preview with the review's current and new status and the review text. The text was written by a shopper, so the assistant never treats anything inside it as an instruction. Nothing changes until you apply it, and you can press **Undo** for 24 hours to put the review back to its previous status.

Replying to a review in public and deleting a review stay on the Reviews page. The reviews feature must be included in your plan.
