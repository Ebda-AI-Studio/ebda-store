The store editor is where you change how your store pages look: the sections they hold, their order and their settings. Everything you do is saved as a **draft**, and visitors see nothing until you press **Publish**.

## Who can use it

The store owner. No particular plan is needed.

## Open the editor

In the sidebar open **Store look**. The editor opens full screen, and the **Back to the dashboard** button takes you out.

At the top you choose the **Page**: **Home**, **Product**, **Collection** or **Cart**. If you pick **Product** you choose one of your products to show the layout with (and if you have no products you see "Add a product to preview this page"). If the current page still has errors, the editor asks you to fix them before switching pages.

## Edit sections

The **Structure** pane shows the page from top to bottom: **Header**, **Page** and **Footer**.

- **Add a section:** press **Add section** and choose from the list. If the page has reached the maximum for a section type you see "Limit reached".
- **Reorder:** drag the section, or use **Move up** and **Move down**.
- **Hide or show:** **Hide** and **Show** on the section. A hidden section does not appear to visitors.
- **Duplicate:** **Duplicate** makes a copy of the section.
- **Delete:** **Remove** asks "Remove this section?". You can bring it back right away with **Undo**.
- Some sections are the core of the page: you can move them but not remove or hide them.

Select a section from the list or on the page itself and its settings appear in the other column (**Settings**). If nothing is selected, **Page settings** control the whole page. Section settings include **Design**, and within it **Spacing** above and below the section, either the same on all devices or different on mobile.

## Colours and fonts

The **Colours and fonts** tab changes the colours and fonts of the whole store. For card style, hero and spacing details see [Fine-tune your store's look](/en/store-look-controls/).

## Preview before publishing

The preview in the middle updates as you edit. With **Preview size** switch between **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile**. If it looks wrong on mobile, adjust that section's spacing for the mobile device only.

## Save and publish

- The draft saves itself, and the status line says **Draft saved**, **Saving…** or **Unsaved changes**.
- **Undo** and **Redo** at the top step through your latest edits.
- Press **Publish**. You see "Published — your store shows the new page". If publishing fails the draft stays saved and you can try again. If something on the page is wrong, publishing stops and tells you what to fix.
- The **History** tab lists published versions and lets you go back to any of them. See [Page versions, publish history and restore](/en/page-versions-and-restore/).
- The **Ask AI** tab edits the page from your description. See [Ask the assistant to edit your page](/en/ask-ai-edit-page/).

## If the page was changed elsewhere

If you open the editor in two tabs, or someone else edits the same page, you see "This page was changed in another tab or by another editor". Choose **Keep mine** or **Discard my changes, load the latest**.

## Common questions

**How do I edit my home page?** Open **Store look**, leave the page on **Home**, edit the sections and press **Publish**.

**Add a section?** **Add section** in the Structure pane.

**Hide a section?** **Hide** next to the section, then **Publish**.

**Mobile looks wrong.** Switch **Preview size** to **Mobile** and adjust **Spacing** for that device.
