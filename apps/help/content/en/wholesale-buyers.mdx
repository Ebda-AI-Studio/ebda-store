Wholesale is part of the Business plan.

## Decide on an application
1. Open **Wholesale applications**. Pending ones are first.
2. **Approve** and pick the wholesale customer group the buyer joins; they get that group's prices. Or **Reject**. Either way the applicant is emailed, with your note if you wrote one.
3. A decision cannot be undone from the screen.

## Remove a wholesale buyer
Open an approved application and choose **Revoke**: the buyer goes back to retail prices at once. They can apply again after 30 days.

## Ask the assistant
The assistant can prepare this for you. It always shows a preview first, and you press the button yourself. The assistant previews each decision with the applicant's business name, the group and your note exactly as it will be emailed. Decisions cannot be undone.
