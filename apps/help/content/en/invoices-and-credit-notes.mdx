## One document per sale

Every order gets a document the moment it is placed:

- a **Tax invoice** (INV-000001, INV-000002, …) when your store is [verified for VAT](/en/vat-registration/) and the order carries VAT;
- otherwise a **Sales receipt** (RCT-000001, …) with no VAT wording.

A **cash-on-delivery** order that is not paid yet has no receipt: its order number (ORD-…) is its order confirmation. The **Sales receipt** number is given when the cash is collected (the order is marked paid or delivered), so a receipt always means money was received. A tax invoice is still issued when the order is placed.

Each type has its own series for your store, and the numbers follow each other with **no gaps**: a number is only used when the order really exists. The number is not the order number (ORD-…), which stays as the order's reference.

The document lists every line the customer pays for: the products, the **shipping**, the **cash-on-delivery fee** and any extra amount, then the discounts, the VAT and the total. The lines always add up to the total.

## Refunds and cancellations

- **Refund** (full or partial, through a payment provider, a return, or a refund you record yourself): a **Credit note** (CRN-…) for a tax invoice, or a **Refund receipt** (RFR-…) for a receipt. It has its own number and names the original document's number and date. Several partial refunds give several notes; together they never exceed the original.
- **Cancelled before any money was taken**: the document keeps its number and is marked **Cancelled**. Nothing is deleted.

## Where to find them

Open your store and go to **Invoices & credit notes** to see every document, filter by type and open or print one. Each order page also lists its own documents.

## Kept for 7 years

Issued documents can never be edited or deleted. They are kept for **7 years**, even if your store is closed or deleted, because Egyptian tax law requires invoices and records to be kept (Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020, Articles 37, 38 and 44). When a store is deleted, its orders' tax figures and Ebda Store's subscription billing records are moved to a locked tax archive without customer contact details. A store cannot be deleted while money is still in flight (paid orders not delivered yet, transfers awaiting confirmation, open returns, or cash on delivery not settled).

## Good to know

- Ebda Store does not submit e-invoices or e-receipts to the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) yet. Each document keeps a separate place for the ETA reference for when that is added; your document number stays the same.
- You stay responsible for your own tax returns.
