When you rename a product, category or page its link changes. The store keeps a redirect so the old link still lands on the new page.

## See and remove a redirect
1. Open **SEO**, then **Redirects** in your store.
2. Search by the old slug, the last part of the old link.
3. Delete the redirect you do not want. A stale redirect can stop you reusing an old slug.

Redirects are created automatically when a slug changes. You cannot add one by hand.

## Ask the assistant
Say "delete the old redirect from old-shoes". The assistant lists every redirect it would remove and asks you to confirm. Deleting a redirect cannot be undone.
