In the store editor, open the **Ask AI** tab and describe what you want: "make the banner slideshow slower", "add a newsletter section", "hide the testimonials", "show my bracelets in the best sellers", "fix the phone view". Arabic or English both work. The assistant knows your products, categories, look and the screen size you are looking at.

## What you see before anything changes

- The proposed page appears in the preview, over your own content. The preview is read-only.
- The tab lists each change with what it was and what it becomes.
- It can add, move and remove items, point a section at your products or a category, and set pictures and links from your own catalogue.
- It only fills empty places. Words, pictures and links you wrote or chose yourself are left as they are, and you are told how many.
- Anything it cannot do safely is skipped and you are told how many.

## Apply or discard

**Apply to draft** saves the change to your draft only. Nothing goes live until you press **Publish**, and **Undo** takes it back. **Discard** throws the suggestion away. If the page changed after the suggestion, ask again.

## Good to know

- The assistant only changes things the editor already lets you change by hand. It never writes code.
- It does not see your customers or orders.
- Each request counts toward your daily AI limit.
