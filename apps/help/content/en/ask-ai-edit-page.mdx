In the store editor, open the **Ask AI** tab and describe what you want: "make the banner slideshow slower", "add a newsletter section", "hide the testimonials". Arabic or English both work.

## What you see before anything changes

- The proposed page appears in the preview, over your own content. The preview is read-only.
- The tab lists each change with what it was and what it becomes.
- If part of your request cannot be done safely (for example changing a picture or a link), that part is skipped and you are told how many.

## Apply or discard

**Apply to draft** saves the change to your draft only. Nothing goes live until you press **Publish**, and **Undo** takes it back. **Discard** throws the suggestion away. If the page changed after the suggestion, ask again.

## Good to know

- The assistant only changes things the editor already lets you change by hand. It never writes code.
- It does not see your customers or orders.
- Each request counts toward your daily AI limit.
