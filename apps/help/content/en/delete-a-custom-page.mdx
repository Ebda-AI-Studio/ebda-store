You can delete the extra pages you made yourself. You do not need Ebda AI Studio staff.

1. Open **Content** and pick the page.
2. Press **Delete page** and confirm. Deleting is permanent.

The Privacy, Terms, Shipping, Returns and Refund policy pages cannot be deleted, because the law expects them on your store. Edit their text instead.
