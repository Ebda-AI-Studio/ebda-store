Notifications tell you when something happens in your store, such as a new order or a failed payment. You choose, event by event, whether it reaches you by SMS or as a phone alert from the orders app.

## Choose which events notify you
1. Open **Notifications** from your store menu.
2. In the events table, tick **SMS** and / or **Push** next to each event: new order, payment succeeded, payment failed, shipped, delivered, cancelled, refunded.
3. Press **Save**. Your choices are kept and shown again when you reopen the page.

If phone alerts are not available for your store yet you will see a short message to try again later. You never need to set up servers or keys yourself.

The **SMS** column appears only when your plan includes SMS and the SMS channel is connected. Connection details are entered once on the same page, on the secure screen, never in chat.

## Get a sound alert for new orders
1. Open the orders app (`orders.ebda.app`) on your phone and install it to your home screen. On iPhone this step is required for alerts.
2. Press **Enable order alerts** and allow notifications in the browser.
3. Make sure **Push** is ticked for **New order** in the events table.

If you denied the browser permission, turn it back on from your phone's site settings, then press the button again.

## Ask the assistant
Ask in your own words, for example "stop SMS for shipped orders" or "alert my phone on every new order". The assistant prepares a preview with the old and the new setting side by side. After you apply it you can press **Undo** for 24 hours. It never asks for SMS or WhatsApp keys, and it cannot register your phone: you press **Enable order alerts** on the device yourself.
