Your home page is built from sections: banner, categories, products, customer reviews, a trust strip and more. They are all edited in the **visual editor** (**Store look** in the side menu), and only the store owner can open it.

## Add a section

1. In the **Sections** tab press **Add section**.
2. Pick the section from the list. Sections that are not part of your store's look are under **More sections**.
3. If a section reached its maximum, its card is disabled and says **Limit reached**.
4. From the **Sections** tab you can also hide a section, show it, duplicate it or remove it with the buttons next to it (removing asks you to confirm, and **Undo** brings it back). **Show on** chooses whether it appears on desktop, tablet or mobile.
5. Press **Publish** so visitors see it.

## Customer reviews

This section shows real reviews from your customers, not text you type in. It takes the **published** reviews on your products (the ones you approved in Product reviews), sorts them by highest rating and then newest, and shows up to 6. The name appears as the first name plus the first letter of the last name.

From the section settings:

- **Heading**.
- **Layout**: **Row of reviews** or **One quote**.
- **Minimum rating** from 1 to 5 (default 4).
- **Verified purchases only**.

If no published review matches these rules nothing is shown. Approve reviews first or lower **Minimum rating**.

## Trust strip

A small strip with points such as delivery, payment and returns. From the section settings:

- Add up to **6 items**. Each item has an **Icon** (Cash on delivery, InstaPay, Delivery, Certified, Returns, Gift wrap, Engraving, Support), a **Title**, and an optional **Subtitle**, in Arabic and English.
- **Layout**: **Row** or **Stacked**. **Size**: small, medium or large. Plus icon, text and background colours.
- A strip with no items is hidden on your store.

## Trust badges

A second section called **Trust badges**: an optional heading, a **Layout** (**Row** or **Grid**) and a background colour, with up to 6 **badges** (icon, title and text). You can add at most two of these sections. The ready-made **Delivery and payment** option gives you three badges: Cash on delivery, InstaPay and Delivery across Egypt.

## Common questions

**I added customer reviews and nothing shows.** Check you have published reviews, and that **Minimum rating** and **Verified purchases only** do not exclude all of them.

**I cannot find a section.** The list shows the sections allowed on the page you are editing. Switch page at the top of the editor.

**The change did not reach visitors.** You must **Publish**. To go back to an older version see [Page versions and restore](/en/page-versions-and-restore/).
