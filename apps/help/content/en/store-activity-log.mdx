Open **Settings → Activity log** to see what happened in your store: who did it, what they did and when. The newest entries come first, and **Load more** shows older ones.

- Only the store owner can open it. Staff members cannot.
- It is read only. You cannot edit or delete entries.
- Customer emails and phone numbers are hidden, and so are keys, tokens and passwords. Only the first part of an IP address shows.
- Each store sees only its own entries.
