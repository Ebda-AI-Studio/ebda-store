Smart hints learn from how your own orders were delivered and returned, and tell you what they found. They only advise. A hint never holds, delays or cancels an order, and the final choice is always yours.

## Switch them on

1. In your store, open Sales, then Smart hints.
2. Turn on **Show smart hints**. They are off until you do, and you can turn them off again at any time.

## What you will see

- **COD risk note on an order.** For a cash-on-delivery order, a note says whether returns to that area are typical or higher than usual, and whether this customer has returned several orders with your store before. When the risk is higher it suggests confirming by phone or asking for an InstaPay deposit. The order is not held.
- **Courier suggestion when you ship.** Under the carrier picker, one line names the carrier with the best delivery record to that area. Your selection does not change.
- **COD amount suggestion.** On the Smart hints page, a card shows when cash-on-delivery orders above a certain amount come back clearly more often, so you can think about asking for a deposit above it.

## How many orders is it based on?

Every hint shows how many orders it rests on. Nothing is shown until there are enough: at least 30 of your own orders spread over two weeks, or, from other stores, at least 100 orders from at least 5 stores. Before that you will see **Not enough data yet**, never a guess. A hint is also only used while the numbers behind it are less than a week old.

## Learn from other stores too

If you switch on **Learn from other stores too**, your store's numbers are counted together with other stores so the hints are more accurate for everyone. Only counts are shared: never a name, phone number, email or address, and no store can see another store or any of its customers. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off whenever you like. A customer's history is only ever shown inside the store that recorded it.

## Privacy and deleting data

Hints use a scrambled customer key, not the customer's phone number. If a customer asks for their data to be erased, their records are removed from the outcome history as described in your privacy policy. When you close your store, its numbers are deleted.
