Customer groups let you split your customers by hand and use the split for group pricing and promotion targeting. For example, you create a VIP group and give it a percentage discount.

## Create a group

1. From your store, open **Customer groups**.
2. Press **New group**.
3. Enter the **Name**, and the **Name (Arabic)** if you want.
4. Keep **Active** ticked if the group should be live.
5. Tick **Default group for new customers** if every new customer should join this group. The first group you create is marked default automatically.
6. Press **Save**.

The list shows each group's name, number of customers and status (**Active** or **Inactive**), with a **Default** badge on the default one. Use **Edit** to change anything.

## Give the group its own price

In the **Pricing** section choose **Group price**:

- **No group discount**: the group is only for segmenting and promotions.
- **Percent off the price**: enter **Discount (%)** from 0 to 90, with at most two decimals. The discount applies when it beats any quantity price break. Coupons do not apply to these lines.

Also tick **Wholesale group (members must be approved)** if the group is for wholesale accounts. Group prices apply only to approved, signed-in accounts. A guest, or someone who typed a phone number or email at checkout, never gets the group price.

Group and wholesale pricing needs the **Business** plan (wholesale pricing). If your plan does not include it you see "Wholesale pricing needs the … plan" and the system answers `FEATURE_DISABLED`. A group without pricing needs no particular plan. See [Plans and billing](/en/plans-and-billing/).

## Put a customer in a group

1. Open **Customers** and press **View** on the customer.
2. Under **Customer group** pick the group. Pick **No group** to remove them.

The choice is saved right away. An inactive group shows with an **inactive** tag in the list. The profile also tells you whether the customer will pay a group's prices once signed in.

## Delete a group

Press **Delete** on the group and confirm. The customers in it become ungrouped; they are not deleted.

## Who can do this

The store owner only. Team members do not see customer groups.

## Common questions

**Create a VIP group.**
Create a new group named VIP, then put customers in it from each customer's profile. For a discount, turn on **Percent off the price** (needs the Business plan).

**A discount for one group.**
Under **Pricing** choose **Percent off the price** and enter the discount.
