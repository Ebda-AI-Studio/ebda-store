A customer group is a segment you define by hand, for example "VIP" or "Wholesale". Groups are used for group prices and for targeting offers.

## Do it yourself
1. Open **Customer groups** in your store admin.
2. Press **New group**, fill in **Name** (and the Arabic name if you want), then **Save**.
3. To give the group a discount, open it with **Edit**, set **Group price** to a percentage and fill in **Discount (%)**, then **Save**. Wholesale prices need a plan that includes wholesale.
4. To put a customer in a group, open the customer and choose the group. To take them out, clear the group.

## Ask the assistant
Say, for example, "create a VIP group with 10% off and add these three customers". The assistant prepares a preview with the old and new values and the number of customers it moves. Nothing changes until you apply it, and you can press **Undo** for 24 hours: the group goes back to its old values and every customer returns to their old group. A group the assistant created is switched off by Undo, not deleted.

Moving more than 20 customers at once always waits for your explicit confirmation. The assistant never deletes a group and never changes which group is the default for new customers; do those on the Customer groups page.
