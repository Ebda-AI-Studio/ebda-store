Every time you publish a page, the editor keeps that version. Open the **History** tab to see them, newest first, each with the date, time and **who** published it.

## Draft and live

Your edits are saved as a **draft** first. Visitors only see the page after you press **Publish**. Publishing replaces the live version at once and the earlier one moves to history.

## Restore an earlier version

Press **Restore** next to a version and confirm. The editor publishes it again as a **new** version (the history is never rewritten) and your current draft is replaced. The new entry says "Restored from version N".

## Good to know

- Colours, fonts, header and footer changes are part of the version, so restoring brings them back too.
- A visitor sees the new page within seconds of publishing; there is nothing to clear on your side.
