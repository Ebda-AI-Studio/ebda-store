A size chart is a table that shoppers open from the product page while they pick a size. You can make it yourself and link it to a product or a whole category. The store owner and team members can make them.

## Make a chart

1. From **Catalog** open **Size charts**.
2. Press **New chart**.
3. Start from a template if you like: **Abaya (size × length)**, **Clothing (S–XXL)** or **Ring size**. A template replaces what you have written (it asks you to confirm with **Replace with the template**).
4. Write the **Name (Arabic)** and **Name (English)**. At least one name is required.
5. Choose the **Unit**: **cm** or **in**.
6. In **Table** write the headers in Arabic and English and the table cells. Use **Add column** and **Add row**. The limits are 2 to 10 columns, 1 to 30 rows, and each cell up to 24 characters.
7. You can write a **Note** in both languages (for example "Body measurements, not the garment"). There is a **Preview** in Arabic or English.
8. Press **Save**.

If the table breaks the limits you see "Check the table: 2–10 columns, 1–30 rows, cells up to 24 characters."

## Link it to a product or a category

- **Product**: open the product, and in the **Fulfilment** card choose from **Size guide**. Shoppers get a "Size guide" button next to the size picker.
- **Category**: in the category details choose from the same list. Every product in the category uses this chart unless the product has its own (**From category**, **None** or a specific chart).

## Edit or delete

The list shows each chart's shape (**columns × rows**) and how many products and categories use it. **Edit** opens the chart, and **Delete** asks you to confirm (**Delete the chart**). If you delete a chart linked to products or categories they show without a size guide.

## Common questions

**How do I make a size chart?** Start from a template and edit it.

**The button does not show for a customer.** Make sure the product or its category is linked to a chart, and that you did not choose **None**.
