Variants let you sell the same product in more than one option: Small, Medium and Large, or a 4 liter and a 6 liter pack. Each variant has its own price, stock and photo, and your customer picks one on the product page.

## Who can do this

The store owner and staff members who can manage products.

## Add variants

1. Open the product from **Products**. On a brand-new product you get an **Add variants** button in the banner on top. On an older product open **More product options** and choose **Manage Variants**.
2. The **Variants** page opens with your product name. At the top you see the product's base price and the **Total Variant Stock**.
3. In the **Variants** card write one row per variant. Fill in the **Name** (for example Medium) and the **Name (Arabic)**.
4. Fill in the **Price (EGP)**. You can also set a **Compare-at (EGP)** price, a **Cost (EGP)** and the **Stock**, and pick an **Image** if the variant has its own photo.
5. To add another variant press **Add another variant**. The page starts you off with two empty rows.
6. Press **Save variants**. You see "Variants saved" with the number saved.

The page creates one option axis (like Size or Volume) and builds the variants from your rows. SKUs are generated automatically as "product SKU-variant name".

## Swatches

When the product has a single option axis, each row also has a **Swatch colour** and a **Swatch photo**. Your customer sees them as a colour dot or a small picture next to the choice, which is useful when the options are colours.

## Products with two axes (Size by Colour)

If a product was set up with two axes, such as Size and Colour, the page shows every combination as a row. You can edit the **Price**, **Stock** and **Image** of each combination, but the names are locked and you cannot create new combinations from this page. An **Option swatches** card also appears so you can save a colour or photo for each value.

## Delete a variant

Press **Remove** on the row. A confirmation asks you to delete the variant. If you confirm, it is removed from the product and cannot be restored.

## If saving is blocked

- **Add at least one variant with a name:** at least one row must have a name.
- **"Name" appears twice:** variant names must be unique.
- **Set a price:** every variant needs a price, and it must be zero or more.
- **Some variants failed to save:** refresh to see the current state, then save again.

<Callout title="Stock">
  The total variant stock counts active variants only. A disabled variant is not counted as stock.
</Callout>
