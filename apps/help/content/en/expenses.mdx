An expense records money your business spent, against your chart of accounts. It starts as a draft; an approved and posted expense creates a balanced ledger entry.

## Do it yourself
1. Open **Expenses** in your store admin and press **Add expense**.
2. Fill in **Expense date**, **Expense Category**, **Paid From Account** (a cash or bank account), the **Amount (EGP)** and the tax if any.
3. Press **Save expense**. It is saved as a draft.
4. From the expense's menu choose **Approve**, then **Post**, when it is correct. **Reject expense** takes a draft out of the accounts.

## Ask the assistant
Say, for example, "record 250 EGP for the internet bill, paid from the bank, today". The assistant shows a confirmation card with the exact amount in EGP, the tax, the date and both accounts. It only saves after you confirm that exact card; changing anything makes you confirm again. It saves a draft only: approving, posting and rejecting stay on the Expenses page, so the assistant cannot undo it. The expenses feature must be included in your plan, and only the store owner can do this.
