Your store details are what shoppers see in the footer and on the Contact page: the store name, description, phone, WhatsApp number, address, opening hours, a Google Maps link and your social links.

## Change them yourself
1. Open **Settings** and choose **Logo and colors** (it holds the contact details too).
2. Edit the fields you need. The WhatsApp number must be an Egyptian mobile (010, 011, 012 or 015); it is saved in the international form.
3. Social links must start with `https://`. Leave a link empty to remove it.
4. Press **Save**.

## Ask the assistant
Say what you want, for example "change my phone number to 011..." or "add my Instagram link". The assistant shows a card with the old and new value of each field. Nothing changes until you confirm, and you get an **Undo** button for 24 hours.

The assistant never changes your currency, plan, domain, payment settings or any password or key.
